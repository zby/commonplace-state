## Connections Found

*Candidate outbound edges for a future edit of the source note. Labels are drawn only from the `kb/notes/COLLECTION.md` table for the stated source→destination pair.*

- [An omitted improvement-loop function and a frozen one need different repairs](../../../../notes/an-omitted-loop-function-and-a-frozen-one-need-different-repairs.md) — **contrasts**: the same under-determination result reached by a different route. That note concludes "a reported improvement cannot separate them" and resolves it by reading the update space; this note concludes the acceptance record cannot separate leverage from maintenance and resolves it by displacing the measurement. A reader who has one should be told the other exists, because the two remedies are not interchangeable — reading the update space is free, replaying a frozen-artifact episode is not.
- [An accepted edit verifies the change, not the rule](../../../../notes/an-accepted-edit-verifies-the-change-not-the-rule.md) — **contrasts**: the neighbouring shape one level down. The distinction worth surfacing is what each silence requires: re-verification at the rule level in one case, a displaced measure plus uptake trace in the other.
- [Retrieval failure is reflection failure](../../../notes/retrieval-failure-is-reflection-failure.md) — **grounds**: supplies the premise the uptake requirement rests on — that delivery through the retention wire is best-effort and separately measurable, and explicitly states "retrieval tests the first connection; uptake still needs a separate perturbation or with/without test." - [Oracle accumulation improves selection for later candidates in its maintained domain](../../../../notes/oracle-accumulation-improves-the-selection-environment.md) — **mechanism**: names one concrete way leverage is produced — a failure retained as a maintained check improves evaluation of every later candidate in its domain and amortizes validation — which is exactly what the "cheaper" and "more reliable" rows would be picking up. The source claims maintenance cost is the entry most easily lost because it is charged to no episode; this note is where that accounting already has a home, so a reader can see the debit is a pre-existing obligation rather than a new one invented for the protocol. *Weaker than the five above — drop it if the footer is getting long.*
