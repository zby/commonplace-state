## Connections Found

Candidate outbound edges for a future edit of the source. Labels are drawn only from the `kb/notes/` authorised table.

### The free band and where it ends — the source's weakest-cited section

- [Weight-resident methodologies provide context-efficient behavioral compression](../../../../notes/weight-resident-methodologies-compress-behavior-in-context.md) — **mechanism**: names how the pinned model supplies its free band — a compact cue activating structure already in weights — and states the limit the source relies on, that the activation is a model-dependent reconstruction rather than a retained, editable specification. The source's "the pinned model supplies whatever it already does with text on the spot" is exactly this mechanism, currently asserted with nothing behind it.
- [Only explicit retention is currently durable, writable, and addressable at once](../../../../notes/only-explicit-retention-is-durable-writable-and-addressable.md) — **grounds**: the source's central move is that a procedure "has to be written into one of them and retained." This note supplies the premise — every tacit retention form fails addressability — that makes explicit writing the only governed option once the weights are pinned. Without it the source's step from "the parametric route is closed" to "so write it down" is a leap.
- [Scaling absorbs scaffolding at fixed task difficulty, not at the deployment frontier](../../../../notes/scaling-absorbs-scaffolding-at-fixed-difficulty-not-at-the-frontier.md) — **extends**: the source's first Open Question asks where the procedure demand starts and concedes it "moves with model capability." This note develops precisely that: it separates the fixed-task question (scaling absorbs the scaffolding) from the frontier question (deployment reopens the gap), which is the distinction the source's Open Question needs to be answerable rather than merely open.
- [Knowledge storage does not imply contextual activation](../../../../notes/knowledge-storage-does-not-imply-contextual-activation.md) — **grounds**: separates knowledge that exists, knowledge read back into context, and knowledge that changes behaviour. The source's opening claim — retained theory is inert without operations — is the theory-shaped case of that activation gap, and citing it converts the opening from assertion to instance.

### Why the crossing into code is not just a cost argument

- [Semantic work can be relocated but not eliminated](../../../../notes/semantic-work-can-be-relocated-but-not-eliminated.md) — **mechanism**: explains what codifying a theory-manipulation step actually does to the re-interpretation cost — it is paid once at authoring rather than at each use, never removed. This is the mechanism under the source's contrast between a procedure "re-interpreted at each use" and one "executed by a defined consumer," and it also bounds the claim: the crossing buys placement, not free execution.
- [System-definition artifacts are crystallized reasoning under context scarcity](../../../../notes/system-definition-artifacts-are-crystallized-reasoning-under-context.md) — **grounds**: distinguishes heuristic rules that merely substitute for read-time reasoning the model could do anyway from authority-bearing constraints and symbolic codification that stay useful even with abundant context. The source's strongest claim — that past the free band the procedure is what lets the theory reach the decision *at all*, not merely more cheaply — needs exactly this distinction to avoid collapsing into the cost argument it explicitly disowns.
- [LLM↔code boundaries are natural checkpoints](../../../../notes/llm-code-boundaries-are-natural-checkpoints.md) — **grounds**: the source claims a codified procedure "can be tested against cases" and "runs the same way twice." This note supplies why: at the transition both semantic underspecification and execution indeterminism collapse at once, which is what makes the boundary testable rather than merely repeatable.
- [Skills derive from methodology](../../../../notes/skills-derive-from-methodology.md) — **contrasts**: describes the *same-medium* path — methodology to skill, with the methodology retained as live fallback — which the note explicitly separates from codification and constraining. The source treats written instructions and code as two carriers on one axis; this note shows a third move that stays in natural language while still fixing a procedure, sharpening what the source's "crossing" actually costs and buys.

### What remains writable under the pin

- [Readable artifact loop is the tractable unit for continual learning](../../../../notes/readable-artifact-loop-is-the-tractable-unit-for-continual-learning.md) — **extends**: the source concludes that what remains under the pin is the natural-language plus symbolic pair. This note argues that same pair is the tractable first loop for representational-form coevolution, because it shares context, runs at current tempos, and already has a codification boundary — turning the source's residual into a positive design target.
- [Reflective coverage is graded across representational forms](../../../../notes/reflective-coverage-is-graded-across-representational-forms.md) — **grounds**: states coverage per represented form and operation profile, and separates controlling an external dependency from having it inside reflective coverage. This licenses the source's move of excluding the pinned parametric form from what the house can revise, rather than treating the pin as a stipulation.
- [Unformalized improvements need a pre-formal stage in the loop](../../../../notes/unformalized-improvements-need-a-pre-formal-stage-in-the-loop.md) — **extends**: the source says a needed procedure "has to be written," but not what happens when the operation has no expression in the language the loop admits. This note supplies the pre-formal stage and its two placements — inside the loop, or fixed at design time in the choice of language — which is the missing step between recognising the demand and writing the code.
