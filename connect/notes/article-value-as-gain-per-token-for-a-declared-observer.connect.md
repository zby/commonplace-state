## Connections Found

- [An experiment identifies only the contrast it actually runs](../../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) — **grounds**: the matched run varies the whole article against nothing, so its treatment grain is the article as one bundle; that note's bound says the result cannot identify a component-level cause, which means a calibration of the five-slot reading against whole-article gain asks a finer question than the experiment runs. The source's Scope section admits the pairing may fail but does not name this as the reason a null result would be uninterpretable.
- [Exact implementation does not validate a requirement against its objective](../../../../notes/exact-implementation-does-not-validate-a-requirement.md) — **grounds**: supplies the general structure the proposal's proxy/instrument pairing instantiates — an artifact can exactly satisfy its requirement while the requirement→objective link stays conjectural — and names ablations and composition as the evidence classes that test the upper link. It is the premise behind the source's own admission that the reading "is checkable in a weak but useful sense" yet "settles nothing" about gain.
- [Known-target discovery benchmarks show reachability, not discovery closure](../../../../notes/known-target-discovery-benchmarks-show-reachability-not-discovery.md) — **exemplifies**: the "Declaring the observer for a literature" section builds a backcast — the prior is the literature up to a date, historical ordering does the leakage control, later outcomes supply the target — and the note states the general claim such constructions license (a target insight is reachable from supplied ingredients, not that prospective value selection works). Reading it would tell the proposal's calibration arm what its labelled cases can and cannot establish.
- [Oracle strength spectrum](../../../../notes/oracle-strength-spectrum.md) — **grounds**: names the **delayed oracle** ("you only know later — did the decision pay off?"), which is exactly the oracle class the literature section adopts when it grades held-out answers against later empirical outcomes, and the soft-oracle class that gain-on-a-question-set otherwise sits in. It is the premise behind the source's claim that grading against later outcomes relaxes the correctness limit for the outcomes sampled.
- [Decorrelated reviewers still share the field's prior, so read their findings by the claim's stance](../../../../notes/reviewers-share-the-fields-prior-so-interpret-findings-by-stance.md) — **grounds**: the proposal answers plural readers with a panel of declared observers (specialist, adjacent-field reader, practitioner). - [Evaluation automation is phase-gated by comprehension](../../../../notes/evaluation-automation-is-phase-gated-by-comprehension.md) — **enables**: the source's first open question asks how the grader's discrimination is established before the instrument is trusted; that note supplies the operational prerequisite as a sequence — comprehension, then specification, then evaluators calibrated against manually labelled examples, then automated optimization — and reports a field case where auto-generated judges produced score gains with no quality gain. ## Synthesis Opportunities

[An experiment identifies only the contrast it actually runs](../../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) and [Exact implementation does not validate a requirement against its objective](../../../../notes/exact-implementation-does-not-validate-a-requirement.md) together imply a claim neither states and this proposal needs: **a fine-grained proxy cannot be calibrated by a coarse-grained instrument.** The second note says the requirement→objective link needs ablation or composition evidence; the first says an ablation identifies only its own contrast. Put together, calibrating a structured, multi-slot proxy against a whole-artifact intervention can establish that the proxy tracks the aggregate and can never license slot-level attribution — so a proxy whose slots are supposed to be separately diagnostic needs a contrast per slot, or must give up the per-slot reading. The proposal's Scope section reaches the edge of this ("the reading may be satisfiable by articles that produce no gain") without the general statement. Do not write this during connect; it is a candidate note, and if written it would sit in `kb/notes/` as ordinary theory, not as a proposal.
