## Synthesis Opportunities

Three independent cases now sit in the KB where a borrowed ontology was bought and later had to be unbundled: the register taxonomy borrowing linguistic-register prestige for a closed set linguistics does not support (ADR 042), Tulving's semantic/episodic/procedural split possibly reducing to "different retention policies" (`three-space-agent-memory-...`), and four of five borrowed psychology principles reducing to standard IR concepts (`psychology-to-agent-transfer-...`). Together they suggest a sharper claim than the source states: that the recurring failure of a borrowed ontology is not that it fails to fit, but that it fits well enough to be *unfalsifiable in the borrowing domain* — the vocabulary keeps working while contributing no mechanism. The source's "'tested' is the weak joint" caveat is the general form; these three are a possible worked case series for it. Do not author during connect.
