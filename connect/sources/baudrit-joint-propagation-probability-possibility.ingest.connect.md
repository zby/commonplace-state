## Connections Found

- [On-balance judgments](../../../../theory/on-balance-judgments-can-guide-improvement-without-a-global-measure.md) — **compares-with**: A judgment may require the probability of a damaging outcome when both frequency and effect are incompletely known. The paper gives an interval for event probabilities under alternative assumptions about dependence.
- [RELAX](../../../../sources/relax-uncertainty-self-adaptive-systems.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: RELAX uses fuzzy language to express the degree to which a requirement is met. Baudrit and colleagues use possibility distributions for partial information about input values. Interchanging these meanings would confuse whether a requirement is satisfied with what is known about the inputs.
