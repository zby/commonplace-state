## Synthesis Opportunities

Three snapshots now sit in the KB as a matched set on one question — `causal-inference-using-invariant-prediction` (invariance identifies causal predictors, with confidence bounds), `towards-causal-representation-learning` (reusable mechanisms explain shift transfer), and this one plus `in-search-of-lost-domain-generalization` (the formal and empirical bounds on what invariance objectives deliver). Together they imply a claim not yet in `kb/notes/`: **the causal route to reach-assessment degrades along a known axis — it is strongest where multiple genuine environments are available and the model class is restricted, and gives no advantage over undisciplined fitting where it is not.** That would sharpen `formal-systems-assess-explanatory-reach-through-causal-and-proof.md`'s "formalization boundary" section from a general warning about translation into a stated condition on when the causal route is worth taking. Do not create it during connect; it is likely better as a revision to the existing note than as a new one.
