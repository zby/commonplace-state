## Flags

The 107 tasks are seeded from accepted-paper problems with reproducible baseline code and benchmarks; 86 successes and average 25.2% relative gain describe selected successful outcomes across heterogeneous metrics. The gain calculation parses reported tables with model assistance. The pipeline revises ideas, experiments and papers; it does not establish self-modification of the harness. Three successive research cycles are one narrow reuse example, not general continual learning.

ScholarPeer review and two review/rebuttal rounds are inside the loop; the Stanford evaluator is held out from that loop but still automated. Human assessment covers 33 successful papers with nine reviewers and lacks a detailed blinding/reliability protocol here. Comparisons with other research systems change tasks, models and resources. Reported integrity checks cover 49 successful ICML cases after filtering: evaluation re-execution, specification adherence, references and method-code agreement. The no-auditor comparison begins with 50, then one specification failure is removed; do not compare denominators as an isolated matched ablation.

Procrustes DS criticism found near-uniform weights, weak alignment gains, harmful feature gating and poorly scaled shrinkage. The final method replaces or removes those mechanisms; its audit explicitly checks Ours rather than reproducing the baseline. Its favorable final comparison is not validation of the initial explanation. TABHARMONY's transfer uses bounded datasets, support/test caps, inner validation and frequent fallback; mean gains do not mean uniform wins.

The DynaSpecRAG appendix trains a small residual module for 40 epochs on legacy training sets and calibrates a blend per domain. Its dual-metric guard constrains a normalized validation sum, not each metric or unseen-test outcomes. Table 7 reports two of seven test datasets regressing despite stronger abstract/conclusion wording. Five new domains avoid retraining weights but still use domain calibration and retrieval; one MAE rises. Preserve these internal qualifications rather than repeating the no-regression or blanket zero-shot claim. No implementation was inspected or executed for this ingest.
