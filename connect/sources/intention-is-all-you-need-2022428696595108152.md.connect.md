## Flags

- **Split candidate:** the source connects to more than five notes across at least three topic areas and makes independent claims about enterprise ontology, intent specification, orchestration, labor allocation, verification, and system learning. A future ingest should disposition those claims separately rather than promote “intention-centric architecture” as one undifferentiated conclusion.
- **Internal tension to preserve:** the source calls object records lossy maps that agents can bypass by reading the “territory,” yet also calls canonical objects a prerequisite. The PostHog and Palantir comparisons suggest that agent legibility does not remove the need for governed definitions and state authority; the future ingest should keep this as a bounded tension rather than silently choosing one side.
- **Evidence boundary:** the source is a forward-looking practitioner essay. Its architectural decomposition and examples can be analyzed, but claims about infinitely scalable execution, dominant future platforms, and teams shipping whole products without manually written code should not be promoted as established empirical results without separate evidence.
