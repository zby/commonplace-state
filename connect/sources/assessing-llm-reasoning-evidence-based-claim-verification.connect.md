## Connections Found

- [Claim modality is the inference form of the refuter](../../../../notes/claim-modality-is-the-inference-form-of-the-refuter.md) -- **compares-with**: both artifacts use deductive, inductive, and abductive vocabulary, but RECV labels the operation annotators believe is needed to reach a supplied veracity from a claim-evidence pair, while local claim modality labels the inference form available to a refuter. The comparison prevents a shared name from collapsing two different classification axes.
- [Reasoning production is not reasoning evaluation](../../../../notes/reasoning-production-is-not-reasoning-evaluation.md) -- **compares-with**: RECV measures production of a SUPPORTS/REFUTES verdict and optional rationale; it does not ask a model to assess the validity of a submitted reasoning path. This positions its deductive/abductive error gap on an axis orthogonal to production-versus-evaluation capability.
- [Systematic prompt variation serves verification and diagnosis, not explanatory-reach testing](../../../../notes/systematic-prompt-variation-serves-verification-and-diagnosis-not.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: RECV's prompt effects vary by dataset and model and sometimes reverse sign, materially supporting a diagnostic or local-optimization reading of prompt variation. Its bundled prompts and single reported outcomes also bound any mechanism claim.
- [Structured-prompt gains do not establish training-distribution selection](../../../../notes/structured-prompt-gains-do-not-establish-distribution-selection.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: RECV bundles persona, examples, step-by-step wording, explanation requirements, and output shape, while effects vary by task and model. The result supports the note's claim that performance changes from such prompts do not identify training-distribution selection as their cause.
- [Selective revision needs a faithful rationale, not just a legible one](../../../../notes/selective-revision-needs-a-faithful-rationale-not-just-a-legible-one.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: RECV scores semantic consistency, evidence appropriateness, coherence, and fluency, calls the best-scoring rationales “faithful,” yet explicitly concedes they may be plausible post-hoc explanations rather than the reason for a decision. This is a worked negative case for equating legibility or reference resemblance with causal faithfulness.
- [An experiment identifies only the contrast it actually runs](../../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) -- **rests-on**: interpreting RECV requires preserving the actual bundles: M-CoT adds demonstrations and wording, Exp changes the required response, and dataset personas/content differ. Performance deltas cannot be assigned uniquely to “rationale generation,” “CoT,” or a reasoning mechanism that was not independently varied.
- [Learning inside a fixed decomposition inherits its mistakes](../../../../notes/learning-inside-a-fixed-decomposition-inherits-its-mistakes.md) -- **rests-on**: RECV fixes a binary D/A taxonomy, support/refute response space, heuristic sampling thresholds, supplied evidence, dataset partitions, prompts, models, metrics, and judges. The observed results search only mappings inside that effective update space and cannot validate the decomposition or reveal excluded alternatives such as abstention, retrieval, hypothesis-set generation, or explicit test selection.
- [Theory warrant should be tracked at the finest granularity evidence licenses](../../../../notes/theory-warrant-tracked-at-the-finest-granularity-evidence-licenses.md) -- **rests-on**: RECV's Mann-Whitney tests support distributional D/A accuracy differences within each benchmark/model/prompt cell; they do not attribute the gap to inference type independently of domain, content, evidence length/sufficiency, annotation, or label policy. The paper's causal explanations therefore require narrower status than its measured contrasts.
- [Ingest: An Enigma of Artificial Reason](../../../../sources/an-enigma-of-artificial-reason-production-evaluation-gap-lrms.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: VAIR separates producing a valid answer from recognizing defects in a supplied derivation, while RECV stratifies verdict production by an annotated inference type. Together they make two independent dimensions hidden by the phrase “reasoning capability.”
- [Ingest: Language Models, Like Humans, Show Content Effects on Reasoning Tasks](../../../../sources/language-models-like-humans-show-content-effects-on-reasoning.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: Lampinen et al. vary semantic content within controlled deductive structures; RECV's D/A strata also vary dataset, domain, evidence length, evidential completeness, and claim content. The former shows the kind of control needed before reading RECV's aggregate D/A gap as an inference-type effect.
- [Ingest: Language Models Don't Always Say What They Think](../../../../sources/language-models-dont-always-say-what-they-think.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: Turpin et al. test rationale faithfulness through causal perturbations of decision-relevant inputs, whereas RECV uses NLI similarity, BARTScore, and perplexity against selected human rationales. The comparison distinguishes causal correspondence from surface explanation quality.
- [Ingest: CEDAR-GRPO: Process-Aware Reinforcement Learning for General Abductive Reasoning in LLMs](../../../../sources/cedar-grpo-process-aware-rl-abductive-reasoning.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: both use “abductive reasoning,” but CEDAR frames it as generating and selecting hypotheses with process-aware rewards, while RECV forces a support/refute verdict from supplied partial evidence. The contrast exposes construct variation before results are combined under one abduction label.

## Synthesis Opportunities

- **“Reasoning capability” benchmarks need an orthogonal factorization.** RECV, [reasoning production is not reasoning evaluation](../../../../notes/reasoning-production-is-not-reasoning-evaluation.md), the [content-effects ingest](../../../../sources/language-models-like-humans-show-content-effects-on-reasoning.ingest.md), [systematic prompt variation](../../../../notes/systematic-prompt-variation-serves-verification-and-diagnosis-not.md), and the [causal rationale-faithfulness ingest](../../../../sources/language-models-dont-always-say-what-they-think.ingest.md) jointly imply that inference construct, production/evaluation role, semantic content and evidence sufficiency, prompt/output structure, and rationale faithfulness should be varied or reported separately. An aggregate “reasoning” result is otherwise a property of a bundle whose axes cannot be recovered after the fact.
- **A forced binary verifier can score calibrated uncertainty as reasoning failure.** RECV removes the not-enough-information class, treats incomplete-evidence cases as abductive, and requires SUPPORTS/REFUTES, then interprets model reluctance to predict as alignment hindering abduction. Combined with [claim modality](../../../../notes/claim-modality-is-the-inference-form-of-the-refuter.md) and the [fixed-decomposition lens](../../../../notes/learning-inside-a-fixed-decomposition-inherits-its-mistakes.md), this suggests a broader claim: benchmark response policy can turn an epistemically appropriate abstention boundary into an apparent capability deficit.
- **Taxonomy validation and within-taxonomy performance are separate experiments.** RECV's annotation agreement, model error rates, and Mann-Whitney tests can characterize behavior after examples are assigned to D/A strata. Together with [the observed-contrast rule](../../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) and [warrant granularity](../../../../notes/theory-warrant-tracked-at-the-finest-granularity-evidence-licenses.md), they imply that construct completeness, label validity, and causal attribution need separate discriminating contrasts rather than being inferred from score gaps inside the fixed taxonomy.

## Maintenance Observations

- The full-PDF snapshot now satisfies the earlier abstract ingest's recommended next action. The abstract snapshot and ingest remain valid historical captures, but their “method unavailable” limitations are superseded for current analysis; a future maintenance pass should make the full ingest the primary traversal surface without treating the two captures as independent evidence.
- Appendix F shows ZS, CoT, and M-CoT prompts that all require a one-sentence explanation, while §6 defines No-Exp as predicting without rationale generation. The snapshot does not show separate No-Exp prompt text, so the decisive No-Exp/Exp contrast is not reproducible from the appendix as captured.
- §7 says the evaluation avoids mentioning reasoning modes and then says this matches prompts “where we do mention reasoning types”; the displayed prompts do not mention deductive or abductive labels. This internal wording contradiction should be preserved as uncertainty rather than silently repaired in an ingest.
- The rationale metric definitions appear directionally inconsistent. `C` is defined as consistency (`1 - contradiction`), but FC and EA subtract `C` while tables mark higher as better and the prose treats higher values as greater consistency. A downstream analysis should not interpret the reported values until the intended formula is resolved.
- The reasoning framework itself has unresolved definition drift: §3 describes deduction with claim-to-evidence implication and calls induction dependent on complete evidence, while Appendix A defines deduction premise-to-conclusion and induction as partial support. The Pluto “abduction” example also imports background assumptions and analogy without enumerating candidate hypotheses. These are construct-validity issues, not copyediting details.
- The paper names broad model families but the snapshot reports no temperature, sampling configuration, run repetition, seeds, API dates, or dated model identifiers. The many cell-level point estimates and significance tests therefore cannot be tied to a fully specified judging configuration.
- The rationale analysis samples 100 items per dataset only from cases where at least three models were wrong. This error-enriched cohort is useful for failure analysis but does not estimate rationale quality over the full RECV population. Pairwise permutation tables and D/A Mann-Whitney tests also report many p-values without an indicated multiple-comparison correction.

## Flags

- **Split candidate:** the future authored analysis connects to at least eight notes across taxonomy, evaluation design, prompting, faithfulness, and evidence-warrant topics, and the paper makes independent framework, benchmark, model-performance, and rationale-quality claims. Keep the immutable snapshot whole, but consider separate extractable claims or clearly separated ingest sections rather than one undifferentiated conclusion.
- **Taxonomy tension:** annotators see the known veracity label before choosing D/A and writing a rationale. The resulting label can describe a post-hoc route to the fixed answer rather than independently identify the inference a verifier would select under uncertainty; the modest full-study IAA (0.75/0.56/0.67) and tiny abductive cells (29/102/36) keep this unresolved.
- **Attribution tension:** every model/prompt cell has a higher reported abductive error distribution, but reasoning type is entangled with domain, content, evidence length/sufficiency, support/refute balance, heuristic sample selection, and annotator judgment. The Mann-Whitney tests reject equal within-benchmark distributions; they do not isolate “abductive capability” as the cause.
- **Faithfulness tension:** RECV's prose calls GPT-4o ZS-CoT rationales most faithful using surface metrics, while its Limitations section says any explanation may be merely plausible and post-hoc. Preserve “reference similarity/quality” and “decision faithfulness” as separate outcomes.
- **No-connections finding:** after complete title/description scans, focused body searches, and inspection of plausible hits, no reference, agent-memory-system, or agentic-system candidate passed the articulation test. No external prospecting was performed.
