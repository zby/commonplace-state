## Flags

Keep subjective learning, objective knowledge growth, linguistic formulation,
formalization, and external software storage distinct. Sections 4 and 6 give
language a strong role in scientific criticism, while sections 8–9 extend
error elimination to biological adaptation. This is a distinction to preserve,
not a license to flatten either into a universal storage requirement.
