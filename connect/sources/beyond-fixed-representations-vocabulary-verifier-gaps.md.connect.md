## Connections Found

- [Learning inside a fixed decomposition inherits its mistakes](../../../../notes/learning-inside-a-fixed-decomposition-inherits-its-mistakes.md) -- **rests-on**: the paper's distinction between strong within-frame search and open-ended innovation depends on separating performance inside a supplied representation, task family, and verifier from evidence that those choices form an adequate update space.
- [Improvements outside the admitted formal language need a pre-formal stage somewhere](../../../../notes/unformalized-improvements-need-a-pre-formal-stage-in-the-loop.md) -- **compares-with**: both diagnose a language-coverage limit, but the note asks where a candidate can exist before admission to a formal gate while the paper asks how a system can invent and stabilise the missing primitive itself.
- [Open-ended improvement must allocate search before decisive evaluation is available](../../../../notes/open-ended-improvement-allocates-search-before-evaluation.md) -- **rests-on**: the paper's verifier gap and proposal to preserve exploratory primitive populations require allocating search before current evaluators can decisively price delayed reuse.
- [Known-target discovery benchmarks show reachability, not discovery closure](../../../../notes/known-target-discovery-benchmarks-show-reachability-not-discovery.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: the paper argues that benchmarks with supplied tasks, admissible outputs, and success criteria test reachability inside a known frame rather than prospective invention of a missing representation or evaluator.
- [The boundary of automation is the boundary of verification](../../../../notes/the-boundary-of-automation-is-the-boundary-of-verification.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: the paper's cross-domain diagnosis says current automation is strongest where code tests, proof checkers, games, or fixed experimental protocols make verification cheap, and weakens when inventing the evaluator is part of the task.
- [System use is an initial selection environment when theory fit lacks a fixed oracle](../../../../notes/system-use-selects-theory-fit-without-a-fixed-oracle.md) -- **compares-with**: the paper proposes future reuse and distributed curiosity as signals for primitives whose payoff is delayed; the note supplies the parallel live-system selection mechanism and its self-confirmation caveat.
- [Minimum viable vocabulary is the naming set that most reduces extraction cost for a bounded observer](../../../../notes/minimum-viable-vocabulary-is-the-naming-set-that-most-reduces.md) -- **compares-with**: both value vocabulary through amortised cost reduction, but the note selects supplied names for a bounded observer while the paper requires autonomous invention that also expands feasible search.
- [Revising an improvement objective is licensed from outside it or is not improvement](../../../../notes/revising-an-improvement-objective-is-licensed-from-outside-it.md) -- **compares-with**: the paper's self-extending evaluator direction and objective-drift warning need the note's distinction between revising a verifier or proxy under a retained objective and changing the terminal objective that licenses revision.
- [Measuring autonomy well enough to see it improve is an open problem](../../../../notes/measuring-autonomy-well-enough-to-see-it-improve-is-an-open-problem.md) -- **compares-with**: the paper's L0-L3 ladder and vocabulary/verifier axes offer a candidate operationalisation, while the note warns that actor allocation is function-specific and that cross-system scalar comparison needs commensurable boundaries and grain.
- [Commonplace agent memory gap plan](../../../../reference/commonplace-agent-memory-gap-plan.md) -- **see-also**: the paper's proposed persistent primitive store, future-value selection, pruning, reuse, and adaptive evaluation have a concrete Commonplace-side companion in the plan's candidate queue, lifecycle, authority, and behavioural-evaluation sequence.
- [Voyager](../../../../agent-memory-systems/reviews/voyager.md) -- **compares-with**: the paper treats Voyager as scaffolded representation-space augmentation; the code-grounded review shows the exact bounded mechanism—successful Minecraft traces become retrieved executable skills under a fixed critic—and the missing provenance, invalidation, and behavioural-faithfulness controls.
- [What the matrix shows across 148 agent memory systems](../../../../agent-memory-systems/agentic-memory-systems-comparative-review.md) -- **compares-with**: the paper proposes a persistent lifecycle for invented primitives, while the comparative review finds automatic writers common but full write/activate/evaluate/maintain loops rare and behavioural activation evidence sparse.
- [Autogenesis](../../../../agentic-systems/reviews/autogenesis.md) -- **compares-with**: Autogenesis evolves prompts, tools, and other addressable resources with versioning and rollback, but its resource registry, orchestration, and current crash-only gate remain a supplied meta-frame, making it a concrete scaffolded-augmentation boundary case.
- [Exo](../../../../agentic-systems/reviews/exo.md) -- **compares-with**: Exo permits cumulative self-rewrite around a protected substrate, yet acceptance remains bounded by build, tests, and immediate behaviour; it therefore separates broad mutation autonomy from the paper's stronger self-extending-verifier requirement.
- [DreamCoder: growing interpretable knowledge with wake-sleep Bayesian program learning](../../../../sources/dreamcoder-wake-sleep-bayesian-program-learning.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: DreamCoder grows reusable symbolic library primitives under an MDL objective, but keeps the domain, task family, grammar-learning machinery, and evaluator supplied; it is the paper's closest formal bounded case for vocabulary growth without full frame autonomy.
- [Self-Revising Discovery Systems for Science](../../../../sources/self-revising-discovery-systems-agentic-ai.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: both treat discovery as a representational transition rather than candidate ranking alone, but the tracked framework requires verified schema or regime changes under an explicit evidence discipline while this paper foregrounds primitive invention and evaluator extension.
- [Interpolation, Extrapolation, Hyperpolation](../../../../sources/interpolation-extrapolation-hyperpolation.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: hyperpolation defines novelty as generation outside the affine hull of observed data and separates generator from evaluator; the paper asks the stronger, level-relative question of whether the system can invent the coordinates and verifier that define the space.
- [Can AI agents conduct open-ended AI research?](../../../../sources/can-ai-agents-conduct-open-ended-ai-research.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: CRUX supplies an empirical shadow-evaluation case in which engineering can be checked more reliably than research judgment, while its fixed task and protocol leave the paper's vocabulary and self-extending-verifier gaps deliberately untested.
- [LLMs can't jump](../../../../sources/llms-cant-jump.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: the tracked antecedent distinguishes deduction from invention of action-controllable premises and world models; this paper generalises the missing-invention problem to representational primitives and adds delayed verification as a second gap.
