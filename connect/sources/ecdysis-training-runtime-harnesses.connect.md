## Connections Found

- [Debugging separates specification gaps and violations](../../../../notes/llm-debugging-starts-with-retry-versus-rewrite-triage.md) — **is-evidence-for**: repeated failure does not by itself locate a harness defect. In the authors' audit, aggregation without collaborative attribution increases the share of model-accommodation decisions; policy and behavior inspection distinguish a valid gift-card option being removed from incorrect cancellation feedback being repaired. The audit's binary categories are not identical to the note's three diagnostic relations.
- [Diagnostic richness](../../../../notes/diagnostic-richness-constrains-outer-loop-learning-quality.md) — **compares-with**: Ecdysis keeps the training-score acceptance rule fixed while changing how failure records are grouped and interpreted before patch generation. Its refinement ablation also changes reasoning cost, so the gain does not isolate richer evidence from extra analysis.
- [HarnessEvolve](../../../../sources/harnessevolve-reference-trajectories.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: HarnessEvolve supplies successful reference trajectories to help localize a failure; Ecdysis aggregates failures across tasks and adds sequential role criticism before producing one patch specification. They are alternative diagnostic-input designs, with no direct head-to-head test in this paper.

## Flags

The 18.56% headline is relative improvement, from 58.67% to 69.56% mean accuracy. Evolution uses Qwen3-8B; the five-model matrix reuses its frozen harnesses. Acceptance concerns aggregate training score, not every task or policy correctness. The MSA audit includes saved candidates later rolled back, uses five author annotators and reports majority labels without an agreement statistic. Its ratio therefore does not directly describe only deployed changes. Data-curation evidence is one reduced-versus-random setting; budget and early-stop paths remain part of the measured treatment.
