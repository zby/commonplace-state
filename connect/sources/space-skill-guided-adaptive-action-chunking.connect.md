## Connections Found

- [Trace extraction as meta-learning](../../../../notes/agent-memory-requirements/use-trace-extraction-as-meta-learning.md) — **is-evidence-for**: programmatic skills induced from successful trajectories supply intermediate supervision, then their boundaries are distilled into a weight policy. The deployment policy has no skill-library access, making artifact retention and parametric learning distinct stages rather than interchangeable descriptions.
- [LLM/code checkpoints](../../../../notes/llm-code-boundaries-are-natural-checkpoints.md) — **compares-with**: a generated action chunk crosses into sequential execution before another model decision. Syntax checks and invalid-action interruption expose execution boundaries but do not verify that a valid action sequence remains appropriate as observations change.
- [Soar chunking](../../../../sources/chunking-in-soar-general-learning-mechanism.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: both reuse successful procedural structure to avoid repeated decision work. Soar retains productions consumed by elaboration; SPACE uses a programmatic training scaffold and deploys a neural policy that emits primitive chunks. Their results do not form a common performance comparison.
