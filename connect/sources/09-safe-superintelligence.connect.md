## Connections Found

- [Self-improvement is relative to a declared objective](../../../../notes/self-improvement-is-relative-to-a-declared-objective.md) -- **compares-with**: superintelligence and recursive self-improvement claims remain elliptical until the improved system boundary, assessment horizon, and objective are named; a universal reward score does not remove those parameters.
- [A proximate target is checked for achievement, not for warrant](../../../../notes/a-proximate-target-is-checked-for-achievement-not-for-warrant.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: Legg's universal intelligence measure operationalizes goal achievement across weighted environments, but scoring it cannot validate the further claim that the score captures intelligence, understanding, ethical value, or safety.
- [Warranted autonomy is bounded by oracle domain](../../../../notes/warranted-autonomy-is-bounded-by-oracle-domain.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: AIXI's exogenous reward channel and idealized environment class show that autonomous optimization is only as warranted as the domain in which its objective and feedback remain reliable and non-manipulable.
- [Computationally directed self-improvement is a fixed-boundary reallocation ending in contraction](../../../../notes/computationally-directed-self-improvement-is-a-reallocation.md) -- **compares-with**: “foom” treats self-improvement as a discontinuous capability event, while the KB note asks which decision-bearing functions move from humans to machinery under a fixed boundary; the chapter supplies no empirical allocation trace that would establish the jump.
- [First-principles analysis maps a design space before selecting within it](../../../../notes/first-principles-analysis-maps-design-space-before-selection.md) -- **rests-on**: the chapter's proposed alternative to essence debates is to state public procedures, criteria, environments, and limits, which requires separating the design and measurement dimensions before selecting a definition or policy.
