## Flags

- The stated gate uses delta = 0 and epsilon = 0.025 over two recent batches: ties can pass and limited local regression is allowed. It is not a guarantee of strict gains or non-degradation over every earlier task.
- A monotone archive peak is a selection property. It does not show every update improves or independently confirm generalization beyond the repeatedly consulted validation set; final test is separate.
- Removing the quality gate leaves an unclear boundary in the prose: preserve the explicit ablation definition rather than assuming every acceptance control disappeared.
- Baselines have narrower editable surfaces. Overall results therefore do not isolate reference guidance alone; the dedicated reference-removal comparison is the relevant evidence.
- The paper does not report repeat/seed uncertainty, complete split counts, or matched search cost. Cross-framework skill transfer is within the same dataset test sets, not automatically cross-domain transfer.
- Reference trajectories are generated with known answers and checked by a model. Candidate isolation limits self-evaluation coupling but does not make evaluation ungameable.
