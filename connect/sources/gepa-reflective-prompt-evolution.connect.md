## Connections Found

- [Reflection buys addressability](../../../../notes/reflection-buys-addressability.md) — **is-evidence-for**: GEPA turns trajectory feedback into inspectable natural-language diagnoses and prompt candidates that can be selected, recombined, and revised.
- [A retained instruction preserves what testing selected](../../../../notes/a-retained-instruction-preserves-what-testing-selected.md) — **is-evidence-for**: GEPA retains prompt text after task-level evaluation, providing a direct case where tested behavior is carried forward in an instruction artifact.
- [An accepted edit verifies the change, not the rule](../../../../notes/an-accepted-edit-verifies-the-change-not-the-rule.md) — **compares-with**: GEPA's task scores establish selected prompt performance under its evaluations, while the generated reflection's stated diagnosis remains a separate faithfulness and explanatory-reach question.
