## Connections Found

Candidate outbound edges for a future edit of the ingest's `## Connections Found` section. None of these is authored anywhere today.

- [Enforcement without structured recovery is incomplete](../../../../notes/enforcement-without-structured-recovery-is-incomplete.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the note's recovery table maps a soft oracle to escalation and a hard oracle to auto-correction; NeurIPS wired a soft-oracle detection to a blocking response with the escalation cell empty — 178 desk rejections with no appeal — which is the note's predicted failure shape at institutional scale rather than inside a hook. The note currently argues the gap from hooks and validators only, and cites no field case where a blocking gate shipped with no recovery column.
- [Warranted autonomy is bounded by oracle domain](../../../../notes/warranted-autonomy-is-bounded-by-oracle-domain.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the note separates oracle *strength*, gate *strictness*, and oracle *domain*, and says unattended evaluation outside the domain stays possible but unwarranted. NeurIPS set high strictness (reject in the 90–100% band) while the domain was never established, and the 42.7%→12.7% swing on a text-window change shows the domain was not even stable under a configuration the operator controlled. It is the KB's cleanest instance of strictness raised without a domain claim.
- [A proximate target is checked for achievement, not for warrant](../../../../notes/a-proximate-target-is-checked-for-achievement-not-for-warrant.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the note predicts that a proximate target with a strong achievement check produces "a green gate, a rising number, a policy the system enforces" and no evidence for the linking claim. AI-provenance cleanliness was adopted as the target, the detector supplied the achievement check (273 papers at 100%), and nothing in the loop evaluated whether excluding high-scoring papers improves review quality. Note for the writer: the detector sits in the verdict position, so it is the oracle; the target is the property it stands in for, and the edge should be articulated at that level, not by calling the detector a proximate target.
- [Maybe we shouldn't be reviewing all this code](../../../../sources/maybe-we-shouldnt-be-reviewing-all-this-code.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: same structural problem, opposite response, on the axis of *where judgment moves when production outruns review capacity*. Laycock's prescription is to move judgment upstream, automate only deterministic checks, and reserve late human review for risky exceptions; NeurIPS installed a non-deterministic check at the late gate and kept the human layer out of it. The pair separates "automate the checkable part" from "automate the gate".
- [Dulleck and Kerschbamer — doctors, mechanics, computer specialists](../../../../sources/dulleck-kerschbamer-doctors-mechanics-computer-specialists.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: on the axis of *what an institution substitutes when verification is unavailable*. The credence-goods paper names liability as the institutional substitute for verifiability; NeurIPS chose a different substitute — shifting the evidentiary burden onto the producer by demanding version histories from 123 authors. Reading them together is what turns the ingest's Extractable Value item 1 from an observation into a comparison with a named alternative.
- [Model types and levels of human interaction with automation](../../../../sources/model-types-levels-human-interaction-automation.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: on the axis of *which automation stage was automated and at what level*. Parasuraman, Sheridan and Wickens' matrix crosses four stages with manual-to-autonomous levels and evaluates the choice by performance consequences. NeurIPS automated decision selection at a level that executes without human veto, on an analysis stage whose output was never validated; the ICLR reference check automated the same analysis stage but left selection to three humans. The matrix gives the contrast the ingest draws between the two conferences a named coordinate system rather than a narrative one.
- [Automated benchmark auditing for AI agents and LLMs](../../../../sources/automated-benchmark-auditing-ai-agents-llms.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: on the axis of *a grader's score moving with unreported configuration*. The ABA paper's score-sensitivity and hidden-acceptance-condition findings are the measured version of what the thread reports anecdotally in the 42.7%→12.7% window change. A writer using the ingest's Extractable Value item 3 needs a source that establishes the phenomenon can be measured, since this thread cannot.
- [Can LLMs perform deep technical comprehension?](../../../../sources/can-llms-perform-deep-technical-comprehension.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: the third member of the scholarly-review cluster the ingest already links two of, on the same axis it names — choosing an evaluation surface the review system can be checked against. ## Synthesis Opportunities

- **The burden-shift mechanism has no note.** The ingest's Extractable Value item 1 — a check that cannot verify a property from the artifact shifts the evidentiary burden onto the producer, and the shift only works for producers who were already recording — is unnamed in `kb/notes/`. The `burden|onus|producer|attestation` body sweep returned nothing that owns it. Three existing notes each hold one face of it: [history has one chance to become checkable](../../../../notes/history-has-one-chance-to-become-checkable.md) (the timing constraint on the producer side), [warranted autonomy is bounded by oracle domain](../../../../notes/warranted-autonomy-is-bounded-by-oracle-domain.md) (why the check cannot settle it alone), and [the augmentation-automation boundary is discrimination not accuracy](../../../../notes/the-augmentation-automation-boundary-is-discrimination-not-accuracy.md) (what the gate needed and lacked). The higher-order claim none of them states is the coupling: *the burden shift is the institution's substitute for the missing oracle, and it silently reassigns the cost to whoever had already paid the recording cost.* Commonplace instantiates it — a grounding gate that cannot check a claim from the note requires retained quotes at write time — and the credence-goods ingest supplies the named alternative (liability). That combination, rather than this source alone, is what would carry a note.
- **Nothing in the KB names the appeal-shaped remainder.** The title sweep found zero notes on `appeal`, `recourse`, `irreversib`, or `retract`. `enforcement-without-structured-recovery-is-incomplete` is the closest and treats recovery as an agent-side strategy (corrective → fallback → escalation), not as a right held by whoever the gate acted on.
