## Flags

The main table reports peaks of admitted libraries on training streams. Gate scoring uses soft scores on those same streams and admits ties or declines within 0.02; two negative-margin candidates pass. Comparing rejected candidates with retained incumbents is a same-round selection contrast, not an independent ungated learning trajectory. The AWM port pools retrieval results into a document shared by every task, while GLoW retrieves per task. The 3.6× figure concerns average word compression; entry compression is about 3.23× at the last round. Held-out ALFWorld and SWE evaluations are separate and narrower than the twelve main-table cells. The paper's repeated no-skill runs estimate baseline sampling variation, not uncertainty of the full learned-policy comparison.
