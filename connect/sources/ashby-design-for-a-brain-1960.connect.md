## Flags

- The snapshot is OCR from a user-supplied Archive.org text file. Retained quotations must preserve its characters and be verified against this exact checksum-paired snapshot.
- “Variation–selection–retention” is not Ashby's stated decomposition in these chapters. Treating the Homeostat that way is an analyst reconstruction, not inherited vocabulary.
