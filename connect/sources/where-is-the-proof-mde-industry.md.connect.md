**Source:** [Where Is the Proof?](../../../../sources/.snapshots/where-is-the-proof-mde-industry.md)

## Discovery Trace

**Description and title scans:**

- Searched the authorized notes and sources collections for software factories, MDE, adoption, scale, family scope, and universality. The closest results were the versioned software-factory ontology, the breadth-pressure and universality notes, the 2003 and 2007 Greenfield program accounts, the 2014 MDE-practice study, and the Santos retrospective.
- Read the snapshot and the closest local notes and ingests. Rejected agentic-factory sources because they concern a different lineage and evaluation problem.

**Link following:**

- Followed the family-scoped ontology to the Greenfield program sources and compared their promised economic and lifecycle reach with this review's evidence boundary.
- Compared the review with the later 2014 practitioner study. The review evaluates the published evidence base; the practitioner study reports patterns among selected MDE users and cannot estimate population prevalence.

## Connections Found

- [A software factory is family-scoped lifecycle production machinery](../../../../notes/a-software-factory-is-family-scoped-lifecycle-production-machinery.md) -- **compares-with**: the ontology reconstructs the proposed machinery, while this review finds that evidence for benefits was mainly small-scale and insufficient to generalize.
- [Universal software factory needs a declared universality axis](../../../../notes/universal-software-factory-needs-a-declared-universality-axis.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: unsatisfactory tool maturity for large-scale adoption and sparse large-project evidence show why bounded implementation evidence cannot establish broad production reach.
- [Broad software demands create pressure for agentic factory development](../../../../notes/broad-software-demands-create-pressure-for-agentic-factory-development.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: the review supplies bounded evidence that scaling specialized model-driven machinery remained unestablished; it does not test the proposed agentic response.
- [Mass Customizing Solutions with Software Development Factories](../../../../sources/greenfield-mass-customizing-software-factories-2007.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: Greenfield presents the broad program; the review shows that contemporaneous industrial benefit evidence did not warrant generalizing that program.
- [The State of Practice in Model-Driven Engineering](../../../../sources/state-of-practice-in-model-driven-engineering.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: the later study reports bounded success and scale-up trouble among MDE practitioners, while this earlier review judges the industrial evidence base itself too weak for general conclusions.
