## Connections Found

- [The deployed system, not the model alone, is the unit of learning](../../../../notes/the-deployed-system-not-the-model-is-the-unit-of-learning.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: WHALE evaluates weight-only, harness-only, prompt-and-weight, and full harness-and-weight adaptation from shared initial model-harness pairs under matched within-domain budgets; the changing single-component ranking and consistent joint gains show that deployed behavior belongs to the coupled system rather than either component alone.
- [Treat continual learning as representational-form coevolution](../../../../notes/treat-continual-learning-as-representational-form-coevolution.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: repeated cycles update distributed-parametric weights and then search a natural-language-plus-symbolic executable harness, with each update changing the trajectories and gains available to the other. The three-domain baselines and schedule ablations make this a more controlled recurrent coevolution case than an all-three-form inventory entry alone.
- [Learning inside a fixed decomposition inherits its mistakes](../../../../notes/learning-inside-a-fixed-decomposition-inherits-its-mistakes.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: the prompt-restricted FST control underperforms full-harness WHALE by 4.15–13.00 points under the same update methods and schedule, while weight-only and harness-only each leave the other component frozen. The result directly shows an empirical cost from narrowing the effective update space, without validating WHALE's own fixed model family, verifier, task, or harness-space decomposition.
- [An experiment identifies only the contrast it actually runs](../../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: WHALE's FST control preserves the weight updater and alternation schedule while restricting harness search to system and user prompts, so the warranted effect is the full non-prompt harness-search bundle rather than any individual parser, tool-interface, feedback, context, or termination component.
- [Code complements the weight–prompt pair with independently executed symbolic operations](../../../../notes/code-complements-weight-prompt-with-symbolic-operations.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: the prompt-and-weight control is separated from a broader executable-harness treatment that can change query rewriting, tool input/output handling, parsers, feedback, turn budgets, and termination. Its gain supports the value of non-prompt symbolic operations as a bundle; it does not attribute the gain to code independently of all other changed harness surfaces.
- [A proposal-selection improvement loop requires search, evaluation, and operative retention](../../../../notes/a-proposal-selection-loop-requires-search-evaluation-and-retention.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: each Meta-Harness phase proposes executable candidates, evaluates them with the fixed task verifier, retains the archive and best score, and makes the accepted harness operative for the next weight-update phase; the RSFT phase remains the note's direct-update contrast inside the same overall method.
- [The readable-artifact loop is the tractable unit for continual learning](../../../../notes/readable-artifact-loop-is-the-tractable-unit-for-continual-learning.md) -- **compares-with**: WHALE demonstrates a research-scale all-three-form loop in which readable harness code remains a separately searchable target while weights also update. It qualifies the note's point-in-time starting posture without resolving its operational argument about validation radius, rollback, or long-lived heterogeneous systems.
- [Co-Harness: Co-Evolving Harnesses and Model Weights for LLM Agents](../../../../sources/co-harness-co-evolving-harness-and-model-weights.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: both alternate full-harness adaptation and fine-tuning, but Co-Harness repairs the harness before training and reports two math-domain rounds with explicit regression-gated local patches, whereas WHALE trains before harness search and supplies single-axis, prompt-only, stagewise, and schedule controls across three domains.
- [Meta-Harness: End-to-End Optimization of Model Harnesses](../../../../sources/meta-harness-end-to-end-optimization-of-model-harnesses.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: Meta-Harness is WHALE's harness-search operator. Its original study freezes model weights and isolates diagnostic trace access, while WHALE nests the same proposal-evaluation-search shape inside a weight-updating loop and studies coordination cadence rather than trace representation.
- [Continual Harness: Online Adaptation for Self-Improving Foundation Agents](../../../../sources/continual-harness-online-adaptation-foundation-agents.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: both couple model-weight learning with mutable natural-language and symbolic harness state, but Continual Harness directly adopts edits during one persistent embodied episode, whereas WHALE alternates separately budgeted update phases, archive-based harness selection, and independent training datasets in resettable benchmark domains.
- [Do Agent Optimizers Compound Terminal-Bench Performance?](../../../../sources/agent-optimizers-compound-terminal-bench.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: both show that an optimizer's later phase can undo or stall earlier gains, but the phased study introduces new tasks and tests transfer plus re-optimization of a fixed-weight harness, while WHALE holds the task distribution fixed and tests whether short interleaving of weight and harness updates beats a single stagewise pass.
- [Harness Engineering for Self-Improvement](../../../../sources/harness-engineering-for-self-improvement.ingest.md) -- **compares-with**: the essay surveys an editable progression from prompts through harness and optimizer code and predicts harness-first near-term improvement; WHALE adds primary controlled evidence that full-harness search can outperform prompt-only search while joint harness-weight adaptation can outperform either side alone.
- [Meta-Harness](../../../../agent-memory-systems/reviews/meta-harness.md) -- **compares-with**: the review establishes the code-grounded fixed-model implementation that WHALE invokes as its harness operator; WHALE's paper-level contribution is to put that implementation behind repeated model updates and show that the best harness changes as the model changes.
- [Trace-learning techniques in related systems](../../../../agent-memory-systems/trace-learning-techniques-in-related-systems.md) -- **is-evidence-for**: WHALE adds a trajectory-run system whose verifier-scored traces feed both readable executable-harness selection and distributed-parametric RSFT across recurring cycles, joining two promotion branches the survey currently presents mostly through separate systems or one-way scaffold-to-weight handoffs.
- [AI Agents in Depth](../../../../agentic-systems/reviews/ai-agent-book.md) -- **compares-with**: the book argues for model-Harness evaluation and cross-carrier evolution but warns that no gain from a model swap alone does not identify a harness bottleneck. WHALE performs both single-component interventions and their alternation, then shows domain-specific and moving bottlenecks, supplying a bounded empirical counterpart to that doctrine rather than an intrinsic component ranking.

## Synthesis Opportunities

- WHALE, [The deployed system, not the model alone, is the unit of learning](../../../../notes/the-deployed-system-not-the-model-is-the-unit-of-learning.md), and [An experiment identifies only the contrast it actually runs](../../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) together imply a missing operational claim: a component bottleneck is conditional on the current counterpart, task regime, and intervention, not an intrinsic property of the model or harness. A small, matched intervention can diagnose the current limit, but an update can move that limit before the next phase.
- WHALE, [Co-Harness](../../../../sources/co-harness-co-evolving-harness-and-model-weights.ingest.md), and [Continual Harness](../../../../sources/continual-harness-online-adaptation-foundation-agents.ingest.md) expose an unsettled scheduling claim for cross-form learning: phase duration must collect enough evidence to avoid noisy promotion while stopping before conditional optimization against a soon-to-change counterpart becomes brittle. The three systems instantiate different orders, clocks, and adoption gates that could ground a dedicated synthesis rather than another general coevolution claim.
