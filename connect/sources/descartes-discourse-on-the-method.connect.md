# Connection Report: Discourse on the Method (Descartes, Veitch translation)

**Source:** [Discourse on the Method](../../../../sources/.snapshots/descartes-discourse-on-the-method.md)

**Authored surface.** The target is a snapshot under the ignored `kb/sources/.snapshots/` tree. Snapshots are immutable and are never link authors (`kb/sources/COLLECTION.md`, "Outbound links"). Every candidate below is a recommendation for the not-yet-existing `kb/sources/descartes-discourse-on-the-method.ingest.md`, whose creation is itself the first prerequisite (see Flags).

**What was read.** Frontmatter and capture header (lines 1-14); Part II body, lines 80-116, covering the architect/city analogy, the rejection of scholastic logic, the four precepts, and the long-chains-of-simple-reasonings passage. The remaining ~148KB (Parts I, III-VI: the provisional moral code, the cogito, physiology, method of physics) was not read; it is out of the KB's interest band and is recorded as unprospected.

**Load-bearing content for this KB (Part II):**

- Precept 1 — "never to accept anything for true which I did not clearly know to be such."
- Precept 2 — "to divide each of the difficulties under examination into as many parts as possible, and as might be necessary for its adequate solution." Note the *dual* bound: as many as possible **and** as necessary — not maximal division alone.
- Precept 3 — "commencing with objects the simplest and easiest to know, I might ascend by little and little, and, as it were, step by step, to the knowledge of the more complex; assigning in thought a certain order even to those objects which in their own nature do not stand in a relation of antecedence and sequence."
- Precept 4 — "in every case to make enumerations so complete, and reviews so general, that I might be assured that nothing was omitted."
- Motivation — "The long chains of simple and easy reasonings by means of which geometers are accustomed to reach the conclusions of their most difficult demonstrations, had led me to imagine that all things ... are mutually connected in the same way ... provided only we abstain from accepting the false for the true, and always preserve in our thoughts the order necessary for the deduction of one truth from another."
- Compounding claim — "each truth discovered was a rule available in the discovery of subsequent ones."

That is a direct antecedent for the note's claim that authored link order, not intrinsic subject-matter order, is what makes traversal read as reasoning.

The Discourse is a high-profile instance of the failure mode, and it generalized the method to all sciences on exactly that basis.
- [learning inside a fixed decomposition inherits its mistakes](../../../../notes/learning-inside-a-fixed-decomposition-inherits-its-mistakes.md) — **compares-with**: on the axis *whether the division is itself revisable*. Precepts 2 and 3 treat the division into parts and the simple-to-complex ordering as given once found; the note's claim is that whatever falls outside the effective update space of a fixed decomposition cannot be repaired by working harder inside it. Reading them together shows the modern condition is the weakened, purchasable form of the Cartesian one.
- [topology, isolation, and verification form a causal chain for reliable agent scaling](../../../../notes/topology-isolation-and-verification-form-a-causal-chain-for-reliable.md) — **compares-with**: on the axis *does good decomposition imply reliability*. - [MAKER: zero errors over one million LLM steps](../../../../sources/meyerson-maker-million-step-llm-zero-errors.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: on the axis *maximal decomposition plus a per-step check*. This pair is the sharpest available demonstration of what the Cartesian program costs once the checker is not assumed free.

## Synthesis Opportunities

**"A decomposition claim and a per-step-check claim are separable, and conflating them is the recurring error."** Four artifacts already circle this without stating it: Descartes asserts that division plus order suffices, with step-correctness stipulated as an act of will; [MAKER](../../../../sources/meyerson-maker-million-step-llm-zero-errors.ingest.md) shows the same program works only once a real per-step oracle is bought and priced; [error correction works with above-chance oracles](../../../../notes/error-correction-works-above-chance-oracles-with-decorrelated-checks.md) gives the oracle's minimum condition and cost curve; [topology, isolation, and verification](../../../../notes/topology-isolation-and-verification-form-a-causal-chain-for-reliable.md) argues verification is a separate requirement rather than a consequence of good topology. The higher-order claim — *a decomposition's warrant and its steps' checkability are independent, and methodological traditions systematically borrow the second from the first* — is not held anywhere in the KB. It is directly useful to the atomic-step article, which is trying to argue that a note should be sized to its check rather than to its claim. Do not create the note during connect.
