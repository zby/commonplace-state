## Connections Found

- [Context efficiency](../../../../notes/context-efficiency-is-the-central-design-concern-in-agent-systems.md) — **compares-with**: shortening repeated input reduces cache reads while increasing cache writes. The paper measures the resulting full-task monetary and capability tradeoff, rather than treating cache reuse or token count alone as the objective. It supplies aggregate-cost evidence, not a proof of the note's per-window feasibility ranking.
- [Insufficient summaries and source fallback](../../../../notes/an-insufficient-summary-precedes-the-source-rather-than-replacing.md) — **compares-with**: ObservationPack keeps exact archived output behind handles, while the reducer checks selected quotations, status and hash before substituting a receipt. These mechanisms make source fallback possible, but exact excerpts do not prove complete diagnostic sufficiency. End-to-end cost and outcome measurements are needed to learn how much reliable work the projection actually replaces.
- [Experimental contrast](../../../../notes/an-experiment-identifies-only-the-contrast-it-actually-runs.md) — **compares-with**: add-one component comparisons estimate each mechanism over Pi within the reported setup. Comparing full-stack and standalone results on their own triggered-task subsets does not isolate interactions. Selecting the highest-scoring single mechanism per backend also differs from evaluating a configuration selected independently of those results.

## Flags

Search uses 152 proposal directions, 535 development environments (495 issue/PR tasks and 40 verifier-driven tasks), over 3,000 runs and 60,000 interactions. Fixed capability tolerances and efficiency metrics are outside optimizer control; nondominated passing candidates survive. Disposable skill-template lineages retain candidate/evidence and discard modified orchestration. Integration and tuning remain development work. Action Fusion's lineage adds a local trigger metric alongside fixed end-task capability checks; distinguish those roles.

The paper says 11 public EdgeBench tasks perform one-way frozen-candidate acceptance and 40 are final generalization tasks, while main tables aggregate all 51. Failed held-out checks reportedly do not return to search, but do not call all 51 an untouched final-only set. The Efficiency point is the fixed full stack; Performance is the best single component selected per backend from Table 4. It is not the same fixed configuration transferred across models.

Full stack versus Pi: GPT average score 44.833 to 42.003, Opus 44.756 to 42.224; token traffic falls 49.0% and 44.7%, cost about one third. Historical prices dated August 17, 2026 govern these numbers. Terminal-Bench CPU-only tasks fall 18/63 to 15/63 despite lower cost per solved task. IMO passes remain 3/6 versus Pi, while Codex passes 5/6. Swarms have one two-hour run per condition: SoL-Pi workers achieve 1,127 cycles at $60.11, Pi workers 1,366 at $82.12, single Codex 1,333 at $39.20. No independent search/run replication or uncertainty estimate establishes general superiority.

Action Fusion joins mutation and a known follow-up, not actions requiring inspection first. Compaction predicts future savings versus cache rewriting and does not separately price the summarization call. ObservationPack retains full large outputs for two requests then a handle/excerpt; exact pages remain retrievable. Reducer runs first on selected build/test logs, validates receipt structure, hash, status, quotations and reduction, and falls back on failure or suspected credentials. File reads/search bypass it; its receipt marker bypasses ObservationPack. These paper descriptions are not code-grounded findings in this pass. No implementation was inspected or executed.

The paper supports retained harness improvements without model-weight changes; its proposed next cycle using the more efficient harness, multi-backend development and search scaling laws remain untested. Preserve end-task capability and efficiency separately rather than treating cost per score as proof of equal capability.
