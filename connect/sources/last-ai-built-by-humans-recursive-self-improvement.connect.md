## Connections Found

- [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11873) — **derived-from**: the future ingest analyzes this supplied external source; the URL, rather than the local snapshot, is its durable source citation.
- [Compounding is tested in later improvement, not by the accepting metric](../../../../notes/compounding-is-tested-in-later-improvement-not-by-the-accepting-metric.md) — **compares-with**: the survey's structural/effective L5 distinction and matched-budget successor tests separate reusing a revised improver from showing that it produces better later improvements. The note supplies a sharper causal and cost-accounting test; the survey does not demonstrate sustained compounding.
- [Choosing what to learn requires both validity and learning-value gates](../../../../notes/choosing-what-to-learn-requires-both-validity-and-learning-value-gates.md) — **compares-with**: section 3.4.4 separates correctness, learner-relative difficulty, and subsequent learning benefit. This develops the same practical distinction between trustworthy experience and experience worth acquiring, without treating uncertainty or difficulty as direct measures of value.
- [Retained system-definition artifacts enable persistent deployment-time adaptation](../../../../notes/retained-artifacts-enable-persistent-deployment-time-adaptation.md) — **is-evidence-for**: section 5.4 reports a concrete quality-checker update retained across delivery batches, tested on excluded task packages while model, tools, and processing budget remain fixed. This is company-reported support for one bounded artifact-update pathway, not independent replication or a guarantee about natural-language rules generally.
- [Knowledge storage does not imply contextual activation](../../../../notes/knowledge-storage-does-not-imply-contextual-activation.md) — **compares-with**: sections 3.5.2 and 6 distinguish update production, loading, faithful use, and benefit. Following the note prevents equating the survey's word “activation” with evidence that skill content causally changed behavior.
- [Real self-improving systems occupy combinations no single rung captures](../../../../notes/evidence/real-self-improving-systems-occupy-combinations-no-rung-captures.md) — **compares-with**: the survey organizes transfer of improvement responsibility into levels, while the casebook profiles reflection, cumulativity, allocation, and evidential limits independently. This is a comparison of descriptive purposes; the survey itself warns that a higher level need not improve quality or efficiency.
- [HyperAgents review](../../../../agent-memory-systems/reviews/hyperagents.md) — **compares-with**: the survey discusses editable task/meta-agent code and cross-domain transfer; the code-grounded review identifies exactly how selected patch lineages enter later runs and where parent selection, evaluation, and archival machinery remain supplied externally. Keep their evidence dates and scopes separate.
- [AIDE2 review](../../../../agentic-systems/reviews-archive/aide2.md) — **compares-with**: the survey's inconclusive effective-L5 case can be inspected through the existing review's separation of task-harness gains, outer-improver reuse, and the missing superiority result. The review is documentary, not an independent reproduction.
- [Harness Updating Is Not Harness Benefit](../../../../sources/harness-updating-is-not-harness-benefit.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: this primary-study ingest qualifies the survey's claim about failure to activate or follow updates: skill loading and judge-rated procedural match are observed, but a withheld/replaced-skill contrast does not identify causal uptake. It also bounds the recommendation to invest capability in the executor to the tested pairings and benchmarks.
- [Frontis-MA1](../../../../sources/frontis-ma1-training-ai4ai-recursive-self-improvement.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: section 5.8 reports Frontis Horizon's enterprise evolution separately from Frontis-MA1's learned operators under a fixed controller. The existing ingest prevents using MA1's MLE performance gains as evidence that Horizon's meta-improvement loop or the controller itself improved.
