## Connections Found

- [Software Factories: Assembling Applications with Patterns, Models, Frameworks and Tools](../../../../sources/greenfield-short-software-factories-oopsla-2003.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: both sources explicitly make factories produce factories, but MDSoFa makes the production relation a metamodel-to-model mapping that emits modeling environments and reports a concrete generated chain, while Greenfield/Short's bootstrapping still centers product-line developers assembling successor templates.
- [Mass Customizing Solutions with Software Development Factories](../../../../sources/greenfield-mass-customizing-software-factories-2007.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: Greenfield defines factory specialization as changing viewpoints, artifacts, activities, and assets; MDSoFa supplies a computational mechanism that transforms generic expertise plus metamodels into specific expertise and packaged tool assets, while retaining human designers for the metamodels, reusable expertise, architecture, and strategy.
- [Program Synthesis](../../../../sources/program-synthesis-gulwani-polozov-singh-2017.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: MDSoFa is a primary constructive instance of the survey's meta-synthesis pattern—domain/program-space knowledge is supplied, then a generic mechanism emits a domain-specific producer—so it establishes automatic specialization construction but not domain-knowledge or intent acquisition.
- [A hand-crafted bootstrap fits the Bitter Lesson only if learning can outgrow it](../../../../notes/a-bootstrap-fits-the-bitter-lesson-only-if-learning-outgrows-it.md) — **compares-with**: MDSoFa can regenerate factories and transform generic into specific expertise, but its metamodel designers, expertise designers, architect, strategic decisions, and methodological rationalization remain outside the computational production step; this is recursive construction without the note's domain-extensibility criterion.
- [Learning inside a fixed decomposition inherits its mistakes](../../../../notes/learning-inside-a-fixed-decomposition-inherits-its-mistakes.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the factory can compose metamodels and generic expertise into tool-specific environments, yet its unified language, transformation mechanism, asset taxonomy, process roles, target parameters, and selection of reusable expertise define the effective construction space and are not tested by successful generation inside it.
- [The deployed system, not the model alone, is the unit of learning](../../../../notes/the-deployed-system-not-the-model-is-the-unit-of-learning.md) — **compares-with**: MDSoFa's operative producer spans metamodels, expertise, tools, frameworks, mappings, processes, packaging, and platform adapters; it anticipates computational edits across symbolic factory machinery but reports human engineering and feedback, not an evidence-responsive loop that selects and retains factory changes.

## Synthesis Opportunities

MDSoFa plus the Greenfield and program-synthesis sources imply a decisive prior-art boundary: computationally producing a family- or tool-specific factory from supplied metamodels, generic expertise, mappings, and parameters was already explicit by 2004. A later `universal software factory` concept is not distinguished by factory generation alone; it must state whether its claimed advance is acquiring the missing family specialization, searching over the factory-defining decomposition, selecting successors from evidence, or accepting an open-ended class of supplied specializations.
