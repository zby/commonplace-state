## Connections Found

- [Feedback-trained memory management](../../../../notes/memory-management-policy-is-learnable-but-oracle-dependent.md) — **is-evidence-for**: ContextPilot explicitly addresses the distance between a context-edit action and its later answer score by averaging rewards from sampled continuations. Its signal still combines outcome judgment, format validity and penalties; lower conditional estimator variance does not establish reward alignment.
- [Context-operation interface](../../../../notes/context-operation-interface-bounds-context-policy.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the paper separately varies the tool vocabulary and the training algorithm. Adding planning, offloading and memory tools changes available operations; learning when to use that fixed vocabulary changes achieved policy behavior. Ordered cumulative ablations do not establish every tool's independent contribution.
- [AgeMem paper ingest](../../../../sources/agentic-memory-learning-unified-long-term-and-short-term.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: both train a policy over explicit memory/context operations, while ContextPilot branches continuations at selected context-management decisions and assigns snapshot-level rewards. Compare the claimed credit-assignment mechanism, not raw scores across different tasks and settings.

## Maintenance Observations

The AgeMem ingest still describes a binary or clear task-completion oracle as necessary, whereas the current theory note explicitly admits delayed, composite and judgment-based signals correlated with downstream value. A future maintenance pass could align the older ingest's analytical claim with that narrower account; this discovery pass does not edit it.

## Flags

- Token-efficiency plots condition on trajectories lasting at least 15 turns and report input tokens per turn, not total training or inference cost.
- The paper's long-term memory tools do not by themselves establish cross-session persistence or knowledge improvement through criticism of retained theories.
- Keep training scaffolding, learned policy, inference prompts and runtime memory as distinct mechanisms. Tool-error reduction measures valid operation use rather than semantic quality of retained content.
