## Flags

OPF adds factor prediction, within/cross-factor orthogonality, factor activity and online-encoder variance to each domain's inherited base loss. Domain adapters, context/target construction and encoder architecture vary; this is a shared algorithm, not a jointly trained universal cross-domain representation. Representation mode discards projectors and prediction heads; operational rollout/planning and scientific factor analysis retain them.

The exact theorem assumes a complete orthogonal basis with Kr=d. The approximate theorem bounds amplification only when the orthogonality residual is below one. The geometry audit uses strict decomposition, exact coordinates and transpose synthesis; predictive models use soft regularization and pseudoinverse synthesis. Do not treat the near-perfect geometry table as a forecast-error result or a proof that learned factors have causal identities.

Reported matched comparisons hold data, backbone, budgets and readout fixed while replacing the monolithic term with the OPF bundle; not every individual regularizer is isolated. CITRIS single/combined one-step error reductions are 34.83%/12.90%, six-step 8.58% over five paired seeds. Ten matched dynamics tasks favor the method, but the separate capacity-matched locomotion experiment favors it in Walker2d and HalfCheetah, not Hopper. Factor masking worsens rollout error; HalfCheetah return falls for F1/F3 in five seeds and F2/F4 in four of five. Probe preferences are not preassigned one-to-one semantics. Molecular comparisons cover four systems and 100 steps, not arbitrary long-horizon simulation.

Biology reports a factor-nominated IL-18/CD73 intervention with cell, three organoid, three fragment and mouse evidence. Detailed nomination, experimental methods and complete uncertainty information are not supplied here; this is neither independent replication nor clinical efficacy evidence. Orbital scaling is one analyzed simulated run checked against a known law. Clinical prediction uses a fixed patient-level UK Biobank split and more than 1,000 risks, without supplying every figure value or full deployment evaluation. No repository inspected or code executed for this paper ingest. Future integration with ScienceIDE/ScienceBuddy, uncertainty-aware experiment choice and representation revision is proposed work.
