## Flags

- Treat the paper's approximately 2.5 meta-depth assignment to prior systems as its proposed classification, not an independently established scale. An unchanged driver exists in Meta-n too; nested wrappers and revising an improver are different properties.
- Archive-best selects the best chain per task and differs from committing to one deployable chain. The paper reports both in many cells, but not all. Keep their estimates separate.
- Appendix E caps the input at 20 traces, switches raw traces to structured summaries at depth three, truncates stderr/stdout, and drops oldest code on overflow. This qualifies claims of strictly growing or full execution context.
- Depth-aware formatting explicitly changes attention, so layer-role differences are not fully unscaffolded emergence. LLM annotation agreement is weakest for abstract roles.
- AlphaEvolve Math aggregates use different problem pools for different methods; AlgoTune seven/eight-task aggregates also differ. GPT-5.2 TerminalBench is single-seed with category variation, not seed uncertainty.
- The GPT-5.2 depth-one ablation changes several channels and uses a much smaller token budget; its gain does not isolate a single pure recursion effect. Component-removal percentages need not form an additive causal decomposition when interactions remain possible.
- Static checks and a fixed proposer reduce particular corruption risks but do not establish the abstract's unconditional stability claim. No code was inspected in this pass.
