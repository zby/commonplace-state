## Flags

This is John Walker's edition of the 1923 English translation of the June 1905 paper. Numbered footnotes belong to the earlier edition; dagger notes explicitly correct translation or original errors, including axis symbols and exchanged derivatives. Treat these layers distinctly. Extracted equations contain broken layout and control characters; no exact symbolic verification or experiment was performed.

The magnet/conductor asymmetry and unsuccessful ether-motion searches motivate the relativity and constant-light-speed postulates. Clock synchronization is operationally specified by equal outward and return light travel times, with consistency assumptions across clocks. Measuring a rod in its rest frame and measuring its endpoints simultaneously in another frame are different operations. Homogeneous space/time gives linear transformations; reciprocal transformations and symmetry fix the remaining factor. Lorentz transformations then constrain simultaneity, length, moving-clock rate and velocity addition.

The clock-transport argument extends to polygonal and then continuously curved paths with an explicit assumption, and the equator/pole comparison is qualified by otherwise identical conditions and excludes pendulum clocks. Do not present it as a complete gravity-inclusive prediction for actual Earth clocks. The electromagnetic part transforms fields and Maxwell–Hertz equations, explaining why electric/magnetic descriptions depend on the frame without changing the underlying phenomenon. Doppler shift, aberration, wave energy and moving-reflector pressure are then derived. Radiation energy and frequency have the same transformation factor.

The final electron dynamics assumes Newtonian low-speed behavior in the instantaneous rest frame and slow acceleration so radiation losses can be neglected. It derives kinetic energy and testable deflection, potential/velocity and curvature relations. Its longitudinal and transverse mass values depend on chosen force and acceleration definitions, a point the text itself emphasizes. These historical quantities should not be silently replaced with a modern mass convention and then attributed verbatim to this source. The paper offers deductions and prospective tests, not the later experimental history or general relativity. No code-based agent system is present.
