## Flags

This is a conceptual and polemical essay by Larry Wasserman, dated February 20, 2012, not an empirical comparison. Its anecdotes about disagreement and unfair rejection provide motivation, not prevalence estimates. It proposes arXiv/personal sites, public commentary and revisions, with review contributions recognized in promotion. It retains refereeing for grants and explicitly focuses on familiar fields such as statistics and computer science; medical risk is acknowledged without a developed solution. Claims that good ideas will be recognized, bad ones ignored and voluntary discovery services will emerge are assumptions about attention and incentives, not demonstrated outcomes. Do not repeat historical origin claims as independently verified facts. No software or agent mechanism is proposed.
