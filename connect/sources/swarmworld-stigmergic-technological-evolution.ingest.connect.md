## Flags

Main evaluation: 48 episodes, four conditions, three populations and four seeds, with 800 ticks. Independent conditions aggregate the endpoint-wise best among isolated agents; they do not physically combine those agents' artifacts into one world. Scheduled action opportunities are matched, not total tokens. World area stays fixed as population rises. Shared conditions improve several portfolio outcomes, but best single-artifact results are not uniformly superior. Four seed pairs limit inference: bootstrapped intervals and exact sign-flip significance differ, with minimum two-sided p=.125. Do not infer universal scaling or multiplicity-corrected superiority.

Private research state and retrieved records enter fixed-model calls; local schemas and bounded action queues constrain execution. The same model and supplied material/action/objective scaffolds persist. All agents are removed at evaluation, while installed artifact programs continue through eight held-out disturbance schedules. Lifetime discovery frontiers differ from final active performance. Programs are bounded straight-line controllers, not unrestricted code execution. Full invention labels require several supplied checks, not patent novelty.

The longer study uses four new seeds at population 100 and 3,200 ticks; do not pool it as another replicate of the initial grid or infer horizon alone from unpaired schedules. Full culture versus no-explicit-culture crossings differ by metric, with no universal crossover advantage. Selected code-fork examples have different artifact contexts, not an isolated program-edit effect. Network reuse includes observation; 95 percent of first reuse is physical observation. Message motifs, community clusters and static graph knockouts are descriptive or graph-structural, not demonstrated live recovery or causal mediation. Program ancestry and recorded teaching do not establish a full theory-criticism-benefit path.

Main-world materials use normalized surrogate physics. The protein world uses a fixed catalog of twelve sequences and supplied hidden profiles; no new protein sequence design or empirical biochemical validation occurs. Its one-seed comparison produces completed artifacts only in the no-communication and isolated conditions; do not infer a full-culture advantage from that extension. Alternative-world portraits are inferred illustrations, not measured artifact geometries. Code analysis is a separate task; this pass inspects only the paper.
