## Connections Found

Candidates for the future ingest report's authored outbound surface:

- [Diagnostic richness constrains outer-loop learning quality](../../../../notes/diagnostic-richness-constrains-outer-loop-learning-quality.md) — **is-evidence-for**: RART holds the outer optimization shape broadly fixed while adding human-rationale mismatch evidence to label mismatch evidence. Its label-only ablation often fails to improve the rubric, while rationale-aware tuning improves specificity and reasoning agreement where the default leaves headroom. This is direct evidence that richer diagnostic feedback can improve the next proposal even when selection still uses validation metrics.
- [Evaluation automation is phase-gated by comprehension](../../../../notes/evaluation-automation-is-phase-gated-by-comprehension.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the paper supplies a production case of the note's sequence. Human experts first define criteria and curate rationale-labelled boundary cases; only then does automated rubric optimization run; live monitoring continues to sample human judgments and update the benchmark.
- [Selective revision needs a faithful rationale, not just a legible one](../../../../notes/selective-revision-needs-a-faithful-rationale-not-just-a-legible-one.md) — **is-evidence-for**: a rejected explanation's reason becomes the generator's revision instruction, so a right verdict for the wrong reason can directly misroute repair. RART therefore asks a meta-judge to identify agreed-fail rationale mismatches. The paper supports the failure path and a mitigation, but it does not run the note's stronger intervention test of natural-language rationale faithfulness.
- [A proposal-selection improvement loop requires search, evaluation, and operative retention](../../../../notes/a-proposal-selection-loop-requires-search-evaluation-and-retention.md) — **is-evidence-for**: RART reflects over mismatch cases to generate rubric candidates, evaluates them on held-out validation metrics with best-checkpoint retention, deploys the selected rubric as both gate and critic, and re-enters the loop when monitored drift crosses a threshold.
- [The deployed system, not the model alone, is the unit of learning](../../../../notes/the-deployed-system-not-the-model-is-the-unit-of-learning.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the paper's user-facing outcome comes from a generator, a retained rubric-conditioned judge, bounded retry policy, drop gate, monitoring sampler, and human review process. Its A/B result evaluates that whole pipeline against no explanation, not an isolated model checkpoint.
- [Retained system-definition artifacts enable persistent deployment-time adaptation](../../../../notes/retained-artifacts-enable-persistent-deployment-time-adaptation.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the tuned rubric and labeling guidelines are readable behavior-shaping artifacts selected from evidence, retained for later calls, and staged behind a human gate with rollback. The monitoring path is live, but the paper states that drift-triggered production re-tuning has not yet fired, so cite it as an implemented adaptation path rather than evidence of a completed post-drift update.
- [Reasoning production is not reasoning evaluation](../../../../notes/reasoning-production-is-not-reasoning-evaluation.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the system separately evaluates verdict agreement and rationale agreement, uses a meta-judge only on agreed-fail cases, and validates that narrower rationale-agreement classifier against 300 human-labelled pairs. It is a non-mathematical production instance of checking the route separately from the destination.
- [Error correction works with above-chance oracles and decorrelated checks](../../../../notes/error-correction-works-above-chance-oracles-with-decorrelated-checks.md) — **is-evidence-for**: the deployment loop uses bounded judge-guided retries and drops unresolved failures, while the limitations explicitly warn that the primary judge and meta-judge share a base-model family and may have correlated errors. Human validation reduces uncertainty about the narrow meta-judge task but does not remove the correlation risk.
- [Oracle accumulation improves selection for later candidates in its maintained domain](../../../../notes/oracle-accumulation-improves-the-selection-environment.md) — **is-evidence-for**: recurring failure patterns found in weekly human review cause experts to revise the shared labeling guidelines, which seed both human evaluation and judge rubrics. The benchmark also accumulates balanced live examples. This is a concrete soft-oracle accumulation path, with manual review retained where quantitative drift metrics miss rubric gaps.
- [Beyond “Not Novel Enough”](../../../../sources/beyond-not-novel-enough-llm-assisted-scholarly-critique.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: both systems frontload human analysis into evaluator construction and measure reasoning alignment separately from conclusion or label agreement. The novelty-review paper tests a scholarly soft oracle; this paper adds continuous production monitoring and judge-guided generation.
- [The Self-Healing Agent Harness](../../../../sources/the-self-healing-agent-harness-2048912026018484317.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: both treat live LLM graders as maintained production components. The self-healing harness connects grader scores to tickets, fixes, re-grading, and rollout gates; this paper concentrates on benchmark/rubric lifecycle, dual gate-and-critic deployment, and a human-relative drift threshold.
- [Autoreason](../../../../sources/autoreason-self-refinement-that-knows-when-to-stop.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: both use bounded judge-guided revision after an initial generation. Autoreason hardens comparative selection with fresh blind judges, Borda aggregation, and incumbent survival; this paper reuses one calibrated domain judge for gating and critique, accepting correlated-role risk in exchange for consistency and operational scale.
- [We Should Take Text Optimization More Seriously](../../../../sources/we-should-take-text-optimization-more-seriously.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: RART is a concrete production instance of the essay's text-layer update claim: evidence drives search over a retained natural-language rubric while model weights stay fixed. The paper supplies empirical and operational detail the manifesto lacks, within one narrow domain.
- [LLM Position Bias Benchmark](../../../../sources/position-bias.ingest.md) — **compares-with**: the lifecycle paper acknowledges systematic LLM-judge biases and anchors its judge to humans, but does not test order sensitivity or other metamorphic perturbations. The benchmark quantifies one such failure mode and bounds how much confidence the lifecycle's human agreement figures should carry outside their tested prompt configuration.
- [ACE](../../../../agent-memory-systems/reviews/ace.md) — **compares-with**: both separate generation, diagnostic reflection, and text-artifact updating. ACE evolves a broad task playbook from execution feedback and reflector tags; RART evolves a criterion rubric from human label and rationale mismatches, adds a validated reasoning meta-judge, and deploys the result as a blocking gate as well as advice.
- [Autogenesis](../../../../agentic-systems/reviews/autogenesis.md) — **compares-with**: Autogenesis gives many behavior-shaping resources a versioned mutation and rollback lifecycle but has weak default semantic selection gates. The lifecycle paper gives one rubric resource a narrow update space with stronger benchmark construction, evaluator validation, live monitoring, and manual deployment review. The comparison isolates breadth of editable state from strength of its oracle.
- [Calibrating semantic gates against labelled fixtures](../../../../reference/proposals/calibrating-semantic-gates-against-labelled-fixtures.md) — **see-also**: the Commonplace proposal independently separates known labelled regression from sampled field calibration. The paper provides a mature production analogue: curated rationale-labelled benchmark construction plus weekly independently human-labelled traffic samples, while also exposing differences the proposal must retain, such as class balance, stratified drift sampling, and human-relative thresholds.

## Synthesis Opportunities

ACE pushes advice into generation; RART gives a rubric validation and blocking authority. The existing artifact-analysis vocabulary may already express this without a new claim.

## Flags

- **Split candidate:** the source makes independent claims about evaluator construction, rationale-aware rubric tuning, production gate/critic deployment, drift monitoring, and user-outcome evaluation. The ingest should preserve these as distinct claim units rather than letting the four-phase narrative imply that the A/B test isolates every component.
- **Whole-pipeline causal boundary:** the five-week A/B test compares judge-aligned explanations with a no-explanation control. It supports the deployed pipeline's user value, but it does not isolate RART, the judge gate, the revision loop, or monitoring as the cause of the lift.
- **Unexercised recovery branch:** weekly monitoring is live and has remained within the human band; drift-triggered re-tuning has not fired in production. Treat rollback and post-drift adaptation as implemented design plus offline validation, not observed production recovery.
- **Correlated evaluator risk:** the primary judge and rationale meta-judge use the same base-model family. Human validation reports 98.6% agreement on the narrower rationale-agreement classification task, which does not establish comparable human agreement for the primary explanation judge.
- **Scope:** the online result covers one mobile surface and similarity-based recommendation explanations. Model identities, specific must-have criteria, and several production details are withheld, and RART is not compared with GEPA, TextGrad, or other general text optimizers.
