## Flags

- Mechanical continuity differs from behavioral identity fidelity. Recall probes and matching bytes do not establish behavioral equivalence.
- Cross-host authority assumes cooperating executions and trusted custody. Local epoch rejection is not remote-provider fencing, distributed consensus, or protection against detached credentialed copies.
- Five supervised round trips and five controls are separate from qualification and the planned fault. Check counts and deterministic test counts are not independent live samples.
- The fault occurs before a reply; one observed task completion does not establish general exactly-once external effects or crash-atomic import/conversion.
- This is the v2 paper observation, spanning three frozen body revisions. A separate current code analysis must not silently attribute every paper result to one source revision.
