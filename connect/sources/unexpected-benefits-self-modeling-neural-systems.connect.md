## Flags

The retained source is arXiv v2 dated July 2024, not an assumed identical 2026 journal edition. The method jointly minimizes classification cross-entropy and mean-squared error predicting selected hidden activations; gradients can change both predicted activations and their targets. Output heads for self-modeling are pruned before classifier-weight and complexity comparisons, so post-pruning networks have identical structure and differing learned weights.

MNIST MLPs, modified ResNet18 CIFAR-10 and an EmbeddingBag/linear IMDB classifier supply three tasks over two modalities, with ten seeds and reported 95 percent intervals. Complexity is estimated using output-weight spread and localized RLCT estimation via SGLD. These are proxies, not a direct demonstration of reduced deployed parameter count, inference cost, readable knowledge or predictability by others. Accuracy mostly stays similar or falls slightly; small IMDB gains are reported. High auxiliary weights in small MNIST models break classification and yield anomalous RLCT estimates because a suitable critical point was not reached. A narrower weight distribution does not alone establish sparsity or deletable parameters.

The RLCT estimator uses qualitatively selected sampling hyperparameters and reports unexpectedly low MALA acceptance rates; the authors acknowledge uncertainty about scheduling and estimator behavior. CIFAR text says 150 epochs while figure captions/results say 250. Supplemental localization prose says 1000 for MNIST/CIFAR, whereas the figure caption says 100 for the ResNet optimum; IMDB RLCT caption names epoch 250 amid a 500-epoch run. Preserve these inconsistencies rather than inventing a resolved recipe. No comparison isolates this auxiliary target from equally weighted generic auxiliary tasks or established regularization alternatives. Biological, clinical and cooperative interpretations are speculative; no clinical population or multi-agent cooperation was tested. Code was described as available on request in this version; any subsequently located repository requires its own version-bound analysis.
