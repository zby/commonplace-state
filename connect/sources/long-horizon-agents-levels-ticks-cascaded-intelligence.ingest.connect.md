## Flags

One retrospective ten-day campaign (eight days under the clock), 211 ticks, 47 resets, 403 routine and 35 strong worker sessions, 30 adversarial reviews and seven critical findings. Twenty-five escalations include nine principal-only decisions; human attendance and standing decisions are part of the system. The paper reports 2,215 GPU-hours against a 3,000 cap, but no controlled baseline, component ablations or total model-cost comparison. Routine/strong labor spend percentages do not establish cost savings. The target is reproducing a known reinforcement-learning finding, not open-ended novel discovery. Four environment redesigns and revised stopping rules make evaluator governance material. Recipe and guard examples show reported later behavior, not quantified lifelong adaptation. General learned tier/wake policies are proposed insertion points, not trained results. Checks preserve only what they actually cover; a learned component cannot outrun a verifier only within that verifier's warranted domain. No implementation inspected for this paper pass.
