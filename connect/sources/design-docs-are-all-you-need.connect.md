## Rejected Candidates

- Zero technical debt as an empirical conclusion: the paper defines debt as a difference from a fresh build, without measuring maintenance quality against incremental development.
- Documents alone as the full sufficient system: models, orchestration, leaf utilities, human reference work and validation also participate.
- Hardware prediction accuracy from reference-model agreement: reproducing hand-audited formulas does not itself validate predictions against hardware measurements.
- General maintenance impossibility or universal document sufficiency: neither source establishes these opposing generalizations.
