## Flags

Zetema is described through functional interfaces and a reference training procedure; preserve design-versus-execution status. GALILEO is a reported external case summarized in section 5.7: five peptide rounds and claimed transfer of a design grammar provide narrower grounding, with human-executed assays inside the declared system. The paper mentions Jiang et al. (2026) there but supplies no corresponding full entry in the retained references. It does not provide the primary study's full methods, sample sizes, uncertainty or matched transfer controls. Do not treat its IC50 numbers as proof of the framework or clinical efficacy. Cross-task memory controls, held-out research mechanisms, independence axes and resource accounting are evaluation proposals, not completed comparative results. New research vocabulary is source-side and has not been adopted into Commonplace's definitions.
