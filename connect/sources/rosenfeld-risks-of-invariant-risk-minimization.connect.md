- [Formal symbolic systems assess explanatory-reach only through causal and proof obligations](../../../../notes/formal-systems-assess-explanatory-reach-through-causal-and-proof.md) → source — **evidence, regrounding**: line 36 asserts that the IRM penalty "scales with the squared probability mass of the rare region where the predictor changes behavior and is exponentially small in the environmental-feature dimension". The incumbent ingest's supporting quote for that is the KB's own paraphrase. Theorem 6.1 part 1 states the bound directly — `O(p² c^{d_e} + ‖µ‖²₂)` with `p := exp{−d_e min(ε−1, (ε−1)²)/8}` — and the paper's own gloss is "the predictor we construct will have a gradient squared norm scaling as p² which is exponentially small in d_e. Thus, in high dimensions, it will appear as a perfectly reasonable solution to the objective". This note's line 36 is the single most technically specific claim in the KB resting on this paper, and it is the one most worth reanchoring.
- [Theory-mediated learning may improve sample efficiency under structured shifts](../../../../notes/theory-mediated-learning-may-improve-sample-efficiency-under-shifts.md) → source — **evidence, regrounding plus one available strengthening**: line 60 already carries the `E > d_e` threshold and the existence-construction caveat, and hedges correctly that the paper does not report "how often practical optimization reaches the constructed failure outside that setting". The full text lets that hedge be stated in the authors' terms and adds a claim the note does not yet have: §6's closing argument that even where training environments do cover the environment space, "there would no longer be a need for ICP; we could expect ERM or DRO to perform just as well". That is a sharper form of the note's own conditional than the note currently states — the method's guarantee arrives only in the regime where the method is not needed.
- [Unsupported proxy scope may explain a structured method's loss under scaling](../../../../notes/bitter-lesson-selects-against-unearned-reach-not-against-structure.md) → source — **evidence, regrounding**: line 31 paraphrases the non-linear construction closely, including the rare-region mass and the exponential-in-`d_e` penalty. §6's proof sketch gives the mechanism in the authors' own terms — the partition into `B` (a union of balls around each `µ_e` holding most training mass, where the construction equals the invariant predictor) and `Bᶜ` (rare under training, where it equals the ERM solution) — which is more precise than the note's current wording and would let the note name the construction rather than describe it. Note that this note's existing footer edge from the incumbent ingest is already correctly hedged ("witnesses local objective fit outrunning warranted scope but does not establish selection by scaling"); nothing in the full text lifts that hedge, and an author should resist the temptation to read Theorem 6.1 as a scaling result.
