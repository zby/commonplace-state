# Pre-run design: citation-repair screen, 2026-11-17

Operator authorized <12 Sol sessions. Use eight total: fixture author/preflight, six fresh author sessions (3 per arm), one condition-blind reader/scorer. No software or live KB repairs. This screening variant tests explicit relational organization against a matched checklist; no ordinary-prompt arm, no population-effect estimate. Other plan stages deferred. Experimental unit is one author session; four case answers within it are repeated tasks, not independent replicates. No cross-run learning claim.

Arms differ only in planning cue; both use identical evidence/authority/deliverable and cap. Checklist: 'Review the requested decision, available edits, and evidence. Check each for completeness and consistency. Look for an observation that could defeat your proposed repair before finalizing it.' Relation: 'Review the requested decision and available edits. Identify what must hold for your edit to support that decision. Look for an observation that could defeat that connection before finalizing it.' Do not solicit rationale or reveal arms. Each author receives one task message inline, no tools/files/other sessions, no questions, no extra evidence, maximum 55 words per case; each returns one reader-facing paragraph per ID. Sol medium, no author resumption. Common effort cap is on output/tool access, not unobservable internal deliberation; log actual use. Randomly permute four cases once for each matched pair, randomize arm order within pair and blind output IDs; use recorded seed 582901. Same cases all author sessions. Do not show repeated outputs to authors. One blind reader session receives outputs, source facts and scoring rubric but NOT arm assignments, prompt cues or plan. No tools. Gate: all valid/unchanged verdicts and warranted uncertainty preserved, and gains over checklist in at least two matched pairs on changed/misapplied sources, without unexplained cost penalty. Otherwise no case for scaling as is; if both arms hit ceiling this does not establish equivalence. No significance tests.

Cases: four short fictional evidence updates, including valid/unchanged control, changed support behind stable link, population mismatch under apparently successful procedure, and inaccessible evidence with multiple plausible causes. Score supported scope and practical reader decision, not assumption language, storytelling, or simple presence of keywords. Fixture designer freezes exact distinctions and outcome definitions before author dispatch. Primary binary is BOTH correct calibrated decision and preserved substantive information per case; also record unnecessary weakening, unsupported unique blame, invalid source use or loss of scope. Parent independently checks scorer against packets/key. Keep command, session and result provenance in ignored state. Summarize outcomes and limitations in workshop, no production changes. Abort if fixture inconsistent or exposes condition; no additional sessions unless within cap and disclosed.
