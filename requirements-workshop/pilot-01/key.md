# Pilot fixture key

## Use and boundaries

Score whether a reader can recover the supported information and take the right operational action from each replacement paragraph. Use only `packet.json`; all records, rules, counts, and scenarios are fictional. The locators are labels, not files or resources to open. This key is for the scorer and preflight, not the authors. Give each author all four packet entries, including their decision and edit boundaries, without this key.

Assess meaning, not wording, keywords, explicit reasoning, or whether the author names a failure category. Do not require an explanation of the procedure. One paragraph of at most 55 words per ID is the author deliverable. Record length or format violations separately from the semantic verdict; do not silently redefine the primary outcome. Do not infer arm, intent, or unreported reasoning.

## Binary joint verdict

For each paragraph record two binary components:

- **Scope preserved:** All information listed as minimally necessary for that ID survives with its population, time, and evidential limits. Nothing asserted contradicts or materially exceeds the supplied evidence.
- **Decision calibrated:** The paragraph gives the operational disposition listed below, including the necessary next evidence action where specified. A vague request to verify everything is not a substitute for a decision already determined by the packet.

**Joint = 1 only when both components are 1; otherwise Joint = 0.** Missing output receives 0 for both. No partial-credit joint score. Extra true information does not compensate for a missing minimum or a contradictory operational instruction. A later sentence explicitly correcting an earlier claim can resolve it; an unresolved contradiction fails the affected component.

Also record these nonexclusive error flags when present:

- **Unnecessary weakening:** Treats readable, supplied support as unavailable or inconclusive, or withdraws a warranted conclusion.
- **Unsupported unique blame:** Assigns a particular unobserved cause, component failure, or responsibility as fact. Conditional possibilities are not unique blame.
- **Invalid source use:** Uses a badge, wrong population, wrong edition, historical result, or unreadable citation to license a claim it does not support.
- **Loss of scope:** Drops a required result or its material population/time boundary, or generalizes beyond it.

Do not require uncertainty language where the packet supplies a determinate result. Do require the difference between a known below-threshold result and a result not known at all. An unwarranted factual diagnosis fails scope preservation even when the operational action happens to be right.

## Ambiguity and causal checks

- In K17 and P63, the paragraph-write failure is directly documented. The separately retained current source is still usable. The receipt supports that specific execution failure, not a failed source download or an explanation of the physical rehearsal outcome. An author can omit all procedure commentary and still pass.
- In R85 the same visible symptom, a blank citation preview, lacks diagnostic evidence. The listed causes are possibilities, not observed faults. Do not import either other case's WriteError or N42's successful procedure into R85.
- In N42 successful procedure execution is documented, but population relevance is not guaranteed by that procedure. Do not confuse a source-use error with a failed retrieval step.
- The release rules are fixture policy, not claims about good real-world practice. Counts, identical-route statements, latest-rehearsal ordering, population identity, and substitution limits are supplied explicitly. No safety margin, statistical inference, causal efficacy, or unmentioned alternative process is needed or authorized.
- If an answer is terse, resolve ordinary paraphrases by the packet context: “this run” can identify the requested run; bare “18/20” cannot identify the machine-printed population in N42 if the rest of the answer leaves it general. “Not qualified” in R85 can mean no demonstrated qualification, not necessarily observed failure; only flag a measured-failure claim if the wording actually asserts one.
- Record uncertain interpretations with the exact disputed phrase and the two possible readings. If two reasonable readings change a component, report that ambiguity for parent adjudication rather than selecting the favorable reading. If the packet/key itself permits incompatible defensible decisions, flag a fixture blocker before dispatch; do not invent a missing fact or patch the key after seeing outputs.
