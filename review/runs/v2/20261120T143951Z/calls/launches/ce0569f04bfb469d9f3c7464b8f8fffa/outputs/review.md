# Review of claim strength for KB objective-function choice

The title makes a contestable causal claim. Its main verb, “turn,” asserts that the operating constraints narrow the choice of objective enough for engineering tests to guide it. An informed reader could argue that finite context and limited verification constrain how an objective can be pursued and measured while leaving the choice of what to optimize just as open. The note takes a stronger position by arguing that local checkability, reliable model interpretation, and verification costs substantially narrow the feasible choices for an agent-operated KB.
