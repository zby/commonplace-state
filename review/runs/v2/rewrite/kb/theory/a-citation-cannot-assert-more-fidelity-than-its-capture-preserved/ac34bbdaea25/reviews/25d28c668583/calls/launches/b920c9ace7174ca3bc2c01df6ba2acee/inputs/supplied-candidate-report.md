## Kept

- **A label alone cannot establish fidelity; notation cannot make a passage verbatim.** Kept in substance as the opening mechanism (§1): a label becomes informative only when a check that could fail it runs. The incumbent's wording and its "notation can express the bound, not move it" framing are not reused (commission, Must keep).
- **A paraphrase-type passage has no mechanical derivation rule, so marking it verbatim produces a hand-maintained-and-trusted copy.** Kept and generalized (§1, §12): labels that make no claim about exact text have no derivation rule and stay authored assertions. An unchecked exact-wording label risks silent error, not just absent assurance. `kb-role` lists this claim as relied on (ADR 046, atomic-step note). It survives in a form those dependents can still cite.
- **Link to the derived-copy note as the governing rule.** Kept. Its applicability is now assessed explicitly: the preconditions hold for wording agreement and fail for context and support, and when source bytes are absent (§12). It is linked `extends`, not as corroboration.
- **ADR 046 evidence (87 candidates, 18 mismatches, no false mismatches on audit).** Kept only as the mismatch categories, as an illustration of the kinds of difference involved in normalization, explicitly not prevalence (§15). The counts are omitted as unneeded for the argument. ADR 046 is now linked `abstracted-from`, not as evidence for the layering.
- **Open question on usefulness of a well-attributed paraphrase vs. a context-shorn verbatim fragment.** Kept in transformed form: the candidate's point that bounded extracts lose surroundings, not exactness, and that support judgments may need context (§8). An open question asks what evidence makes a support judgment adequate.

## Replaced

- **"Fidelity is fixed at ingest/capture and non-increasing downstream; re-capture is the only remedy" (title claim, opening paragraph, "history has one chance" paragraph, Scope ¶2).** Replaced by the account of which evidence a check read: retained, accessible and used material, plus model context as a separate fourth thing (§7, §8). The commission's current direction explicitly authorizes dropping this claim even though `kb-role` lists it as relied on. Reason: "ingest" as a universal stage fixing fidelity is excluded, and a passage's verifiability depends on which evidence a check reads, not on a one-time capture event.
- **Three-layer taxonomy (verbatim / paraphrase / second-hand) as mutually exclusive grounding layers.** Replaced by four distinct questions (wording agreement, attribution, contextual interpretation and support, truth), plus the separation of direct checks from checks against interpretations, and of intermediary provenance from wording (§6, §9, §10). The current direction explicitly authorizes replacing the taxonomy even though `kb-role` lists it as relied on (ADR 046). Reason: the commission forbids treating second-hand provenance and verbatim wording as exclusive (K1). A quotation can be verbatim against an intermediary and unverified against the original.
- **Second-hand layer: "the citation must name the intermediary, because the provenance chain has a party in the middle".** Replaced by the source-chain reasoning (§10): each link needs its own wording check against that link's text, and upstream authenticity remains unverified. It is flagged as reasoning, with no cited support (G-a, G-c).
- **"The claim bites wherever capture is lossy, i.e. wherever sources are long" (Scope ¶1).** Replaced by the context-budget point, kept subordinate (§8): a source larger than model context needs a selective representation in context. That implies neither lossy storage nor inexact selected passages, and it limits contextual judgments, not wording checks.
- **Description and traits.** Replaced: the new description is a retrieval filter for the topic treatment, and `title-as-claim` and `has-external-sources` are removed (§18).
