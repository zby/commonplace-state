# Reconciliation report: Quote checking

The candidate is a compact theory note on quote checking in general, written from the frozen claim account. The body is about 850 words in four sections plus `## Scope`. Title, H1, path, `type` and `tags` are unchanged. The frontmatter drops `title-as-claim` (commission reserved decision) and `has-external-sources`: the candidate cites no material outside the project, and the casebook corpus appears only through ADR 046. It also gets a new description. No `user-verified` field was present. The note has no `brief` declaration.

## Kept

- **A label alone cannot establish fidelity** (incumbent: "Notation can *express* the fidelity bound. It cannot move it"; marking a paraphrase `verbatim` does not make it verbatim). Kept as the opening: an exactness marker is an assertion resting on the author's word until checked, and paraphrase markers give a check nothing to match. Basis: claim account 3 (ADR 046 ln 17, 39). This is the Must-keep insight; the incumbent wording is not reused.
- **A paraphrase has no mechanical derivation rule, so it cannot be string-checked.** Kept as "markers that never claimed exact wording … give the check nothing to match" and as the wording/support split under the derived-copy argument. This is one of the claims `kb-role` lists as relied on (by the atomic-step note and ADR 046), so it stays.
- **Mechanical checking of verbatim quotes follows from the derived-copy note.** Kept, but limited to the wording relation and conditioned on source bytes being present at check time (claim account 12; conflicts C2 and C5).
- **Second-hand citations involve an intermediary whose fidelity is assumed.** Kept, recast: second-hand provenance and exactness are independent; each exact check in a chain verifies only its own pair, and upstream fidelity is not mechanically checked (claim accounts 2, 6, 10).
- **Distinction between what a check establishes and what reviewers or readers must judge** (incumbent: fidelity vs. authority, paragraph 2). Kept in substance as the four-questions split and the cost difference between wording checks and semantic judgment (claim accounts 2, 11). The fidelity/authority vocabulary and the trace-extracted-memory link are dropped; see Omitted.
- **Scope: textual sources.** Kept and widened into the claim account 15 scope bullets.

## Replaced

- **"Fidelity is fixed at ingest and non-increasing downstream; the only operation that raises fidelity is re-capture."** The commission explicitly authorized replacing it even though `kb-role` lists it as relied on. It is replaced by: a pass is relative to the evidence a check used (original, capture, extract or nothing). Identity checks bind compared bytes to a recorded capture only. Retained, accessible and used material differ, and missing bytes are unverified rather than passed (claim accounts 6, 8). Reason: the old claim conflated capture-time content with the evidence a later check uses. It also treated "ingest" as a universal stage, which Must exclude forbids.
- **Three-layer taxonomy (verbatim / paraphrase / second-hand) as mutually exclusive layers of a capture.** The commission explicitly authorized replacing it although `kb-role` lists it as relied on by ADR 046. It is replaced by the four separable questions (wording, attribution, context, truth) and by treating exactness and second-hand provenance as independent axes (claim accounts 2, 10). Reason: Must exclude forbids forcing verbatim and second-hand into exclusive categories.
- **"Capture is layered because it is forced to be" (context window, copyright, provider filters).** Replaced by the bounded context-budget point: a source larger than the checker's context appears there as a selection or compression, which implies neither lossy storage nor inexact passages (claim account 9). Reason: Must exclude drops the universal forced-lossy-retention claim. No source discusses model context budgets, so that paragraph rests on explicit reasoning; it is the reconstruction's gap "Model context budget".
- **"Practical consequence: provenance quality is decided before any note is written; promote load-bearing passages into the verbatim layer at capture time or quote-mine the authoritative artifact."** Replaced by the claim that claims depending on distributed material need the full capture with its availability declared, and that exact extracts verified against pinned bytes move the necessary semantic judgment to one place (claim accounts 8, 10). Reason: the original was procedural advice resting on the replaced capture-ceiling claim.
- **Old title-claim reading of the H1.** The H1 and title are kept only as a transition constraint, and the body does not defend them. **The authorized retitle to "Quote checking" (with path change) remains for the session agent after publication approval.**

The candidate instead says that an untested locator supplies context but is not evidence, and that uniqueness or range containment locates a passage (claim account 5).
- **Boundary-preserving-rival argument and link** ("no rival capture design drops the layering"). Omitted together with the forced-layering claim it defended.
- **"History has one chance to become checkable" link and the "one chance here is ingest" instance.** Omitted: it rested on the fixed-at-ingest claim. `kb-role` lists it as unused.
- **Fidelity vs. authority as opposing directions, and the trace-extracted-memory link.** Omitted as a named distinction. The claim account gives no commitment for it, the substance survives as the four questions, and `kb-role` lists it as unused.
- **Scope paragraph on lossless short sources (tweets, abstracts) collapsing the layers and making position binding.** Omitted because the layer model it scoped is gone.

- **Evidence footer: 18 of 87 `verbatim` spans mismatched in the epistack casebooks, with no false mismatches.** Omitted as a count, per claim account 14 and the commission's treatment of historical measurements as peripheral. The mismatch kinds from the same run are kept in one scoped sentence.

## Dependent impacts (not edited)

- **ADR 046 ln 39** says `paraphrase layer` and `second-hand` are unchecked because no derivation rule exists, "which is precisely why a citation cannot assert more fidelity than its capture preserved." After the rewrite, the linked note no longer asserts a capture ceiling or the layer taxonomy. The reasoning should point to the wording/support split: paraphrase markers give a string check nothing to match. The link text should change after the retitle. ADR 046's marker set can stay as a labelling choice, but the ADR should not imply that the markers name exclusive capture layers.
- **`kb/theory/a-note-is-an-atomic-step-relative-to-the-check-that-reads-it.md` ln 75–78 and footer ln 239.** It uses the old note for "a passage never captured word for word cannot be quoted into this route without returning to the source", and its footer labels the edge `grounds: the capture-time bound …`. The candidate supports a narrower point: only exact material, checked against bound bytes, can take the quoting route, and a passage absent from the retained evidence needs that evidence obtained. It does not support a capture-time bound. Change the footer label from "capture-time bound" to something like "what a wording check can and cannot establish", and update the link text after the retitle.
- **`kb/sources/peters-chin-yee-llm-generalization-bias.ingest.md` ln 95–97** says it is evidence for the old note and that the note "treats a paraphrase-layer passage as unverifiable." The candidate has no paraphrase layer. The nearest claims are that paraphrase markers cannot be string-checked and that semantic hops compound. The ingest's point (LLM paraphrase broadens scope) bears on the compounding-hops claim. The "evidence for" relation and the layer wording should be rewritten, or the link moved to the summary note it already cites alongside.
- **`kb/sources/rome-locating-and-editing-factual-associations-in-gpt.ingest.md` ln 58** says a note inheriting stronger phrasing "would assert more than the capture preserves, which a citation cannot do." That is a broader use than even the old body. The candidate does not support it: the risk described is semantic overstatement, which is question 3 (context/support), not a capture ceiling. Rephrase it to say that string agreement cannot establish support for a broadened restatement, or drop the link.
- **`kb/tags/claims-and-grounding-README.md` ln 34** describes the note as "capture fixes the evidence a citation can claim; stronger notation cannot restore lost fidelity." Replace it with a description of the new contribution, for example "what wording, occurrence and source-identity checks establish, and what remains for judgment", and retitle the entry after publication.

## Proposed write brief (not added; for the operator's approval)

- **Intent:** a compact theory note on quote checking in general: the four separable questions, what makes "exact" meaningful (normalization, occurrence and location), and which evidence a pass is relative to (identity, retained vs. accessible vs. used, context budget, chains).
- **Must keep:** the four-questions split; normalization as a two-sided error; uniqueness establishes location, not intent; the distinction between retained, accessible and used material, with missing bytes counted as unverified; exactness independent of second-hand provenance; the derived-copy argument limited to wording.
- **Must exclude:** a capture-time fidelity ceiling, "re-capture is the only remedy", a mandatory layer field in locators, ingest as a universal stage, procedures and command documentation, corpus counts beyond one scoped clause.
- **Reserved decisions:** title and path ("Quote checking"); any new external evidence.
