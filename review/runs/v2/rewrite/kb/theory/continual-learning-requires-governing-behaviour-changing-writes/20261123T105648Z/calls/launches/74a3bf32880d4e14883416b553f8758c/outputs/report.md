# Rewrite report

## Title mismatch (reserved for operator)

The title is kept as the workflow requires, but it no longer matches the body. The title asserts that continual learning in general *requires* governed behaviour-changing writes. The body has a narrower contribution: it distinguishes acquiring or revising a governing rule (instruction, check, routing rule) from retaining evidence under unchanged rules, and it gives a diagnostic for an actual system. It explicitly does not define continual learning by rule revision, and it does not claim rule revision is necessary (that question is left open). The body does not argue for the title's claim. `## Scope` notes this. A retitle such as "Acquiring a governing rule differs from retaining evidence under unchanged rules" is a separate operator decision.

- `user-verified` was absent in the original and stays absent.
- Inferences not established by the sources: the incident, its three interpretations and the rescoped validator are hypothetical constructions. The open-question candidate conditions are conjectures.
