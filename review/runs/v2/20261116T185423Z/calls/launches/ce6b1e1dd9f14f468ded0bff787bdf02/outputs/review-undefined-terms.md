# Review

## Summary

Most technical terms are introduced with enough local context:

- "Theory fit" is introduced through what poor fit looks like.
- The second paragraph spells out what a "complete fixed oracle" would have to decide.
- The surrounding sentence explains "selection environment" by describing claims that earn provisional standing through consequences.
- "Mediation evidence" and "selection evidence" can be inferred from the graded withholding and rival comparisons beside them.

The weak point is the blame-assignment paragraph. It ends with "refinement path" and "read-back", two KB-internal process terms with no gloss, in the sentence that states the practical requirement. "Branch allocation" is a minor gap that a reader can partly recover from the list it appears in.
