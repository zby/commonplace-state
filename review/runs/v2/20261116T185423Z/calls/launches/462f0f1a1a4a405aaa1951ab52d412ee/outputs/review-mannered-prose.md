# Review

The candidate figures are "earn provisional standing," "portfolio of consequences," "selection environment," "self-sealing" and "reward its own misconceptions." Each one either works as conventional shorthand or comes with its own literal explanation. "Selection environment" is the note's technical term, and the note uses its evolutionary mapping when it says claims compete and survive consequences. "Portfolio" adds the meaning of several partial, complementary signals, and the next sentence ("No one item is a complete oracle") uses that meaning. "Self-sealing" is a standard epistemology term for an environment that cannot expose its own errors. "Reward its own misconceptions" is followed directly by its literal mechanism. None of these adds connotations the note leaves unsupported, and a literal replacement would not be clearer.
