# Review

## Summary

The title "System use is an initial selection environment when theory fit lacks a fixed oracle" is contestable. An informed reader could argue the opposite: that live system use cannot select for theory fit because it rewards self-confirming misconceptions. They could also argue that, without a fixed oracle, fit should be left to human holistic judgment or to a learned evaluator rather than operational consequences. The note itself engages these rivals in its self-confirmation and "initial" sections. The title is therefore not a truism, and the claim strength is adequate.
