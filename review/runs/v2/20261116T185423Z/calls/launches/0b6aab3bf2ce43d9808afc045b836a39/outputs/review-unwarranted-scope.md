# Review

## Summary

**Claimed scope.** The claim covers settings where a working theory guides a live system and no complete fixed oracle decides claim fit. In those settings, consequences of use act as an initial and provisional selection environment.

**Support.** Support comes from derivation from the two linked notes. One separates the test record from fit. The other says system use evidences fit and causal usefulness but is not an independent test. A stated mechanism, counterfactual effect, supplies the rest.

**Exercised domain.** The derivation holds wherever its premises hold. Those premises are that the theory is used for consequential work and that its use leaves observable, delayed consequences. The title's conditions, "system use" and "lacks a fixed oracle", restrict the claim to that domain.

**Limits on the surplus.** The claimed strength is limited by "initial", "provisional", "not a final authority" and the self-confirmation section. Unresolved discriminating power is marked in Open Questions. The Scope section limits the no-oracle premise to "this research setting".

The central claim's scope therefore matches its presented support or is marked as conjecture. Only the comparative sentence about static coherence overreaches its support without a direct marker. Because it is a supporting claim, it is recorded as INFO.
