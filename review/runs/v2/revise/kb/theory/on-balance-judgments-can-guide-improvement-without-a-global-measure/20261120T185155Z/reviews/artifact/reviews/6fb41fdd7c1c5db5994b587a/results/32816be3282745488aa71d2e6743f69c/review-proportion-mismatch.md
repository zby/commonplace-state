# Review

## Summary

The core claim is that a change can be judged better on balance from reasons about whose work changed and why it matters, without a global score. The note also claims that further investigation is warranted only when an uncertainty could reverse the choice.

The longest material is the Python 3.11 release judgment and its table. That material is not secondary support that grew by accretion. It is the main demonstration that such a judgment can be made and defended while a loss is acknowledged. The investigation rule gets one bolded paragraph in "What analysis can settle". "What to do with an incomplete comparison" then applies it to the `AGENTS.md` case, so the operational half of the claim is adequately developed.

The partial-order argument is compact, as recorded in the INFO finding. No section warrants splitting into its own note.
