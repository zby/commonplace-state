# Review

## Summary

The note is mainly a conceptual construction derived from linked definitions, and it mostly presents itself that way.

- **Frame.** The opening states that "better" is relation-relative and that the claim is auditable rather than a verdict on quality.
- **Limits.** The Scope section bounds what antecedence buys and marks the two-component relation as one declarable shape. It also calls the successor routes a reading of an episode rather than a partition of processes.
- **Open questions.** The two unresolved items are placed in Open Questions.
- **Derived claims.** The unfalsifiability argument and the experiment-contrast inference are presented as consequences of stated premises, which is what they are.
- **Empirical-sounding remarks.** The statement that an inspectable acceptance test "can be gamed" is a modal remark, not a measured one.

The four-declaration list is asserted plainly, but later scope marking makes its proposal status recoverable; see the finding.
