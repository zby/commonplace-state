# Simplification synthesis: A better-factory claim compares operative states

## Opportunities

### 1. Make the four declarations the note's spine

- Location: opening paragraph ("The claim holds between two operative states of the factory, …") through the second paragraph ("Four declarations make a better-factory claim determinate …"), and the section headings that follow.
- Combines: core-claim-obscured (claim stated at three levels of completeness; headings do not follow the four-item list); marginal-value-redundancy (the opening sentence lists the parts and the next paragraph lists them again).
- Direction: open with the retention background as one subordinate clause, then the four-declaration sentence. Give the successor route a visible place in the sequence, either as its own heading or as an explicitly labelled part of the relata section. Mark the preserved-path and non-entailment sections as consequences or boundaries rather than further declarations. One possible resolution, for illustration only: "Retention is silent about quality, so whether a factory got better is a separate claim, made determinate by four declarations: …".
- Preserve or decide: the opening sentence's one distinct point, that the learner boundary does not settle evaluator location, must survive the cut. The title (the note carries `title-as-claim`) names only two of the four declarations. The author must decide whether the title stays a two-element claim, with the route and evaluator location as subordinate parts, or whether the claim the note asserts is the four-declaration one. This synthesis does not choose. The successor route also appears in Scope as "a declaration about how a given episode is being read". Whether it is a peer declaration or a reading choice that sets evaluator location affects how the sections can be aligned.

### 2. State the "relation-relative, not a quality verdict" limit once

- Location: cross-cutting. The opening ("Throughout, *better* means better under the declared relation …"), the antecedence paragraph ("That is all antecedence buys …"), the oracle paragraph in "Evaluator location and learner boundary are separate declarations", and Scope bullets 1 and 2.
- Combines: detail-overhang (caveat stated four times); marginal-value-redundancy (antecedence-limit bullet and no-oracle bullet duplicate body sentences); branch-bloat (the oracle paragraph's first two clauses open a branch that Scope defers).
- Direction: keep one short definitional clause in the opening so "better" is fixed as relation-relative from the start. Give Scope the full limit, separating no oracle from properly declared but wrong dimensions. Trim the oracle paragraph to its final clause, which is the part that supports separating evaluator location from the learner boundary. If the cost and gameability remarks are kept, they could become an open question.
- Preserve or decide: keep the distinction between the two limits (no oracle; wrong dimensions declared correctly). Keep the antecedence paragraph's positive point that fixing dimensions in advance makes the comparison capable of failing; only the negative restatement is redundant. The detail-overhang and marginal-value checks disagree about whether the body or Scope should keep the antecedence limit. The author must decide which placement to keep.

### 3. Decide the preserved-path section's role before compressing it

- Location: section "Preserving a repeatable factory-development path falls out of scope indexing", plus Scope bullets 4 ("A relation permitting retirement …") and 6 ("… stated for factories because the preserved-scope clause draws on factory development being in scope").
- Combines: branch-bloat (corollary and compounding contrast form a branch, and the retirement bullet only qualifies them); marginal-value-redundancy (the "not an extra virtue bolted onto the comparison" sentence repeats the derivation a third time). detail-overhang judged the same section proportioned, so the checks disagree.
- Direction: if the section is an illustration of scope indexing, cut it to the derivation plus one sentence on what it rules out, and move the compounding contrast to the linked compounding or repeatable-path note. If it is part of the contribution, keep it, but cut the defensive restatement and present it in the opening as a consequence of the relation.
- Preserve or decide: this is a structural choice the author must make, not a compression edit. The last Scope bullet uses the preserved-path clause as its reason for stating the structure for factories. If the section is rehomed, that bullet needs another justification or should say the structure is general. The retirement bullet would then become unnecessary. The pending `TODO!` about replacing "software factory" with "software house" bears on the same factory-specific framing and should be settled with this choice.
