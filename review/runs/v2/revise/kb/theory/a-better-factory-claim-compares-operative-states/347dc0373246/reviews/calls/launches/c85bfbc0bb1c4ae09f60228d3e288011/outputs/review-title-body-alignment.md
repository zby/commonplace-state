# Review

## Summary

The body supports the title's core: the relata are predecessor and operative-successor states rather than the change or a discarded candidate, and the assessment relation (non-regression on prior scope plus a named target) is declared while only the predecessor exists. The misalignment is in strength. The title presents antecedence as a feature of better-factory claims as such, while the Scope section concedes that antecedence is required only for this audit-proof form and that a retrospective criterion can ground a legitimate improvement claim. The opening paragraph's "relation-relative and auditable" framing points the same way. Adding an "auditable" or equivalent qualifier to the H1 would make the title match what the body establishes. The body also covers evaluator location and the non-entailment of improvement and learning, which the title does not signal; that is noted as info.
