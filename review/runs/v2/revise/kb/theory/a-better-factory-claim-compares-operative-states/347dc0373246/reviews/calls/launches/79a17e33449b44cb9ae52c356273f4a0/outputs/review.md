# Friction check: A better-factory claim compares operative states under an antecedent assessment relation

**Central claim (one sentence):** A claim that a factory got better becomes determinate only when it compares the predecessor and the installed, depended-on successor under a relation fixed in advance (non-regression on the predecessor's prior scope plus a named target), and declares the evaluator's location separately from the learner boundary.

## Filter
**Filter:** SURVIVES

The note holds four commitments at once: operative states as the relata; a relation declared while only the predecessor exists; two routes to a successor; and evaluator location declared apart from the learner boundary. They can coexist as one framework. The first two fit together, because the predecessor is defined as the state current at declaration. The relation's relativity is stated openly ("better under the declared relation"), so the claim does not also promise to track factory quality. The nearest thing to a contradiction is between the third and fourth commitments. The admission route treats the admission evaluation as evidence and not as the claim, while the evaluator-location argument treats the admission judge as the evaluator. This is an unclosed gap and not an incompatibility: the note could name a post-installation comparison as the claim's evaluation without giving up anything else.

## Thinnest joints

The top two are rated unsupported because each draws a conclusion from a premise that the note itself undercuts or never connects to it:

- **Admission judge as evaluator.** The evaluator-location joint depends on the admission judge being the claim's evaluator. The relata section had excluded that.
- **Scope carry-over.** The carry-over sentence draws its line at a clause that belongs to a different part from the one it withholds.

The middle joint, on whether evaluator location must be declared, is thin rather than unsupported. Its conclusion can be rescued, but only by a Scope claim that sits uneasily with its own causal wording. The last two are local: a dilemma that is asserted and not shown, and a "this is why" that covers more reasons than it supports.

Two joints were weighed and left out of the surfaced set:

- **Preserved factory-development path.** The claim that preserving the path "falls out" of scope indexing is conditional on the prior scope including that path, and the note says so.
- **Unfalsifiability without antecedence.** "Close to unfalsifiable" is hedged, and its ground (some favourable dimension can almost always be named after the fact) is plausible.

The definitional joints hold under the skeptical default: a discarded candidate never enters the comparison, and the compared states span the whole deployed system.

## For the human
Look first at the admission route: say who or what evaluates the installed successor against the predecessor once the staging comparison has been reduced to evidence, because the evaluator-location section depends on that answer.
