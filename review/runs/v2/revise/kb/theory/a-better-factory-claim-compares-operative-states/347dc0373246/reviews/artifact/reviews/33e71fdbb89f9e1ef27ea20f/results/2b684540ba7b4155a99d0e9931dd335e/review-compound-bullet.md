# Review

## Summary

I checked each bullet near or above the 30-word threshold. None packs two separable claims into one item.

- The **operative successor** definition gives one term, and its dash clause says what "operative" means. That is elaboration of the same idea.
- The Scope items on the oracle, on antecedence not choosing the right dimensions, and on antecedence guarding auditability each state one limit, then add a sentence that sharpens or illustrates it. The added sentence depends on the first one and would not stand alone as a separate point.
- The two-component relation item says the declared shape is not the only possible one. It then gives an alternative relation and what that alternative changes. This is a single point with its consequence, not two independent claims.
- The two-successor-routes item states one point: the routes are a way of reading an episode, not a split between physical processes.
- The final Scope item says the structure is stated for factories, explains why, and says which parts carry beyond factories. Together these give one account of how general the structure is. Splitting it would separate the restriction from its reason.

The Open Questions bullets each pose a single question.
