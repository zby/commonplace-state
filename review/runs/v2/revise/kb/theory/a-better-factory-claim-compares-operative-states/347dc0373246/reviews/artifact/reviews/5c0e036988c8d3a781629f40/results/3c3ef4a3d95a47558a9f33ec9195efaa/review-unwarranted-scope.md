# Review

## Summary

**Claimed scope.** The central claim covers better-factory claims: comparisons between factory states under a declared relation.

**Support.** The support is derivation from linked definitions plus the note's own argument about falsifiability and causal paths. That derivation covers the claimed scope, because the claim is about how such comparisons must be declared, not an empirical generalization over factories.

**Reliance the note declines.** The opening and the Scope section disclaim reliance beyond the declared relation. Better-factory claims are "not a verdict on the factory's quality over its open production family". The two-component relation is one declarable shape.

**Surplus.** The only extension beyond the exercised domain is the Scope bullet carrying the structure to other self-revising systems. Its transfer relation is gestured at rather than articulated; see the INFO finding.
