# Review

## Summary

The central claim's abstract terms are earned by what they forbid.

- **"Operative states".** The concrete term would be "installed factory versions". "Operative" is defined through behavioral authority and forbids comparing against stored or staged states that nothing depends on. A merely "installed" version, one installed but never consumed, would not be excluded.
- **"Assessment relation".** The concrete term would be "a benchmark". The relation is general because it combines non-regression thresholds with a target, under a scope index. A single benchmark score would allow regressions on the prior scope, which the relation forbids.
- **"Antecedent".** It forbids post-hoc dimension selection, which is a specific exclusion.

**Distribution.** The note's abstract vocabulary is predecessor, candidate, operative successor, evaluator location and learner boundary. Each term is defined and does a separate job in the argument; abstraction is not spread without a load-bearing site.

Whether "factory" is narrower than the argument needs is a qualifier question, not a generality one.
