# Review

The note makes no claims about model cognition. The words that come closest — "learns", "learner", "learning by the factory" — are tied to the linked definition of factory learning as experience-responsive retention that improves the factory, so they name a declared causal and retention condition rather than a mental property. Where "the operator can be the learner" and "people holding the production knowledge" appear, the subjects are humans, so the mental vocabulary is literal. Phrases such as "the evaluation decides" and "internal controls a factory places on what it retains" are conventional metonymy for system behaviour and make no claim about internals.
