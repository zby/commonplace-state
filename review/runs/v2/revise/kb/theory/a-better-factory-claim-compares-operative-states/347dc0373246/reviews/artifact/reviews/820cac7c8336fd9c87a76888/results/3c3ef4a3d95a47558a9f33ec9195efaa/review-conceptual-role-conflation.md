# Review

## Summary

The passages that import or apply concepts keep their roles apart.

- **Factory learning.** The opening links the factory-learning definition as the source of retention and learning. It then states its own contribution: a separate improvement claim with different relata.
- **Antecedence.** The note calls its antecedence "the pairwise-comparison form" of the linked condition. That is an application to a narrower object, not a redefinition. Whether the application is faithful is a grounding question, not a role conflation.
- **Operative.** "Operative in the behavioral-authority sense" points to the linked definition and does not re-explicate it.
- **"Better".** The note fixes the term as "better under the declared relation", which keeps the term apart from any property of factory quality. The Scope section repeats that split.

No passage permits two materially different role assignments.
