# Review

## Summary

The core claim, from the title, is that a better-factory claim compares operative states under an assessment relation fixed in advance. Two sections carry it:

- The relata section defines predecessor, candidate and operative successor, and explains why a discarded candidate is excluded.
- The assessment-relation section is the longest body section. It covers the two components, prior declaration and what it does and does not secure, the non-regression scope, the extent of the compared states, and preservation of factory development.

The section "Improvement, retention and learning are different claims" is secondary. It separates the comparison from the retention and learning claims. Its examples are about the same size as the relata section, but they serve the opening claim that improvement is a separate claim. They do not crowd out the core. The Scope section is a list of bounded qualifications of the core claim.

The development is concentrated on the load-bearing idea. No section needs to be split out.
