# Friction check: A better-factory claim compares operative states under an antecedent assessment relation

**Central claim (one sentence):** A claim that a factory got better compares the installed predecessor and successor states. It uses an assessment relation declared before development: non-regression on the predecessor's prior capabilities plus a declared gain on a named dimension.

## Filter
**Filter:** SURVIVES

The claim commits to four properties at once:
- operative states as relata;
- antecedent declaration of the requirements;
- a non-regression-plus-gain shape;
- relation-relative auditability instead of a quality verdict.

These properties can coexist. The note itself names the closest tension: "prior capabilities" may not be specifiable when the production family is open. It treats this as an open question, not as a commitment it contradicts. The Scope section also disclaims any claim that the declared dimensions track factory quality. As a result, the claim does not commit to both auditable relativity and genuine betterness.

## Thinnest joints

Two joints rank highest because they carry the note's distinctive additions to the linked self-improvement note: a scope fixed as the predecessor's prior capabilities, and antecedence as something beyond independence.

The scope joint ranks first. The note's own open question undercuts the claim that naming the scope makes losses explicit. The antecedence joint ranks second. Its key premise, that independence is unverifiable after the result is seen, is absent, and 'audit-proof' overstates what the note establishes.

The post-hoc-selection joint follows. It holds under a condition the note leaves unstated: one binding declaration.

The last two joints are definitional moves that the note presents as inferences. One is the 'therefore' that yields new relata. The other is the move from candidate to installed state. Both are weaker as arguments than as stipulations.

Several other joints hold under skeptical reading:
- the unit-of-learning argument places all operative machinery inside the compared states;
- the preserved-development-path consequence follows from the scope by definition;
- the ablation argument for learning follows from the linked contrast principle.

## For the human
Look first at the scope joint: what concretely counts as "the predecessor's prior capabilities," and can it be listed with thresholds before development starts?
