# Review

## Summary

The note makes no claims about model cognition. Its subject is a software factory and the comparison of its operative states. Model weights appear only as a component that may be held fixed, with no mental properties attributed.

The note says a factory "learns", but the linked definition treats learning as experience-responsive retention that improves the factory. That is a defined technical term, not a claim about cognition. Phrases such as "the comparison names" and "the framework indexes and disciplines the comparison" are conventional metonymy for artifacts and specifications. No verb such as "knows", "understands" or "believes" is attributed to a model.
