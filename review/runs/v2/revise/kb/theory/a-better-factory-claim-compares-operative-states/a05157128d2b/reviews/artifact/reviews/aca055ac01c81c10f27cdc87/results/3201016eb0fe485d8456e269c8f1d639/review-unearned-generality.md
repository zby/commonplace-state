# Review

The abstract terms in the central claim are earned:

- **"operative states" rather than "installed factory versions".** "Operative" is a linked defined term. It forbids something the concrete wording would allow: a stored but unconsumed change, which is installed in a file sense but has no behavioral-authority path. The note also uses "state" to cover a measurement-boundary snapshot of a continuously revised system, which "version" would not fit.
- **"assessment relation" rather than "evaluation thresholds".** The relation is given a structure: a non-regression scope with thresholds plus a named improvement dimension. The Scope section notes that other relation shapes, such as retirement-permitting ones, are declarable and change the consequences. The abstraction is used, not decorative.
- **"better-factory claim"** stays concrete about its object: a software factory.

The abstraction sits where the argument needs it, in the relata and the relation. Elsewhere the note uses concrete language: synonym search, a scheduled search-tool upgrade, prompts, tests, evaluators. The extension to "other systems that revise their reusable machinery" is a Scope consequence, not the central claim's vocabulary.
