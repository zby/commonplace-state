# Review

## Summary

Every identity claim checked against its target holds.

- Retention and learning: the note's "learns when such retention improves it" matches the factory-learning note's opening, which defines factory-level learning as experience-responsive retention that also improves the factory. The note's paraphrase of retention ("later work depends on") is slightly looser than the target's "later production depends on that change", but the core concept is the same.
- "Operative in the behavioral-authority sense": the operative-change definition requires a behavioral-authority path (consumer, channel, force) and excludes inert persistence, matching "not merely stored".
- "An improvement objective be independently specifiable": the self-improvement note states that antecedence, read as independent specifiability, blocks objectives fitted after the fact, matching "Independent specifiability blocks objectives fitted to the result".
- "The deployed system rather than the model alone is the unit of learning" is the target's title claim, and its opening lists prompts, tools, validators and similar machinery, supporting the inventory in the note.
- "Revision path that remains applicable after the transition": the repeatable-operative-path note defines repeatability as the path's causal functions remaining applicable after the transition, and distinguishes it from repository-wide writability, matching "not merely editable files".
- "An experiment identifies only the contrast it actually runs" is the target's title claim and supports attributing contribution only through a with/without contrast.
- Software factory and factory development are used consistently with their definitions.

All targets resolved.
