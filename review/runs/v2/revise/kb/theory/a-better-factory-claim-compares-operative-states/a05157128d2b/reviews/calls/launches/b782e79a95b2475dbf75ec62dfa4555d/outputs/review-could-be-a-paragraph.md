# Review

## Summary

Collapsing the note into one paragraph would lose several load-bearing parts:

- **Candidate exclusion.** A discarded candidate is not a relatum, so a better-factory claim cannot be read off a construction event. Staging evidence supports adoption but is not the improvement claim.
- **Two-component relation.** The relation combines non-regression thresholds with a declared improvement amount. Its antecedence blocks post-hoc selection without validating the chosen requirements.
- **Factory-development preservation.** Preserving factory development is a conditional consequence. It holds only when that capability is in the checked scope, and it requires an applicable revision path, not editable files.
- **Three separable claims.** The cases separate retention, improvement and learning. They lead to the inference that learning needs a with/without contrast, not just a sequence.
- **Scope boundaries.** The note marks several limits: antecedence is not constitutive, relations may allow capability retirement, and evaluator control of adoption is a separate causal question.

These are distinct claims, failure conditions and inference steps. One paragraph would drop them or leave them unsupported. The multi-section structure is justified.
