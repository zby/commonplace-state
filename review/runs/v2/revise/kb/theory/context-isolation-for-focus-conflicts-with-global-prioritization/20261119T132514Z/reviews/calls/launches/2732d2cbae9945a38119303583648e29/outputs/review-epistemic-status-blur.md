# Review

## Status inventory

- **Central claim.** The note explicitly labels the central claim "Claim mode: universal", gives its conditions and states what would refute it. The claim is presented as a derivation from stated premises: context is bounded, and the right decision depends on unseen competing work. This status is recoverable.
- **Failure direction.** The claim that workflow defaults set the failure direction is presented as reasoning ("Missing context alone explains... it does not by itself explain..."). It is not presented as an observation.
- **Practitioner evidence.** The quotations support observations of equal-urgency escalation and earnest tangents. The note explicitly keeps them from licensing the mechanism. The account "does not isolate" the cause, and the Scope section calls the account "origin" that supplies no controlled comparison. This is a good example of blocking spread.
- **Commonplace illustrations.** These are labelled "local design examples, not corroboration that the split outperforms alternatives."
- **Residual frictions.** These use modal wording ("can look cleaner") or derive from a linked note.

## Assessment

Statuses are generally well separated. Evidence that attaches to the phenomena is not presented as establishing the proposed architecture or the causal direction. Two passages carry declarative or empirical-sounding wording whose status a reader must reconstruct (see findings). Neither carries a load-bearing mechanism presented as established without support.
