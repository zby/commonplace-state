# Review

Core claim: a derived copy of recomputable truth must be machine-checked or absent, never hand-maintained and trusted. The asymmetry section supplies the direct justification, and the preconditions section specifies when "checked" is achievable. Together these carry the claim, and the preconditions section is the most developed part of the note, as it should be.

The scope section ("Where the rule applies") is long, especially its first paragraph about lineage, timestamps and holistic rework in the judgment regime. That material serves the claim by explaining where the rule stops and why the check price flips the regime. It does not compete with the core argument. The instances section is proportionate evidence. No section warrants splitting. The relatively compact asymmetry section is recorded as INFO.
