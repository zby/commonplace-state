The asymmetry section supplies the direct justification, and the preconditions section specifies when "checked" is achievable. Together these carry the claim, and the preconditions section is the most developed part of the note, as it should be.

That material serves the claim by explaining where the rule stops and why the check price flips the regime. It does not compete with the core argument. The instances section is proportionate evidence. No section warrants splitting. The relatively compact asymmetry section is recorded as INFO.
