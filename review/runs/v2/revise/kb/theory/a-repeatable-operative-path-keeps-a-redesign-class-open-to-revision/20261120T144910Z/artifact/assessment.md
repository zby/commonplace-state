# Revision assessment

**Proposed purpose** — Help maintainers and evaluators of improvement systems determine whether a specific kind of redesign took effect and whether the installed successor can undergo another change of that kind.

**Decision** — Stop for a commissioned rewrite of the test. The note’s central claim about causal continuity survives. The current checks can give a false negative for a repeatable direct update, and changing the checks affects dependent notes.

**First sentence a hostile reviewer would attack** — “After installation, the successor and its authority path remain addressable, and checks 1–5 remain applicable to the successor for the same redesign class.”

The fixed-test harness example makes it easy to see how an evaluator can lie outside the redesign class and how a replacement admission rule can disable the very route that installed it.

## Ranked changes

1. Separate the system’s ability to reach and revise a successor from a reviewer’s ability to inspect and describe its authority relations. Apply that distinction to checks 1 and 6 and their justification. **Cost** — dependent review and a commissioned rewrite. **Route** — `commonplace-revise rewrite NOTE --commission FILE`, with the commission prepared using `cp-skill-write-multistage`. The premise-decomposition case below changes a verdict under the published test.
2. Set the evidence threshold for a fresh successor-state revision, then apply it to the HyperAgents and tag-README cases. Resolve the two Commonplace reuse assertions in the same commission. **Cost** — focused source and trace verification. **Route** — claim decisions within the commission. The causal attribution to ADR 028 requires evidence before it can be retained.
3. Once the criteria and examples are settled, make the test easier to scan. Let the checklist carry the causal sequence, keep the direct-update and shared-actor guidance, and bring the evidence qualifications close to the inferences they govern. **Cost** — one structural read and diff. **Pass to reassess** — `readability-and-flow`.
4. Give readers a concrete gloss for the kind of change under test and for the authority relations the reviewer must identify. Identify the evidence cases where their names first matter. **Cost** — a few short glosses and source checks. **Passes to reassess** — `abstractions` for terminology and `place-external-systems` if the rewritten comparison still needs the case placements made explicit. Reconsider the title after settling the claim that the opening should lead with.

## Claims to keep

The following claims are the proposed divergence beyond any write brief. The supplied artifact declares no brief.

- Operative revision requires an installed successor on which later operation depends. Repeatability requires a causal route from evidence to another operative change in the same redesign class from that successor state.
- General editability and complete addressability do not guarantee survival of a particular revision route. Survival of one route does not establish complete addressability.
- A later episode can witness continuity from the installed state it started from. A specified transition mechanism can support continuity prospectively within its model.
- People and artifacts can share the causal work. A successor can replace the rules governing revision if it preserves or installs a viable route for the same redesign class.
- The Agno extracts establish probe-driven revisions of the target agent and leave revision of the surrounding method or platform unestablished.
- Expanding the system boundary to include a maintainer does not create a retained causal route for the next revision.
