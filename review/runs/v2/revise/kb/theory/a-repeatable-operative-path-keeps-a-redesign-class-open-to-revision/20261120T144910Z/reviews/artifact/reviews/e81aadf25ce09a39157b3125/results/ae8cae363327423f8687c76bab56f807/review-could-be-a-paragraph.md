# Review of one-paragraph compression

A paragraph could carry the headline claim that an installed redesign is
repeatable when the successor retains a viable route for the same class of
change. That compression would lose the diagnosis of where a route fails.
A change can be admitted and installed, then govern later work while disabling
admission of future changes. It would also lose the inference that an admission
rule can itself be replaced if the successor supplies another route that does
the same causal work. The contrast between a later revision episode and a
specified transition mechanism also matters because each supports a different
scope of continuity claim.
