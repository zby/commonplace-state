# Review

The mechanism sentences mostly name their mechanisms at the right generality. Scope and detail are kept separate from abstraction rank. Search and link-following are both admitted as routes into a switch. The cost gradient is conditioned on each pointer supporting a sound next-read decision. Fluidity is relative to the task and to the navigation operations available. The Boundaries section limits the claim further by separating it from grain, pointer accuracy and recall.

The one framing issue is that the link-context paragraph's closing sentence casts bearing preservation as a link-strength effect. It does not exclude any case, so it stays informational.
