# Review

The title and opening claim apply to any knowledge base. The reader may be
human or agent, and the views are defined only by scope and detail.

The narrower vocabulary is concentrated under the heading "Commonplace
mechanisms". That vocabulary includes claim titles, a description as a "fixed
routing cue", curated indexes, contextual link phrases and generated summaries.
The heading marks these items as one system's realizations, not as
requirements of the general criterion.

Outside that section, the wording is system-neutral. The criterion questions
say "an available route such as an authored link, index, or search". The
Boundaries section allows that "Backlinks, an index, or search may supply the
outward route". This explicitly avoids committing the general claim to
Commonplace's authored-link convention.

"Resolution" comes from imaging, but the note redefines it locally and
excludes the abstraction-rank reading. It does not carry over source-domain
commitments.
