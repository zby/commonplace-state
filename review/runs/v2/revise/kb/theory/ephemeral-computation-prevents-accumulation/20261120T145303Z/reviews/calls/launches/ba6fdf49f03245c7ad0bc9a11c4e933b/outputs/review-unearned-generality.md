# Review

The abstract terms in the title and central claim (`experience-dependent state`, `cross-run accumulation`, and `artifact`) are earned by the argument. Downgrading `experience-dependent state` to scripts or files breaks the central boundary claim because the causal-isolation mechanism in `## What must survive` and `## Scope` operates on any cross-run causal carrier, and the body works multiple concrete carrier classes under that term (scripts, theories, examples, test results, policies, natural-language procedures, external records, and writable model parameters). The general wording forbids claiming accumulation when all causal carriers are discarded and forbids equating single-artifact disposal with total learning loss. While the worked examples of discarded items are all code or scripts, the mechanism itself does not depend on code.
