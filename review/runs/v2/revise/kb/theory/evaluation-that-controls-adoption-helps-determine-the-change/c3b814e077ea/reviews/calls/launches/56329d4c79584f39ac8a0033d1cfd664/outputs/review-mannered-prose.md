# Review

The note is written almost entirely in literal terms. Its few figurative candidates are conventional and precise:

- "approval waits", "blocking step" and "human stop" name a step that halts execution until a person acts. The meaning is literal in workflow terms and is defined by the note's own contrast ("Execution continuing without a person establishes only the absence of a blocking step").
- "who deserves causal credit for an improvement" is a standard phrase for attribution of an outcome. Its deservingness connotation is the point of the contrast being ruled out, so it adds no unsupported connotation.
- "shape proposals" is a conventional verb for influence and is immediately qualified by "without creating a human stop".

No phrase meets the test of adding interpretation work or unsupported connotations without contributing precision.
