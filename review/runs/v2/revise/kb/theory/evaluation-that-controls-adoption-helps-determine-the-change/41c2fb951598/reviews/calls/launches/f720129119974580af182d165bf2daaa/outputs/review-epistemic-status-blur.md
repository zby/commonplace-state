# Review

## Summary

The note's material claims are conceptual distinctions and normative consequences derived from them. They are not empirical findings or causal mechanisms. The cases are explicitly marked as "two hypothetical cases", and the weekly-review example states its own limit: "This example establishes a gap against the specification, not that skipping review causes harm." The second paragraph also refuses to let an unfulfilled requirement count as evidence of failure or degradation.

The distinction is the note's own construction. It is qualitative and presented as a separation of concepts, with no quantitative or empirical-sounding claims. The note cites the routine-approvals note only for the claim that repeated acceptance does not settle what happens to a check, and that support does not spread to other claims. Nothing well supported is hedged unnecessarily.
