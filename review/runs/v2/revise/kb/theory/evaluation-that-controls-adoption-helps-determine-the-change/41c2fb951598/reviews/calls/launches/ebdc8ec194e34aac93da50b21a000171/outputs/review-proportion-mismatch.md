# Review

The core claim is that removing a human approval wait differs from removing a specified evaluation task, so a full-automation claim must say which tasks it preserves and which requirements it retires. The opening defines the two conditions (blocking dependency versus unfulfilled requirement). "The same activity can perform different roles" contrasts required approval with optional assessment to show the distinction does not follow from the activity itself. "Name the process being automated" applies the distinction to a specified-but-undone task and to later revisions, and states what an automation account must report. The Scope section is two short bullets.

Each section contributes directly to the core claim, and the sections are of similar length. No secondary section outgrows the load-bearing material.
