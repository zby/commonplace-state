# Review

## Summary

The note attributes no human-like mental properties to models. Its agentive verbs for the agent ("proposes the instruction", "adopts it", "must wait for it") describe observable process steps in an agent workflow, not internal states such as knowing, believing or understanding. Phrases such as "the process depends on human intervention" and "the specification requires" are conventional metonymy for process and specification structure.
