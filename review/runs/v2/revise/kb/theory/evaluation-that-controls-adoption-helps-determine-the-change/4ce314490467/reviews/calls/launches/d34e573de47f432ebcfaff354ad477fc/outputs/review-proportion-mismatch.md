# Review

## Summary

The core claim is that removing a human approval wait differs from automating or retiring a specified evaluation task, so a full-automation claim must say which tasks it preserves and which requirements it retires. The opening defines the two conditions: an actual wait and an unfulfilled requirement. "The same activity can perform different roles" shows that one activity can fall under either condition. "Name the process being automated" applies the distinction to a full-automation claim and to later revisions, and it ends with the preserve-or-retire requirement. Both sections bear directly on the core claim, and they are about equal in length. The Scope section is short and limits the claim rather than competing with it. No secondary material outgrows the load-bearing argument, and nothing needs to be split out.
