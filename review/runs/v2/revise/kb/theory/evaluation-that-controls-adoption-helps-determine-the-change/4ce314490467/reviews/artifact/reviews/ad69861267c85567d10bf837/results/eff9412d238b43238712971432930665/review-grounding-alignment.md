# Review

The note has two links to library artifacts.

**The comparison of operative states.** The target claims that a better-factory claim compares a predecessor and an adopted successor under an assessment relation declared in advance. Its Scope section says that whether an evaluator also controlled adoption is a separate causal question. The note uses the link as an adjacent complement: an improvement judgment does not settle whether human evaluation was required. That is consistent with the target. The only difference is the widening from installation to operation, reported as INFO.

**Routine approvals.** The target claims that repeated approval does not show that a human check is unnecessary and that full automation requires automating or excluding the exceptional check. The note invokes it when saying that removing a required approval stop requires deciding whether its check can be automated or is unnecessary. That matches the target's claim and scope.

Neither link is cited for a broader mechanism or wider scope than its target supports. The note has no source quotations or ingest links.
