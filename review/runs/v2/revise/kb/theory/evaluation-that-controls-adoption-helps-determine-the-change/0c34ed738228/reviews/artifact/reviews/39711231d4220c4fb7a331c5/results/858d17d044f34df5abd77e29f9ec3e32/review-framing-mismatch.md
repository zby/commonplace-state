# Review

The note's central mechanism is that a judgment's role depends on whether it controls adoption. The note frames this at the right level of generality: the opening paragraph separates control from the evaluator's identity, the worked cases use control over adoption as the distinguishing variable, and the Scope list keeps selection, learner boundary and quality as separate questions.

One paragraph narrows its illustration to human evaluators. This shifts emphasis but excludes nothing, so it is recorded as INFO.
