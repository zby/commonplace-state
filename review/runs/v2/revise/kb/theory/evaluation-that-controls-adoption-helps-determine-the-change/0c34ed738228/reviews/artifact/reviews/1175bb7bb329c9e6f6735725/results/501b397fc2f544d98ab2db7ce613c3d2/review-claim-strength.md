# Review

The title is a proposition, but its predicate nearly follows from its subject. "Controls adoption" already means the judgment decides whether a change enters use. So "helps determine the change" adds little that a knowledgeable reader could dispute.

The note's live claim is in the contrast and its consequence. Required approval before adoption is part of the selection that explains a system's improvement. Assessment after adoption is not, and calling both "evaluation" does not erase the difference. Someone who treats all evaluation as external measurement of a self-improving system could reasonably contest that claim. The title should state it.
