# Review

The note makes two identity claims that tie its concepts to linked notes. Both resolve and are supported.

1. "the adoption-versus-measurement distinction in [the better-factory comparison]". The target separates a discarded or staged candidate from the installed successor ("A staging comparison can supply evidence for adopting a candidate; the improvement claim concerns the state actually installed and used"). Its Scope also states: "A comparison judges the adopted states. Whether an evaluator also controlled adoption is a separate causal question; a description cannot change whether its judgment was required for installation." This matches the distinction the note says it supplements.

2. "Adoption here means entering a live behavioral path on which later operation depends, as distinguished by [operative change]". The definition makes a change operative when it reaches later behavior through a behavioral-authority path, and it excludes "an approved patch never merged". This supports the note's contrast between approval and actual adoption. The note leaves out the definition's horizon condition. That is a paraphrase at lower resolution, not a substantively different concept.
