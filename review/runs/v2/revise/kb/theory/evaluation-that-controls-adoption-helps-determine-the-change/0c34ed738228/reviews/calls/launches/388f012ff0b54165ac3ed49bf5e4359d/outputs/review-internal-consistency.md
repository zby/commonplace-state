# Review

## Summary

I compared key claims across sections.

- **Assessment and later influence.** The opening says post-adoption assessment does not, "through that assessment alone", determine adoption. The cases section adds that a later assessment can cause a future revision or rollback, and assigns that effect to a different transition. The Scope bullet makes the same limit explicit. These are consistent.
- **Selection and learner membership.** The approval case says the maintainer's judgment helps select the instruction and must be included in the account. The Scope says participation in selection does not settle learner membership but must still be acknowledged as a contribution. These are consistent.
- **Description and body.** The description says a different description cannot remove an evaluator's causal contribution. The body says a different description cannot turn required approval into assessment after adoption. The two match.

Control is used throughout to mean required approval of the change being examined. I found no contradiction or definition drift.
