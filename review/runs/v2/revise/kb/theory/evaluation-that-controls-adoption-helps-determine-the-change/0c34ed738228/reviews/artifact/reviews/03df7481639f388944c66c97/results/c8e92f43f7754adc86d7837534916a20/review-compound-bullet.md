# Review

Only two Scope bullets are longer than about 30 words. The second ("Participation in selection does not by itself determine…") develops one idea: membership in the learner depends on the declared boundary, and an external judgment must still be acknowledged as a contribution. The extra detail elaborates that single point, so the item passes.

The third bullet holds two separable points. Its opening point is that approval does not establish quality. Its closing point is about tests that the proposing system can optimize against, and about justifying the assessment. That closing point stands on its own and would be easier to see as a separate bullet. The first bullet and the Open Questions bullet are short and contain one point each.
