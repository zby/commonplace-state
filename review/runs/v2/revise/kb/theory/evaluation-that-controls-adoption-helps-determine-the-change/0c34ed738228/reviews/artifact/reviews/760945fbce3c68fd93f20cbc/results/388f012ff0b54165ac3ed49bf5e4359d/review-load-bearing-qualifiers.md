# Review

## Summary

I applied the deletion test to the qualifiers in the title, description and opening.

- **"that controls adoption".** This is the claim's discriminating condition. Without it, the claim would cover post-adoption assessment, which the note argues against. It is load-bearing.
- **"helps".** This keeps the claim from asserting that the evaluator alone determines the change. The proposing agent and the Scope's separation of contribution from learner membership depend on that partial role. It is load-bearing.
- **"through that assessment alone".** This preserves the boundary case of a later assessment that causes a rollback or future revision. It is load-bearing.
- **"required".** The phrase "approval was required" is the operational test for control. It is load-bearing.

The opening explicitly removes one possible narrowing qualifier, human versus program. The KB-instruction setting appears only in the hypothetical cases and does not narrow the central claim. No qualifier narrows the claim without doing work.
