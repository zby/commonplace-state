# Review

## Summary

The core claim is that a judgment controlling adoption contributes to selecting the change, while a judgment made after adoption does not, through that judgment alone. The section "The same activity can perform different roles" carries this claim with two contrasted cases and a closing paragraph stating which verdict controls adoption. It is the most developed section.

"One episode can contain both roles" extends the claim to mixed episodes and fixes the meaning of adoption. It is shorter and directly supports the core distinction. The Scope section bounds the claim in three brief bullets and does not outgrow the core material. No secondary section is overdeveloped relative to the load-bearing argument, and nothing calls for splitting.
