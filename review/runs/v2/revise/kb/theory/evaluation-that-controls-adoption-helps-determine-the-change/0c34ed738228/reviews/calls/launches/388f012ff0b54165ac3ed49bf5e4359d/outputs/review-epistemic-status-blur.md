# Review

## Summary

The note's material claims are conceptual derivations about causal role. Judgment that gates adoption is part of the selection process. Assessment after adoption is not part of that selection, though it can cause a later transition. Whether approval was required is a fact about the process, not a matter of description. These follow from the stated meaning of controlling adoption and are presented as such.

The worked cases are explicitly labelled as hypothetical, "not reports of observed behavior", so they are not presented as evidence. The empirical-sounding remark that a test available to the proposing system can be optimized without improving capability is stated as a possibility ("can"), not as a measured result. The Scope bullets limit what the distinction establishes: it does not establish absence of other influence, learner membership, or quality. The open question about evaluators outside the learner boundary is marked as unresolved.

No load-bearing claim takes on confidence from support that belongs to another claim. No well-supported claim is hedged without reason.
