# Review

## Summary

The note makes three identity claims. All three match their targets.

- "the preserved-capability consequence of [the better-factory comparison]": the target says that when the checked prior scope includes a factory-development path, non-regression requires the successor to preserve that capability. Its Scope section calls this "the preserved-path consequence". The note's naming differs only in wording.
- The note says "those functions" (evidence leading to a proposed change, an adoption decision, installation and later use) are what a [repeatable operative path] keeps available. It also says "that note supplies the detailed test". The target's operational test covers determination, admission, installation and dependence. Its continuity check keeps these applicable after the transition for the same redesign class. The target also includes representation, which the note's list leaves out. That is a narrower summary, not a different concept.
- "That contribution is [tested in later improvement episodes]": the target's title and opening say that compounding evidence must come from later improvement episodes, not from the accepting metric. This matches the note's claim that contribution to later improvement is not inferred from the accepting comparison.

All targets resolved.
