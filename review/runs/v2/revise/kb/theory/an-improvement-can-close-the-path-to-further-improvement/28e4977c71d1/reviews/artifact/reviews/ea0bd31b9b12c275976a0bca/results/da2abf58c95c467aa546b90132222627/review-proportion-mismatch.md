# Review

The core claim has two parts: a production improvement can disable the revision capability, and non-regression prevents this only when that capability is in the checked scope. The first section develops the first part with a worked hypothetical case and a boundary on what counts as survival. The second section develops the conditional protection in three paragraphs, including the point that the capability does not have to stay in its original component. These two sections receive the most space.

The third section, about remaining able to improve versus improving again, is two short paragraphs that delimit the claim and defer to a linked note. The Scope list is brief. No secondary section is larger than the load-bearing material, and no section calls for splitting.
