# Review

## Summary

The title asserts that an improvement can close the path to further improvement. An informed person could reasonably argue the opposite. One view says a change that disables future revision is not an improvement at all. Another says that non-regression already preserves revision capability. The note answers the second view directly: the better-factory comparison it supplements treats preserved capability as a consequence, and this note limits that consequence to cases where revision capability is in the checked scope. So the claim has a live opponent and passes.

The weakness is the modal "can". On its face, it reads as a possibility claim that nobody would contest. Most of the note's force lies in its mechanism: checking the gain does not check whether the revision mechanism survived. Sharpening the title toward that mechanism would make its contestability visible without the body's help. This is recorded as advisory.
