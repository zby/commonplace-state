# Review

## Summary

The H1 says an improvement can close the path to further improvement. The opening paragraph and the section "A gain can consume the mechanism that produced it" support that claim directly. The hypothetical agent completes its declared tasks faster but has lost its route for testing and installing further instruction changes.

The later sections stay within the title's subject. "When non-regression protects the revision path" states when the closure is caught. "Remaining able to improve is not improving again" limits what keeping the path open implies. The Scope section bounds the claim by system boundary and kind of revision. None of these sections moves the note to a different claim or to a broader or narrower scope than the title. The note has not drifted, so it passes. The informational finding records that the non-regression condition is a substantial part of the body but appears only in the description.
