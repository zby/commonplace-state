# Review

The central claim is a possibility claim: an improvement *can* close the path to further improvement, and a production gain alone does not establish that the path is preserved.

**Claimed scope.** The note invites reliance in one direction only: do not infer preservation of the revision path from a production gain, for any system that revises itself.

**Support.**

- A derivation from how comparisons work: a check covers only its checked scope.
- A coherent hypothetical instance.
- The preserved-capability consequence inherited from the better-factory note.

**Exercised domain.** The derivation's premise holds for any comparison over declared dimensions, so the derivation covers the full claimed scope. One coherent instance is enough to show that the outcome is possible.

**Limits.** The note claims no frequency or prevalence. Its Scope section explicitly declines the stronger claim that every system must preserve self-revision. The claimed scope matches the exercised domain.
