# Review

## Summary

Most mechanism sentences are framed at the right level of generality. Warrant is defined broadly enough to cover empirical, formal, source-derived and procedural claims. Fit is tied to explanation, search, prediction, modification and revision rather than to one domain. The contradiction paragraph lists several repair locations instead of naming only the new claim.

One sentence frames relational fit as concerning "warranted claims", although the mechanism covers unwarranted claims too, as the "apparent fit" paragraph shows. Because the paragraph is about a warranted new claim, this narrowing reads as emphasis, so the finding is INFO.
