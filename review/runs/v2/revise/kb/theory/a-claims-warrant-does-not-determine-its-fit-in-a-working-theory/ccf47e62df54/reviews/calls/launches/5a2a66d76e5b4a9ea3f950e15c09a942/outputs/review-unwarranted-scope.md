# Review

Claimed scope: any claim used in any working theory. For all of them, the note says warrant does not determine fit, and apparent fit does not establish warrant.

Presented support: a derivation by counterexample classes. The note shows that each direction of determination fails through mechanisms that can occur in any working theory. A warranted claim can duplicate an existing claim, sit at the wrong abstraction level, or be irrelevant to the theory's purpose. An unwarranted claim can integrate smoothly because its neighbours already assume it.

Exercised domain: a negative "does not determine" claim needs only that such cases are possible in the class. The counterexample classes depend only on a theory containing several claims and having a purpose. Those properties hold for every working theory the claim covers. So the derivation covers the full claimed scope, and no transfer relation is needed.

The secondary claims are worded as possibilities with "may" or "can", and they stay within what the derivation supports. The Scope section further limits the claim: it is not a claim of statistical independence. The reliance the note invites matches the support it presents.
