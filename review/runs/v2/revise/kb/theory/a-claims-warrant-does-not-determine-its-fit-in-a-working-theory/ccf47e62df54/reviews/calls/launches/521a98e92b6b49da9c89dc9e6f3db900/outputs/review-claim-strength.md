# Review

## Summary

The title "A claim's warrant does not determine its fit in a working theory" is contestable. An informed reader could reasonably argue the opposite: that a true or well-supported claim belongs in any theory about its domain, or, from a coherentist position, that fit within a body of claims is itself what warrants a claim. The title separates two evaluations that many practices of knowledge curation conflate, such as "it's true, so keep it" or "it works, so it's right". The claim is therefore not a truism, and the gate passes.
