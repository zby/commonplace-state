# Review

Across sections, the key claims are these. Warrant and fit are separate evaluations. A warranted claim may fit poorly. Apparent fit does not imply warrant. A contradiction may call for revising something other than the new claim. The evaluations may exchange evidence but must not substitute for each other. Warrant and fit are not claimed to be statistically independent. No two of these claims contradict each other.

The title is one-directional: warrant does not determine fit. The description and body argue both directions. That widens the claim rather than contradicting it, and the description states both directions.

The one tension is in how fit is defined, recorded as INFO. The opening and the third paragraph treat fit as a property any claim can have. The relational summary in the fourth paragraph frames fit as the composition of warranted claims. The third paragraph's choice of "apparent fit" may be meant to resolve this, but the note never says whether apparent fit is a kind of fit or something different.
