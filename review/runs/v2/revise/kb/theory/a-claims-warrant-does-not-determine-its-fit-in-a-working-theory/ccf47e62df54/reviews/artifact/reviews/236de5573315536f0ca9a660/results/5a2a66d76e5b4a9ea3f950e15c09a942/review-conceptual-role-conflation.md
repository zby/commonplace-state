# Review

The note introduces two evaluations, warrant and fit, and applies them to claims within a working theory. It imports no external concept, cites no prior account and assigns no attribution. So the roles at risk of blurring are limited to the term, the note's own explication and the object it applies to.

The passages that define a concept are the opening paragraph and the two Scope entries on warrant and fit. Each one keeps the roles apart. The opening states the question each evaluation asks. The Scope entries say how broadly the note uses "warrant" and that "fit" is deliberately not given a complete scalar definition. The object of application is "a claim used in a working theory", and that stays the same throughout.

"Theory fit is therefore relational" could be read either as a definition of fit or as a consequence derived from the contradiction case. Either reading gives the same scope and the same contribution: fit concerns how claims compose rather than how a claim performs in isolation. That fails the materiality test. Any wording drift between paragraphs belongs to internal consistency, not to this gate.
