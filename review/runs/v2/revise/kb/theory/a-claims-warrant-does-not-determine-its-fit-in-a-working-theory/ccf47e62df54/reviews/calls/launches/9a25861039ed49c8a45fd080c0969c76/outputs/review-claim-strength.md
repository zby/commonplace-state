# Review

## Summary

The title "A claim's warrant does not determine its fit in a working theory" is contestable. An informed reader could argue the opposite from several positions: a coherentist could hold that fit with the surrounding theory is what warrant consists in, and a simple accumulation view of knowledge bases holds that any well-supported claim deserves inclusion. The title therefore asserts something that knowledgeable people could reasonably dispute, not a truism. It also names the non-obvious point the note develops: independent support for a claim and its role in an operative theory need separate evaluations.
