# Friction check: A claim's warrant does not determine its fit in a working theory

**Central claim (one sentence):** Whether a claim is independently warranted and whether it fits a working theory are distinct evaluations, so neither settles the other, although each may supply evidence the other must weigh.

## Filter
**Filter:** SURVIVES

The note wants three things at once. Neither evaluation determines the other. The two evaluations may exchange evidence. They are not claimed to be statistically independent. These can coexist if "determine" means "settle" and "inform" means "supply defeasible evidence", and the note's Scope section adopts that reading. The main pressure is the relational paragraph's definition of fit as the composition of *warranted* claims. That definition would make warrant necessary for genuine fit, so the "neither direction" symmetry would need to become "warrant is necessary but not sufficient for fit". The tension narrows the claim but does not split it into incompatible commitments, so it appears as the top joint rather than as dissolution.

## Thinnest joints

The top joint ranks first because it is the note's only explicit theoretical characterization of fit. It is reached by a "therefore" from premises about repair options, and it introduces a dependency of fit on warrant that the rest of the note denies. The next three joints all fall at boundaries the note leaves unspecified. The contradiction paragraph does not show what the distinction adds beyond holism. Prediction appears on both sides of the distinction. The line between legitimate evidence exchange and substitution is drawn only with "silently". Each of these is a place where an operator applying the note would have to supply the missing criterion. The coherence joint is surfaced as the weakest of the joints that hold. Its mechanism, circular pre-assumption, is stated and its conclusion is hedged to match. The warranted-but-poorly-fitting paragraph is stronger still: its list of fit failures gives concrete counterexamples.

## For the human
Look first at "how warranted claims compose" in the relational paragraph. Decide whether an unwarranted claim can have genuine fit or only apparent fit, because that choice decides whether the title's non-determination holds in both directions.
