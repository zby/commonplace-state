# Review

The core claim is that warrant and fit are distinct evaluations, neither determining the other. The opening section gives it most of the space: one paragraph defines the two evaluations, and one paragraph each develops the two directions of non-determination (warranted but poorly fitting; fitting but unwarranted). A fourth paragraph draws the consequence for interpreting contradictions.

The secondary section on how the evaluations inform without substituting is two short paragraphs, and the Scope list is four bounded qualifications. Neither outgrows the load-bearing material. No section warrants splitting into its own note.
