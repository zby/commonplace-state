# Review

The title "A claim's test record does not determine its fit in a working theory" is contestable. An informed opponent could argue the opposite: that a theory should retain whatever claims are best supported, so that a strong test record is sufficient grounds for inclusion. That position is common in naive accumulation-style knowledge bases and in some reliabilist views of belief revision. The note denies it and gives reasons: a well-tested claim can be irrelevant, redundant, mis-scoped or at the wrong level of abstraction for the theory's decisions. The claim is therefore not a truism, and the title states the non-obvious point the note argues.
