# Review

## Summary

The passages that define or import concepts mostly keep their roles distinct. The note states its own explications of "test record", "independent" and "fit" as its own definitions. It also names the change of term explicitly ("uses 'test record' where it earlier used 'warrant'") and gives the reason, which separates the term from the concept it replaced.

The one place where roles touch is the Popper paragraph. There the source account (corroboration as a dated appraisal) supports the note's broader explication (a test record that includes formal and source checks). Nearby wording keeps Popper's vocabulary as his, so a reader can tell that the note applies his distinction and does not claim that he defined "test record". This is recorded as INFO only.
