# Report

Fixed finding 1 (warn, derived formal claims) with
`fix-strategy-taxonomy.md#boundary-case-acknowledged`. The note admits formal
claims whose test is a derivation, but it said that every surviving claim is
still a conjecture and that universal claims are never established. Both
edits stay inside the scope passages and say what a derivation settles: the
formal claim, relative to its premises. The premises stay conjectures, so the
section's main claim, that a test record is not truth, holds without being
widened. No defended claim was narrowed. The Popper citation and every link
are unchanged.

uses that rely on neighbouring retained claims). Its passage is outside the

"A test record is not truth".
