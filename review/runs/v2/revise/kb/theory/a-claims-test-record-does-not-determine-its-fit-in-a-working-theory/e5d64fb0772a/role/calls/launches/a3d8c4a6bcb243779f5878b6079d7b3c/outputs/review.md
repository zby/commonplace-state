# Role in the KB: A claim's test record does not determine its fit in a working theory

## Role
The note supplies the premise that a claim's independent test record and its fit in a working theory are separate standings, with fit counting as a test only where the claim could have failed. Two theory notes on system use argue from it as their ground, two tag heads cite it as the KB's statement of that distinction, and three source ingests position formal results (ATMS, formal learning theory, AGM) against it as counterpoint or support, two of them recommending additions.
Relied on: the test-record/fit distinction; "a good test record does not make a claim fit"; "fit is not an independent test" and its could-have-failed condition; the operational clause of fit (changes explanation, prediction, search or modification); the dating of a test record; retention and repair as decisions not made by the test record. Unused: the explicit truth question and the Popper §84/§82 citations as such; the kinds of test (observation, derivation, source check) and the "does not presuppose the working theory" clause; "The two kinds of evidence inform each other"; the scope bullets on objectives, commitments and authority, and on statistical non-independence; the paragraph on retiring "warrant".
