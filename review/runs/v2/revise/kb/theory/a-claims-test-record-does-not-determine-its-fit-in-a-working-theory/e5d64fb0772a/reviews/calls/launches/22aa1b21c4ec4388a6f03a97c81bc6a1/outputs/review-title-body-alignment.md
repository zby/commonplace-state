# Review

## Summary

The body fully supports the title's claim. The section "A good test record does not make a claim fit" establishes it directly. The body is broader than the title, though. What the body establishes is this: truth, test record and fit are three separate standings of a claim. No answer about one settles another, and agreement with the working theory adds to the test record only when the claim could have failed it. The title covers one of three separations and one of two directions.

A more precise title would name the separation in both directions. For example: "A claim's test record and its fit in a working theory are separate standings". It could also add that neither substitutes for the other. The description already carries this broader scope, so the drift is between the H1 and the body, not between the description and the body.
