# Review

## Summary

Most load-bearing assertions are resolved by nearby context. The note defines fit through its components. It names the kinds of test for each kind of claim. It states the criterion that makes use in the theory count as a test. It describes repair after a contradiction as a decision that compares named factors.

The material ambiguity is the independence condition "does not presuppose the working theory". It decides which evidence counts as an independent test, and two reasonable readings of it produce different test records for the same claim.
