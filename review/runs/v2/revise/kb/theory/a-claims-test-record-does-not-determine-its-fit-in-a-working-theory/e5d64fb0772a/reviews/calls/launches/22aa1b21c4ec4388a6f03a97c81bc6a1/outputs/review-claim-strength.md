# Review

## Summary

The title "A claim's test record does not determine its fit in a working theory" is contestable. An informed reader could reasonably argue the opposite: a verificationist or a naive pragmatist could hold that a well-tested claim belongs in any theory whose domain it covers, so its test record decides retention. The note argues against that position. It shows that a well-tested claim can be irrelevant, redundant, mis-scoped or set at the wrong level of abstraction. The title is not a truism like `validation should be fast`. It states a non-obvious separation that changes how a knowledge base decides what to retain.
