# Review

## Summary

The note has six headed sections after a three-question opening, and each carries its own claim or a necessary boundary unit. Sections do not outnumber claims.

## Claims and sections

Distinct claims:

1. A test record is not truth: corroboration is dated, truth is not ("A test record is not truth").
2. A good test record does not make a claim fit; retention is a decision about what the theory is for ("A good test record does not make a claim fit").
3. Fit is not an independent test, except where the agreement could have gone against the claim ("Fit is not an independent test").
4. Fit evidence and test evidence inform each other in both directions but cannot substitute for each other ("The two kinds of evidence inform each other").
5. A contradiction leaves open which claim to give up; the choice weighs test records and fit ("A contradiction does not say which claim to give up").

The "Scope" section is a support unit carrying boundary conditions: fit has no single scale, objectives and authority lie outside testing, and the three questions are separate but not statistically independent. That last boundary qualifies the title claim directly and needs its own place.

Six sections against five claims plus one boundary unit. Each section adds a step the others do not make; claims 4 and 5 in particular go beyond restating the three-way separation. The first section is the closest to background, as recorded in the info finding.
