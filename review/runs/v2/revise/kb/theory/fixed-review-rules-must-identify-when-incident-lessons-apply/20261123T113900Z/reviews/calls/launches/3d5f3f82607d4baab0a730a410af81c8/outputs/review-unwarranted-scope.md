# Review

## Summary

The central claim has two parts. The title's "only if" is a derivation that holds over the whole class it names. The opening's "can remain the review rule" is a possibility claim, and one worked example is enough to show a possibility. The claimed scope therefore matches what the support exercises. The surplus risk is in the closing sentence, which readers may generalize beyond the single supposed incident without a stated transfer relation. That is a supporting claim, so it is INFO.
