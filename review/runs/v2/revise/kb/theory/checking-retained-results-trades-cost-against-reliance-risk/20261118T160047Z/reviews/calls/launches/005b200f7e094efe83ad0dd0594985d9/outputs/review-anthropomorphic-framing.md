# Review

The note makes no claims about model cognition. Its actors are consumers, validators, checkers and artifacts, and the verbs it gives them describe behaviour, not internals: a consumer "reads", "skips" or "stops"; the validator "performs the membership derivation" and "flags a gap". "A false mark tells exhaustive consumers to stop looking" and "the copy suppresses the search" are conventional metonymy for an artifact's contract and its effect on a consumer. The note immediately states that effect as a mechanism with conditions: the consumer actually stops, and the suppressed search would find more. "Calls for judgment" names the kind of assessment a task needs. It does not attribute a mental state to a model. Nothing meets the test.
