# Review

Most of the article's detail matches its argumentative role. The one-change walkthrough has three stages: admission, targeted narrowing and criterion revision. Each stage is later cashed in, as payoffs in the comparison table and as single-loop versus double-loop in the precedents section. Do not shorten it. Both the costs paragraph and the four-hazard list are fair concessions that give the bet its credibility. The PRODIGY and EURISKO paragraphs each carry exactly one distinction (targeted correction versus single-loop, and double-loop revision). The Harness Evolution parenthetical (the scorer saw a failure mode the proposer never received) is a non-obvious PASS. Without it, citing a rival study that defers to weight training would read as a dodge rather than a rebuttal.

The WARN is limited to one stray sentence in the on-balance paragraph. It adds a rule about when to investigate unknowns, and the article never uses that rule. The two INFO findings mark modest trims: one clause in the Gödel machine setup, and the terminological aside after the definition. These may still help readers who are new to the framing.
