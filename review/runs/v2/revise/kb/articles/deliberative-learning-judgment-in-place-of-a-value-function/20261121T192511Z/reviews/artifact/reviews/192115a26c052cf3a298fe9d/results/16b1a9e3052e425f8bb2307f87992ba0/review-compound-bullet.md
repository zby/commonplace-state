# Review

## Summary

Only a few list items run past about 30 words. They are the two definition conditions, the "Accretion and maintenance drag" hazard, and the three "Where to go next" entries. Each develops one point.

The first definition condition states where retained content lives and adds that the system learns by editing it. That is one idea. The second condition states the proposed–judged–rejectable decision, then describes the judgment and its limiting case. Its last sentence could stand alone, but it qualifies the same condition, so it is recorded as information only. The accretion hazard gives a mechanism (salient local gains, diffuse costs) and its consequence; that is one hazard with its explanation. The "Where to go next" entries each describe one destination or one group of companion drafts. The second entry pairs a related note with "paired with", which is a navigation pointer, not a second claim. The test conditions and the other hazards are short and single-pointed.
