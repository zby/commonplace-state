# Review

## Summary

**Claimed scope.** For retained derived results in general, check frequency depends on check cost relative to reliance risk. Accepting risk between checks can be right if the choice is explicit. A mechanical check requires four conditions.

**Presented support:**

- *Derivation from stated premises.* One production is amortized over many uses, and checking spends part of the saving. Source and copy have separate update paths. Absence of a known change implies freshness only with full change delivery.
- *An inherited conditional mechanism.* Suppression of retrieval, taken from the linked index note, which itself states the mechanism for any artifact treated as exhaustive.
- *Two worked cases.* The completeness mark and the connect report, grounded in their type contracts.

**Exercised domain.** The derivation's premises hold for any persisted result derived from a source that can change independently. That is the class the title names. The worked cases illustrate the conclusion but are not the only support.

**Surplus and transfer.** The claim does not go beyond what the derivation covers. Where it might overreach, the note marks its boundaries. The Scope section says the trade-off is qualitative and uncalibrated, makes no prevalence claim about stopping or fallback success, and treats the connect report's contract as no evidence of consumer behaviour. Open Questions leave checker adequacy and the frequency of silent omissions unresolved.

The invited reliance matches the support on the page.
