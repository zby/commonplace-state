# Review

## Summary

A one-paragraph rewrite would lose load-bearing content, so the multi-section structure is justified.

## Paragraph test

A one-paragraph version would keep the headline: when no fixed oracle decides theory fit, live system use is an initial selection environment. To fit in a paragraph, it would have to drop or flatten several separate elements:

- **Support path.** The test changes shape instead of disappearing: a portfolio of distributed consequences, none of which is a complete oracle, can discriminate better than static coherence.
- **Inference step.** Counterfactual effect is the selecting property. Retrieval without changed work, perturbation-sensitive mediation and survival against rivals form an ordered evidence gradation.
- **Failure condition.** Self-confirmation occurs when earlier design choices, neighboring artifacts or a narrow workload make a mistaken claim look indispensable. The note pairs this with countermeasures such as rivals, withholding, held-out demands and transfer.
- **Second failure condition.** Blame assignment: use exposes failure without identifying whether the theory, its application, a premise, the implementation or the evaluator caused it. This constrains the refinement path.
- **Boundary cases.** "Initial" excludes permanent human judgment and allows learned or weight-updating evaluators. The absence of an oracle is local to the research setting, and objectives and authority may stay external.

These elements are distinct and non-obvious. A single paragraph could not hold them without losing at least the failure conditions or the counterfactual gradation.
