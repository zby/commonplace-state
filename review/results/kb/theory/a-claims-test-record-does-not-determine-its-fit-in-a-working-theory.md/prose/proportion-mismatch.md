# Review

The title's claim is that a test record does not determine fit. The description adds the converse limit: fit counts as a test only where the claim could have failed. Each direction gets its own section. "A good test record does not make a claim fit" gives five concrete ways a well-tested claim can fit poorly. "Fit is not an independent test" gives the mechanism, the Popper §82 condition and the deciding criterion. Together they make up the largest share of the argument.

The secondary sections are shorter or comparable: the truth/test-record distinction, the interaction section, the contradiction section and the Scope bullets. None of them has grown at the expense of the core claim, and none looks like it should become a separate note.
