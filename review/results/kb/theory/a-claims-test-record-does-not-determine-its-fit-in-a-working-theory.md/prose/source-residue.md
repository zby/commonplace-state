# Review

The title and opening paragraph claim generality over any working theory, defined as "the set of claims a system currently uses to explain, predict, search and modify". The body's operational vocabulary stays within that system framing: "the current implementation", "local search or repair", "guide recovery" and "grants of authority". The note's own definition already includes a system that acts on its claims, so this vocabulary does not narrow the claim.

Popper's vocabulary ("corroborated", "sincere attempt to refute") appears only inside attributed citations. Elsewhere the note uses its own general terms, "test record" and "independent test". It also explicitly widens "test" beyond empirical observation to cover derivations and source checks. I found no leftover domain-specific examples or metaphors that are not framed as such.
