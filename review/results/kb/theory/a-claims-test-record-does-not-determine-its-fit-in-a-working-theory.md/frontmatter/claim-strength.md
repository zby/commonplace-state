# Review

## Summary

The title "A claim's test record does not determine its fit in a working theory" is contestable. An informed reader could reasonably argue the opposite: a common accumulative or confirmationist view holds that a theory should retain whatever claims have survived testing, so a good test record is enough reason to keep a claim. The note denies this. It argues that a well-tested claim can still be irrelevant, redundant, at the wrong scope or level, or inert, and that retention is a decision about what the theory is for. That is a non-obvious position, not a truism, so the title passes this gate.
