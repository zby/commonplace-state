# Review

Three bullets exceed about 30 words: "What is its test record?", "Does it fit?" and the third Scope bullet.

The test-record bullet holds several sentences: the definition, what makes a test independent, what kinds of test apply, and that the record carries a date. Each sentence specifies the term the bullet defines. None is a separate point that would read as its own item in the three-question list.

The fit bullet is one definition, followed after a colon by the conditions it comprises.

The third Scope bullet states that the three questions are separate but not statistically independent. Its second sentence gives the correlations that make them not independent. That is one point with its elaboration.

No bullet joins two independent claims with a dash, semicolon or "but" in a way that would bury the second one.
