# Review

## Summary

Only the first Scope bullet exceeds about 30 words. Its two sentences make one scoping point. The concession that a fixed deployment can give up self-revision is the premise for the clarification that follows: the note claims only that a production gain does not establish preservation, not that preservation is mandatory. Split apart, the second sentence would lose its referent ("it"), so the item is one point with elaboration rather than two separable claims.

The second bullet is under the length threshold, and its maintainer sentence illustrates the boundary-relativity claim rather than adding an independent one. The third bullet is a single claim.
