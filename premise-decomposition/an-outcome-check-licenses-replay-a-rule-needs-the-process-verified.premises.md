# Premise decomposition: A checked outcome licenses retaining an episode, not abstracting its explanation

**Note:** kb/notes/an-outcome-check-licenses-replay-a-rule-needs-the-process-verified.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** For an explanation claimed from one bounded episode, a result-only pass can justify retaining case-level evidence, but abstraction into an explanatory rule requires evidence that the proposed process faithfully produced the result and an explicit boundary for where that explanation transfers.

## Premises and counterexamples

1. **A single final-outcome observation is compatible with more than one producing process.** — HOLDS — Lucky guesses, spurious shortcuts, and cancelling errors remain concrete alternative routes to the same checked result; attempts to distinguish them required another observation, an intervention, or information about intermediate execution and therefore ceased to be a single result-only observation.
2. **An explanation claimed as an abstraction from an episode commits both that its proposed mechanism participated in producing the episode's result and that the mechanism remains relevant beyond that case.** — HOLDS — A valid explanation reconstructed after the fact can lack the first commitment, and an extensional mapping can lack both, but then its authority does not come from abstracting the episode's producing explanation; no counterexample preserved that claimed provenance while dropping either commitment.
3. **An outcome-only check tests neither the faithfulness of a proposed producing process nor the process's relevance in other cases.** — HOLDS — A final-state test can reliably reject wrong results, but observationally equivalent routes still pass it, and its one checked case supplies no comparison across transfer conditions; adding assertions about the route or other cases changes the check's target or evidence set.
4. **The checked result is correctly linked to the recorded episode strongly enough to support the case-level claim that this episode produced it.** — DOUBTFUL — A harness can accidentally grade a cached output from an earlier run while attaching the pass to the current trace. The outcome itself meets the target, yet the recorded episode did not produce that checked artifact. The note's weaker retention license therefore still depends on capture integrity or result provenance that it currently leaves implicit. — LOCAL — instance
5. **Evidence that does not connect an inspected process artifact to the route that actually produced the result cannot establish the episode's producing explanation.** — HOLDS — A coherent post-hoc rationale and an evaluator's independently reconstructed proof both provide concrete candidate counterexamples, but each confirms only that a valid route exists; neither shows that the episode used that route, which is the proposition at issue.
6. **A faithful execution trace or a controlled intervention can provide evidence about the process that actually produced the result.** — HOLDS — Logging can be incomplete and interventions can be confounded, but those cases make the evidence unfaithful or the intervention uncontrolled; no counterexample showed that evidence satisfying the stated faithfulness or control condition necessarily fails to bear on the producing process.
7. **Evidence of a valid and faithful process in one episode does not by itself state where that process will keep working.** — HOLDS — Even a causally faithful trace leaves open changes in inputs, environment, invariants, and mutable state. A proof or exhaustive characterization can close those questions only by supplying a domain and conditions, which supplies rather than eliminates the required boundary.
8. **Repeated, varied, or exhaustive outcome tests can warrant an extensional rule over a declared domain without thereby identifying the mechanism behind one success.** — HOLDS — Exhaustive testing over a finite equivalence-class domain establishes the mapping, but multiple internal implementations can realize that same mapping; the counterexample defeats a categorical ban on outcome-supported rules, not the note's narrower distinction between extensional coverage and an episode-derived explanation.
9. **The proposition a checker targets is independent of how reliably the checker discriminates that proposition.** — HOLDS — A deterministic end-to-end test can be a highly reliable outcome checker while leaving the producing route unresolved, and a process-directed evaluator can be unreliable while still targeting the route; changing reliability alone did not change either target.
10. **A prior outcome-only pass does not by itself establish that literal re-execution is safe or reproducible.** — HOLDS — Non-idempotent actions, such as charging the same account twice, and executions against mutated state give concrete cases where the original result was correct but replay misfires; every attempted rescue required an added reset, idempotence, or stability premise absent from the prior outcome check.

## For the human

Look first at premise 4: the case-level retention license needs an explicit capture-integrity or result-provenance condition so that “this produced the target result” cannot be satisfied by a checked artifact misattributed to the recorded episode.
