# Premise decomposition: Continual learning outside the weights

**Note:** `kb/articles/continual-learning-outside-the-weights.md`
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** For deployed systems whose experience arrives as describable events and whose needed concepts can be stated, continued learning can live outside model weights in a governed artifact layer interpreted by fixed weights.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **A system-level capacity change selected and retained in response to the system's own experience counts as continual learning even when no weights move.** — DEFEATED — A support system whose maintainer updates its playbook after each incident plainly becomes more capable from experience, but that case is still naturally classified as external maintenance of a fixed agent unless the system itself selects and admits the behavior-changing write; the article's alternative depends on replacing Sutton and Javed's weight-change criterion with a functional one. — GLOBAL — instance
2. **The article's exhibited case shows a deployed system, not merely a human-maintained wrapper, learning through artifact revision under fixed weights.** — DEFEATED — The project instance explicitly keeps a maintainer inside the boundary and the article says it does not show "a frozen interpreter forming a new concept on its own," so the worked example demonstrates human-agent composite maintenance rather than the claimed substrate operating autonomously. — GLOBAL — instance
3. **For a useful class of deployment shifts, the concepts and corrections the system needs are expressible as named natural-language or symbolic artifacts.** — HOLDS — The article scopes the claim to describable-event shifts and explicitly excludes proprioceptive, sensorimotor, and other unsayable feature-learning cases; within that boundary, policy changes, operational definitions, procedures, and code-level theories remain plausible targets.
4. **A frozen language-model interpreter can consume and apply retained artifacts reliably enough that revising the artifact layer produces durable downstream behavioral change.** — DOUBTFUL — If the interpreter systematically misreads a class of instruction such as exception clauses or fails to surface the right artifact at the moment of use, then the correction can be perfectly sayable and still fail to change behavior; the article itself treats interpreter misapplication and routing failure as live limits. — LOCAL — instance
5. **Governed artifact writes can keep the changing layer correct enough to serve as a learning substrate.** — DOUBTFUL — The article concedes that two artifacts can each pass their gates and still jointly contradict, that nothing selects which artifact to criticize after a failure, and that the project measures no drift, so governance here is only partial and may not preserve coherence as the layer grows. — LOCAL — prevalence
6. **Artifact-only updates can sometimes recover from environment shifts with less collateral change and less target experience than weight updates.** — DOUBTFUL — A deployment shift that looks describable at the surface can still be cheaper to absorb with a small adapter or fine-tune than with repeated artifact, routing, and review edits; the article presents the efficiency advantage as a prediction for a future comparison rather than as a shown result. — LOCAL — instance

## For the human
Look first at premise 2: the article's concrete example still keeps a maintainer inside the learning loop, so the worked case may show governed human maintenance around frozen weights rather than system-level continual learning outside the weights.
