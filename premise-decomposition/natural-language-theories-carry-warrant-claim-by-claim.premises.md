# Premise decomposition: Theory warrant should be tracked at the finest granularity evidence licenses

**Note:** kb/notes/natural-language-theories-carry-warrant-claim-by-claim.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** Evidence warrants the most specific truth-apt unit and scope it identifies, and support that identifies only a joint bundle must not be attributed to the bundle's components without additional evidence or an argument establishing their individual contributions.

## Premises and counterexamples

1. **Epistemic warrant can be represented as support for determinate truth-apt content under an interpretation over a determinate domain.** — HOLDS — A hunt among apparent exceptions such as instrumental models and whole-theory endorsements still required a truth-apt proposition about predictive adequacy, comparative superiority, or reliability and a domain over which reliance was licensed; the representation can absorb those propositions without treating an entire document as uniformly warranted.
2. **Evidence can identify warrant at different levels, including an individual claim, a conjunction, or an integrated model, so no one fixed theory unit is always the evidential bearer.** — HOLDS — A component-isolating intervention can support one mechanism claim, while an observational comparison between two integrated models can distinguish only their joint predictions; neither case supplies a universal fixed unit that defeats evidence-relative granularity.
3. **Merely placing claims in one document supplies no evidential relation that transfers warrant among them.** — HOLDS — A document can combine a measured regularity with a speculative mechanism and an extrapolated boundary; successful measurement of the regularity does not support the mechanism or boundary just because the prose presents them together.
4. **Whenever support warrants a conjunction only jointly, its component propositions receive no warrant unless further evidence or an argument identifies each component's individual contribution.** — DEFEATED — A valid proof of `A and B` warrants both `A` and `B` immediately by conjunction elimination even though it does not identify either component's contribution to the conjunction's success; the non-distribution rule is defensible for comparative support of an integrated model but false as stated for every warranted logical conjunction. — GLOBAL
5. **The evidence and an interpretation determine a sufficiently stable finest-grained content unit for bookkeeping.** — DOUBTFUL — Two readers can parse “A causes B in context C” into different causal, modal, or boundary commitments while the same evidence supports only one parsing, and non-identifiable models can admit several incomparable parameterizations of the same supported content; the note acknowledges interpretation dependence but gives no rule for recording or choosing among these units. — LOCAL
6. **Explicit premises, mechanisms, consequences, scopes, and falsifiers make a theory criticizable but do not themselves warrant its claims.** — HOLDS — A fabricated mechanism can expose precise dependencies, risky predictions, boundaries, and a falsifier while having no favorable evidence or sound derivation; explicit structure reveals what could test the account but does not perform the test.
7. **Support beyond directly exercised cases or failure modes requires a justified bridge from the evidence to the target scope.** — HOLDS — Representative sampling, coverage arguments, and invariant mechanisms looked like counterexamples because they warrant unobserved cases, but each is the very bridge the premise requires rather than support produced by the tested cases alone.
8. **After initial attribution, a theory's stated dependencies are sufficient to determine all downstream inheritance and loss of warrant relevant to this bookkeeping rule.** — DOUBTFUL — With probabilistically coupled claims, learning that one proposed premise is false can raise warrant for a rival claim through a shared latent cause even when neither claim is a stated derivational dependency of the other; dependency propagation remains useful, but it is not a complete account of evidential updating. — LOCAL
9. **Formalization improves consequence checking only for the chosen translation and cannot warrant natural-language content that the translation omitted or changed.** — HOLDS — A valid proof over a predicate standing for “causes” does not establish an omitted interventionist meaning or the empirical adequacy of that predicate; the proof warrants its formal consequence, while the translation and interpretation remain separate obligations.

## For the human

Look first at premise 4: distinguish comparative evidence for an integrated bundle from warrant for a logical conjunction, because conjunction elimination defeats the blanket requirement for component-specific attribution.
