# Premise decomposition: The framework is often larger than the durable contribution

**Note:** kb/notes/the-framework-is-often-larger-than-the-durable-contribution.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** When an artifact applies a familiar, reconstructable framework to a particular case, its durable contribution is usually the case-specific recognition anchor rather than a recap of the framework, so authors should default to minimal prose plus a framework link and retain more only when it supplies accessibility, disambiguation, warrant, fidelity, or behaviorally demonstrated value.

## Premises and counterexamples

1. **Across the intended consumer population, a framework that is familiar to the author is usually familiar and accessible enough to future consumers that they can reconstruct its relevant content from its name or link with adequate fidelity.** — DOUBTFUL — Model changes, heterogeneous consumer populations, and framework names with several live interpretations can make it ordinary for a consumer to know the generic framework without recovering the version or constraints the author used; the note acknowledges that reconstructability is observer-relative but supplies no prevalence evidence showing that these failures are exceptional in the population to which the default applies. — GLOBAL — prevalence
2. **When a familiar framework is applied to a particular case, the new information is usually the recognition condition, mapping, operation, relation, or local fact rather than the framework content itself.** — HOLDS — The strongest apparent counterexamples were applications that also derive a new framework mechanism, establish warrant, or fix an authoritative interpretation, but the note expressly requires those additions to remain and does not classify them as reconstructable framework recap; no prevalence-shaped case was found in which ordinary applications reverse the claimed distribution of new information.
3. **Agents tend to reproduce active framework content in artifacts even when that content adds no durable value.** — DOUBTFUL — Template-constrained authoring, strict length limits, retrieval-first notes, and editorial training can make omission of familiar background the ordinary behavior rather than leakage, and the note gives no comparison or prevalence evidence across authoring conditions. — LOCAL — prevalence
4. **A stable framework shared across many case notes can be factored into its own artifact and linked without losing the case-specific contribution.** — HOLDS — Context-sensitive interpretations and case-specific evidence initially look like counterexamples, but those are content the note requires the case artifact to retain for disambiguation, warrant, or fidelity; no counterexample was found to factoring the genuinely stable shared portion while keeping those local additions.
5. **A framework name, link, and recognition cue usually activate the intended reconstruction strongly enough that omitting the recap preserves the contribution's downstream effect.** — DOUBTFUL — Consumers that do not traverse links, retrieval policies that omit the target, ambiguous framework labels, and prompts whose stronger local instructions suppress the cue can all leave stored or parametric knowledge behaviorally inert; these look like potentially ordinary operating conditions, and the note proposes testing them but does not establish that the smaller form usually matches the fuller one. — GLOBAL — prevalence
6. **Authors can usually identify, before compression or through low-cost subsequent feedback, when framework material carries accessibility, disambiguation, warrant, or fidelity and therefore must remain.** — DOUBTFUL — A minimal note can produce the expected immediate action while silently losing the derivation an auditor later needs, and a passive or rarely exercised artifact may provide no feedback until after repeated reliance; the note names the values to preserve but does not show that authors usually detect their necessity soon enough for minimal-first retention to be safe. — GLOBAL — prevalence
7. **Adding omitted warrant or explanation after a demonstrated miss is cheap and targeted, whereas trimming excess framework material later is expensive.** — DEFEATED — In a regulated clinical decision artifact, omitting the rationale that establishes a recommendation's applicability can trigger review failure or unsafe reliance before correction, while deleting an unnecessary explanatory appendix is a bounded editorial change; the domain explicitly charges missing warrant more heavily than surplus explanation. — LOCAL — priced-exception
8. **A representative behavioral comparison of the name alone, the recognition anchor, and the fuller restatement can determine whether the smaller form preserves the contribution within the tested model, task, and consumer population.** — HOLDS — Rare untested tasks and future model changes do not defeat the premise at its stated validity-window scope, while accessibility, auditability, or fidelity requirements can be included among the representative behaviors being compared; no case was found in which genuinely representative tests agree while the smaller form nevertheless changes behavior inside that same window.

## For the human

Look first at premise 5: the minimal-plus-link default depends on cue-and-link activation usually preserving behavior, but the note currently offers a test for that prevalence claim rather than evidence that the intended consumer population satisfies it.
