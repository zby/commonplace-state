# Premise decomposition: Candidacy evidence licenses escalation to assessment, not acceptance

**Note:** `kb/notes/candidacy-evidence-licenses-escalation-not-acceptance.md`
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** In staged evaluation, evidence that licenses only escalation to assessment cannot, by that fact alone, license acceptance of the assessed conclusion.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **Support for "this candidate is worth assessing" is not, by itself, support for "the assessed conclusion is true."** — HOLDS — A concrete defeater would need a staged truth-assessment where the very same support remains candidacy-only yet still entails the truth-apt verdict; the note's own allowance that one item can satisfy both bars blocks the easy pseudo-counterexample where the evidence was verdict-grade all along.
2. **Warrant must stay at the finest granularity the evidence licenses, so warrant for pursuit-worthiness cannot silently transfer to the assessed conclusion.** — HOLDS — Joint or bundle-level warrant is not a counterexample here: if evidence really warrants the wider bundle that contains the verdict, then it is no longer merely candidacy evidence.
3. **The two worked witnesses instantiate one shared authority pattern rather than two different preliminary signals that merely happen to come before a later check.** — DOUBTFUL — The note's own `## Open Questions` concedes that domain pricing and thematic source fit may differ in the property that matters. Domain pricing is author-external evidence about exception status inside a settled practice; thematic source fit is a relevance heuristic for source nomination. They may share timing and cheapness without sharing one stable evidential role. — GLOBAL — instance
4. **The Pirolli case shows "correct nomination without verdict authority," not just that one bundled rerun produced stricter support judgments.** — DOUBTFUL — The case changed role isolation, prompts, and intermediate representation together. That supports "procedure affected the verdicts," but it does not isolate nomination without verdict authority as the cause of the tightening. — LOCAL — instance
5. **A workflow that lets routing-grade evidence decide acceptance would open an immunizing slot rather than an acceptable lower-stakes or differently scoped acceptance regime.** — DOUBTFUL — A concrete alternative is a workflow that records provisional acceptance for triage or local use while still requiring later reassessment for stronger commitments. That would collapse fewer distinctions than the note fears unless the note narrows "acceptance" to a stronger epistemic commitment. — GLOBAL — instance

## For the human
Look at premise 3 first: if the two witnesses do not share one authority property, the note's universal title outruns the evidence it currently has.
