**Central commitment (one sentence):** Inside a declared human-inclusive boundary that contains a judgment-capable interpreter, the right current record of theory building is a dated actor-by-function allocation, and Commonplace's traced evidence already supports a partial profile with computational criticism and candidate-revision proposal, human coherent modification, admission, world-checking, and demand choice, and other functions left untraced or only best-read.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **Scope-limited (taken as given): with a judgment-capable interpreter inside the boundary, some functions of theory building can move from human actors to computational actors even when full theory possession remains unsettled.** — HOLDS — The active hunt found no traced case in the frozen text that restores Naur's stronger human-only premise for these narrower functions; the repair episode is already a case of computational criticism and repair proposal.
2. **Universal (undeclared): for this question, a dated actor-by-function allocation is the right measurable object, whereas "does the system hold the theory?" is not here a question with a measurable answer.** — DEFEATED — The article itself later appeals to Naur-style reliability tests and pre-registerable occasions of coherent extension, which are concrete measurement shapes for a bearer-style yes/no question. The repair episode can already count as negative evidence on that alternative test, so the rival object is not unmeasurable; it is presently unresolved or negative. — GLOBAL — instance
3. **Universal (undeclared): the function list in the table is fine-grained enough to localize the reallocations that matter for this article's claim.** — DOUBTFUL — The tag-README pathway splits noticing strain, framing the objective, formulating a candidate, mechanically checking it, and judging its design shape. The published table compresses several of those into broader rows such as "evaluate a candidate revision" and "choose which demand to work on," so a real movement of one subfunction could occur while the profile stayed unchanged. — LOCAL — instance
4. **Universal (stated): a retained theory is operative only when some consumer gives it behavioral authority; routing and loading alone do not show use.** — HOLDS — The hunt found no defeater. A routed artifact can be present in context and still be causally inert.
5. **Scope-limited (stated): the repaired-note episode warrants classifying criticism of a theory claim against explicit criteria and counterexamples as computational.** — HOLDS — In both cycles the pass found a genuine overreach and supplied the criticism without a human providing that critical judgment.
6. **Scope-limited (stated): the same episode warrants classifying candidate revision proposal as computational.** — HOLDS — Each cycle produced a candidate repair from the machinery before the maintainer intervened.
7. **Scope-limited (stated): the same episode warrants classifying coherent modification within the note's intended structure as human.** — HOLDS — In both cycles the generated repair retreated to a true but purposeless claim, and one maintainer sentence restored the intended thesis. The hunt found no traced case in this article where the machinery preserved purpose by itself.
8. **Scope-limited (best reading): candidate-revision evaluation can be summarized as "joint" at this snapshot.** — DOUBTFUL — The tag-README trace is genuinely split between mechanical checking and human design judgment, but the repaired-note episode has a different shape: the computational pass accepted its own narrowed repair and the maintainer later rejected it for losing the note's point. One row labeled "joint" blurs those path-specific evaluation shapes. — LOCAL — instance
9. **Scope-limited (stated): application of the theory to a case, and the deciding part of derivation, are correctly left untraced or only partially traced rather than claimed as computational.** — HOLDS — The article explicitly withholds those stronger allocations because routed-note presence and artifact lineage do not yet show which component supplied the deciding judgment, and the hunt found no counterexample to that caution.
10. **Universal (stated): stronger formalization moves the open question but does not remove it, because proof establishes consequences under stated assumptions and does not itself show that those assumptions fit the world.** — HOLDS — The active hunt found no defeater. Verification can close derivation inside a model while leaving theory-to-world correspondence open.
11. **Scope-limited (best reading): checking a formal model against the world stayed human at this boundary.** — DOUBTFUL — The article offers this as a current best reading rather than a traced row, and executable probes, telemetry comparisons, or model-directed tests can shoulder part of correspondence checking. That does not refute the row everywhere, but it makes the present placement provisional rather than directly located by the cited episodes. — LOCAL — instance
12. **Scope-limited (stated): choosing which demand to work on stayed human in every traced case.** — HOLDS — The article scopes this row to the traced episodes and explicitly refuses to generalize it into a disposition; within that scope, the hunt found no counterexample.

## For the human
Look first at the "allocation is the right measurable object" premise: the article's own later appeal to Naur-style tests gives the bearer question a measurement shape, so the central claim should likely narrow from exclusivity to "the best current record here is an allocation profile."
