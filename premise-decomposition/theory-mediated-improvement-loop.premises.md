# Premise decomposition: A provisional theory-mediated improvement loop

**Note:** kb/work/explanatory-theories-deployment-time-learning/theory-mediated-improvement-loop.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** An explicit, prospectively specified system theory can serve as a shared intermediate between a system-change loop and a separately governed theory-revision loop, while episode-local and retained-theory treatments support different causal claims about immediate reasoning and cumulative theory learning.

## Premises and counterexamples

1. **There exist deployment-time improvement episodes in which one explicit object-level system theory causally contributes at more than one point in proposal formation or judgment, so theory can be a shared intermediate rather than merely an evaluation prior.** — HOLDS — Direct fixes and harmful false theories show that explicit theory is neither necessary nor uniformly beneficial, but they do not defeat this existential premise; a scoped dependency account can both locate an intervention and predict which checks discriminate its expected mechanism.
2. **The stated minimum contract—pre-outcome fixation plus mechanisms or invariants, assumptions, scope, and falsifiable held-out consequences—is sufficient to distinguish a theory treatment from a plan, prediction cache, or candidate-ranking policy.** — DOUBTFUL — A candidate-conditioned prediction table can be fixed in advance, label its stable input-output mapping an invariant, state assumptions and scope, and make held-out falsifiable predictions while remaining exactly the prediction-cache control the note says is not a theory; the treatment boundary needs a further discriminator such as a required derivational relation among mechanism, intervention, and consequence. — LOCAL — instance
3. **Separately representing deployed system state as `S_n` and retained theory as `T_n` is coherent even though operative behavior can depend on both, and a stored `T_n` that is neither retrieved nor otherwise consumed has no behavioral effect as retained theory.** — HOLDS — A theory may be compiled into a policy, evaluator, or other part of `S_n`, but the resulting effect travels through that derivative state; it is not a counterexample in which the unused retained record itself affects behavior.
4. **Whenever a prospectively recorded theory makes outcome-relevant consequences that differ from relevant rivals, candidate-trial evidence can bear separately on the candidate and on the theory.** — HOLDS — A trial predicted equally by every rival or selected only from the theory's favored surface would not discriminate, but those cases violate the stated conditions rather than refute this conditional premise.
5. **Candidate acceptance and theory revision are distinct decisions because a candidate can work under a false explanation and a failed candidate can still supply evidence that warrants revising a theory.** — HOLDS — A proof-carrying or exhaustively modeled candidate may let one body of evidence support both decisions, but it does not make the two targets identical or make their outcomes coincide in every episode.
6. **Whenever a theory claims reuse beyond one candidate or independent revision, the candidate's acceptance evidence does not automatically warrant that theory's claimed content and scope, so the theory needs its own assessment and retention decision.** — HOLDS — An implementation may execute both decisions atomically, but a shared transaction is not a counterexample to separate warrant: behavioral evidence for the candidate can still leave the reusable explanation underdetermined.
7. **Current-task outcome improvement and sampled historical retention behavior, without process evidence or a rival-discriminating identification route, do not establish the broader explanatory reach of a rationale.** — HOLDS — Exhaustive coverage of a closed domain or a causal or proof argument could support a broader claim, but those add an evidential route excluded by this premise; the checked outcomes alone remain compatible with rival explanations.
8. **Object-level system theories and improvement-process theories make different warrant-bearing claims, so correctness or usefulness established for one does not by itself warrant the other.** — HOLDS — One document can contain both kinds of claim and a theory of the improvement process can also describe part of the deployed system, but neither overlap supplies the missing entailment from a promotion rule to an object mechanism or from an object mechanism to a worthwhile retention policy.
9. **With the objective and prediction boundary treated as episode inputs, diagnosis and search, candidate generation and prioritization, evidence selection, post-evidence decision, and evidence interpretation or theory revision cover the object theory's proposal-formation and judgment roles through acceptance.** — HOLDS — A theory can also help revise the objective, prediction boundary, or post-acceptance activation policy, but the first two change inputs fixed by this premise and the last is explicitly assigned to the distinct operative-retention control surface.
10. **Success or failure in any one of the five object-theory roles does not entail the same result in the others.** — HOLDS — The hunt produces ordinary separating cases: a correct fault localization can accompany a bad regression projection, and a discriminating evaluation can be selected even when no useful candidate is generated.
11. **For an explicit theory artifact to have mediated candidate search or evidence selection, the relevant artifact or projection must be fixed before the choice or outcome it is claimed to influence.** — HOLDS — A later rationale may faithfully describe latent reasoning that occurred earlier, but the later artifact could not have caused the earlier action; that is a different treatment from mediation by the explicit artifact.
12. **For at least some experimental tasks, prospective access variation together with compute-, structure-, and content-matched controls can distinguish an immediate effect of theory content from the effects of extra inference or generic deliberation.** — HOLDS — In some tasks mechanisms and recommendations cannot be varied independently without introducing incoherence, but that does not defeat this existential premise; synthetic or otherwise independently modeled tasks can support recommendation-preserving mechanism corruption and matched non-explanatory controls.
13. **An on-the-spot treatment that observes only the current episode and discards `tau_n` cannot establish accumulation of an addressable, separately revisable theory or that such retained theory improves a later episode.** — HOLDS — An ephemeral theory can produce a durable evaluator or harness change that later helps, but that is the separately acknowledged deployment-time-learning path through `S_{n+1}`; without retaining `T_n` and measuring a later episode it does not establish the retained-theory claim stated here.
14. **A retained-theory treatment can affect later behavior only when later work retrieves and consumes `T_n`, and its proposed revision must be rejectable independently of the contemporaneous system candidate.** — HOLDS — Mere storage is causally inert, while unconditional overwrite supplies no theory-selection decision; no case was found that preserves the claimed retained-theory loop while dropping either condition.
15. **A provenance record of retrieval and consumption establishes uptake but does not establish that the retained artifact caused a later improvement; that causal claim requires an intervention or an equivalent identification strategy.** — HOLDS — Logged access is compatible with unchanged behavior, whereas withholding, corrupting, or otherwise identifying the artifact's contribution distinguishes use from causal benefit.
16. **Using one LLM for theory formation, candidate generation, and evaluation can create correlated self-confirming error, and the listed held-out, fixed-evaluation, omitted-region, rival-theory, or role-separation controls can expose some such errors without serving as complete theory oracles.** — HOLDS — Shared blind spots can survive every listed control, but completeness is expressly denied; a held-out rival-theory case on which the shared account predicts wrongly is a concrete case those controls can expose.
