# Premise decomposition: Representational form does not determine whether a theory is a prototype

**Note:** kb/notes/a-natural-language-theory-is-a-prototype-codified-or-rejected.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** A theory has prototype standing while its commitments remain cheap to revise or reject because consumers are loosely coupled to their current version, and natural-language rather than symbolic representation does not itself determine that standing.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **Within the note's stated engineering gloss, prototype standing is a lifecycle property of cheap revision or rejection, not an epistemic status conferred by conjecture or acceptance.** — HOLDS — An accepted but unintegrated account can remain cheap to change, while an unaccepted scheduling rule can already govern production; the attempted cross-cases separate epistemic status from the stipulated lifecycle property rather than defeating it.
2. **Neither natural-language nor symbolic form fixes how tightly operational consumers bind to a theory's current commitments.** — HOLDS — An approved prose safety case can control training and certification, while a mechanized toy model can have no consumer beyond its exploratory checker; the counterexample hunt therefore found both high-binding prose and low-binding symbolic cases.
3. **Codification assigns formal consequences to the choices actually encoded but does not by itself adopt the artifact or create a downstream operational dependency.** — HOLDS — A policy language file becomes operative when a loader, repository contract, or deployment step consumes it; attempts to make formal syntax alone sufficient all smuggled in such an adoption mechanism.
4. **How far a revision propagates into already dependent machinery is determined by the semantic commitments to which consumers bind, not by interpretive openness or formal determinacy alone.** — HOLDS — A compatible revision can remain local in a formal artifact, and a changed reading of authoritative prose can force coordinated updates; apparent form-driven cases depended on what the consumers treated as stable.
5. **Loose downstream operational coupling is sufficient for a theory's commitments to remain cheap to revise as well as cheap to reject.** — DOUBTFUL — A large exploratory proof development can have no downstream operational consumer yet a small change to a foundational definition can invalidate thousands of internal lemmas and make revision expensive; the note does not say whether those internal artifact dependencies count as consumer binding or as a separate form-sensitive construction cost. — LOCAL — instance
6. **When consumers genuinely bind at different component grains, prototype standing can differ by component rather than attaching indivisibly to the whole theory.** — HOLDS — A checked invariant can be operative while adjacent explanatory premises remain unused; cases in which changing those premises also changes the invariant merely show that the actual binding grain was wider than first described.
7. **Codification and epistemic acceptance are independent: formalization can precede acceptance, and acceptance for a use can occur without a formal consumer.** — HOLDS — Candidate theorem encodings are routinely explored before anyone relies on them, while qualitative theories can be accepted for explanatory use without executable formalization; neither direction supplied an entailment for a counterexample to break.

## For the human
Look first at premise 5: clarify whether intrinsic proof or model-maintenance dependencies count as operational binding, or narrow the cheapness claim to downstream propagation and rollback cost.
