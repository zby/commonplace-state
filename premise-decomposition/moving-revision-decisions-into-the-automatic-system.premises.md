# Premise decomposition: Moving revision decisions into the automatic system

**Note:** kb/articles/moving-revision-decisions-into-the-automatic-system.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** Moving a revision decision from a maintainer into machinery without losing governance and while keeping the path open to later revision requires six represented conditions, and Commonplace's six audited paths show broad automation around adoption but leave generic admission and model-realization-dependent decisions unable to move because their inputs are not represented.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **Conditional universal: every governed, inspectable, repeatable moved decision requires complete addressability of every repository-defined artifact and relation through which behavioral authority or revision governance is exercised.** — DEFEATED — A validator revision can have a represented target, adequate tests, authorized admission, operative installation, later dependence, and a surviving path for another validator revision while an unrelated publication or model-selection authority path remains opaque. The cited repeatable-path account explicitly distinguishes this target-local continuity from system-wide complete addressability. — GLOBAL — instance
2. **Bounded universal: Commonplace has no representation of realized model identity that machinery can use for a model-dependent comparability or freshness decision.** — DEFEATED — Retained child `turn_context` traces record the realized Sol executions and exposed the false Luna provenance; the current regression reference calls `turn_context.payload.model` the concrete execution record to compare with persisted `runner_model`. Machinery can read the two records and reject or abstain on a mismatch. What is missing is a trusted, routinely consumed requested-to-realized binding or execution attestation, not representation of the input at all. — GLOBAL — instance
3. **Bounded universal: the cited Commonplace episodes establish that noticing, drafting, and checking moved to machinery even though adoption stayed with a maintainer.** — DOUBTFUL — The retained cases establish changed artifacts, agent participation, and some executable checking, but they do not record the decision supplier for each surrounding function. The same ADR and commit traces are compatible with a maintainer noticing the problem and supplying the consequential drafting or checking choices while an agent implements them. — GLOBAL — instance
4. **Implicit exhaustive universal: the six named requirements cover every representation needed to individuate and govern a repeatable revision path.** — DOUBTFUL — The article's own trait-scoped gate and type-and-trigger-scoped validator cases show that consumer, channel, and force can remain fixed while the governed cohort or activation condition changes. Because the current authority-path representation omits that applicability distinction, all six headings can appear populated while the path being governed is still misidentified; the article acknowledges the gap but does not clearly place it inside one of the six requirements. — GLOBAL — instance
5. **Conditional universal: a governed machine-held revision decision needs a represented target boundary, behavioral-authority path, authorization route, and purpose.** — HOLDS — A hard-coded updater or capability-scoped service initially looks like a counterexample, but its target scope, write capability, consumer, and acceptance predicate are symbolic representations of the same functions; removing them leaves the machine unable to determine what it may change or what counts as better.
6. **Conditional universal: a governed, inspectable, repeatable path needs an applicable retained revision method, although that method may route to open-ended theoretical reasoning rather than settle the answer.** — HOLDS — An unattended agent that improvises an improvement without retained routing can still supply the decision computationally, but it is the ungoverned case the commitment explicitly excludes. A general search-and-evaluation procedure or an explicit route to open-ended reasoning is already an applicable method at the level claimed.
7. **Conditional universal: substantive warrant and admission must both be represented, because reasons to prefer a successor do not by themselves authorize it and authorization does not make it preferable.** — HOLDS — A proof, test result, or policy decision can serve both functions in one episode, but separating the functions still changes what its failure means: a Goodharted score may authorize an unwarranted successor, while a well-supported proposal may remain unauthorized.
8. **Conditional universal: a governed revision path needs operative installation through a known behavioral-authority channel.** — HOLDS — A machine may make the selection while a person or fixed deployment job performs the mechanical installation, but that does not defeat the premise: the requirement is that the installation path be represented and operative, not that machinery execute every action. A selected successor that never reaches behavior falls outside the article's explicitly operative claim.
9. **Conditional universal: a path advertised as open to the next revision needs continuity of determination, admission, installation, and later use across successors.** — HOLDS — A one-shot optimizer can make and install a revision that disables itself, but that case concedes rather than defeats the stated condition because it is not open to a later revision. Merely retaining the successor is insufficient, and the article's narrower repeatable-path test preserves the stronger reading.
10. **Bounded universal: Commonplace lacks a canonical generic record of maintainer designation, grant scope and duration, generic admission conditions, and the binding from an approval to the installed content version.** — HOLDS — The counterexample hunt found path-specific approval instructions, role names, and Git history, but no repository-owned `CODEOWNERS`, `OWNERS`, `MAINTAINERS`, branch-policy, or equivalent artifact that supplies the complete generic grant and exact approval-to-content binding.
11. **Bounded existential: the six paths establish broad, path-relative addressability and several operative reuse cases without establishing repository-wide completeness.** — HOLDS — The goal, criterion, validator, lifecycle, requested-model, and admission paths span distinct natural-language and symbolic authority surfaces, while the audit expressly withholds an exhaustive-coverage inference; an opaque unexamined path would not defeat this bounded breadth claim.
12. **Bounded universal: model-dependent comparability and freshness decisions require evidence of the realized model rather than only a requested model, alias, or partition label.** — HOLDS — The 268 results persisted as Luna despite Sol execution are a concrete case in which requested identity would admit invalid model-specific evidence; no counterexample was found in which a knowingly false realized identity remained adequate for those model-partitioned decisions.
13. **Bounded universal: routine invocation of the semantic gate establishes that application of the revised explanatory-reach criterion moved to machinery.** — DEFEATED — The revised criterion requires observed fit, while the article concedes that the invoked gate omits observed fit and that no retained review shows later dependence on all four revised parts. Machinery routinely applies a partial criterion, not the revised criterion as a whole. — LOCAL — instance
14. **Bounded universal: every adoption decision in the cited Commonplace cases was supplied by a maintainer.** — DOUBTFUL — The article reports maintainer adoption, but the same audit says no retained generic admission record binds an approving actor or event to the installed versions. That absence does not show a computational adoption occurred, but it prevents the artifact record from independently establishing the universal actor-allocation claim. — LOCAL — instance

## For the human
Look first at the model-realization premise: the realized model is represented in retained `turn_context` traces, so the article should name the missing trusted binding and operative consumer rather than say that the decision input is not represented at all.
