# Premise decomposition: Opposed recompute factors do not decide documentation segmentation

**Note:** kb/notes/human-recompute-is-dear-and-rare-agent-recompute-is-cheap-and-constant.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** For documentation that is only a cache over re-readable source, positive crossed rankings of audiences' net saving per equivalent reconstruction and reconstructions avoided do not by themselves order cache value, and even exact audience-specific cache values do not decide segmentation, which additionally requires comparing marginal specialization value with incremental maintenance and drift burden.

## Premises and counterexamples

1. **Within the stated scope, the document materializes an answer that each audience could instead reconstruct to the same reliability threshold from authoritative, re-readable source.** — HOLDS — A public API contract that overrides disagreeing implementation source was the strongest attempted counterexample, but such a contract records a binding fact that the source cannot regenerate and is expressly outside the note's cache-only scope.
2. **As an explicitly approximate decision aid, avoided-reconstruction benefit over a fixed decision horizon can be represented by the savings on equivalent reconstruction opportunities and summarized as an opportunity-weighted net saving multiplied by the number avoided.** — HOLDS — Heterogeneous tasks and correlation between task difficulty and document use break a product using an unweighted typical cost, but they do not break the stated model when the per-reconstruction term is the opportunity-weighted average at a fixed output and reliability threshold; non-cache benefits are conceded as outside this model's narrower use.
3. **The target regime has positive net savings and positive avoided-reconstruction counts, with each audience's values expressed on a defensible common scale.** — HOLDS — A document that costs an audience more than source reconstruction, or audiences whose resources have no defensible exchange rate, falls outside the expressly conditional regime; the note routes the latter case to incomparability instead of deriving an ordering.
4. **For positive factors, the facts that one audience has the larger per-reconstruction saving and the other has the larger avoided-reconstruction count do not determine which product is larger.** — HOLDS — Products of `(10, 2)` and `(5, 3)` put the first audience ahead, while `(6, 2)` and `(5, 4)` put the second ahead; an ordering always required magnitude information absent from the two directional inequalities.
5. **The crossed factor directions contain no audience-specific maintenance magnitudes, so subtracting maintenance cannot yield an ordering entailed by those directions alone.** — HOLDS — Equal maintenance or a known maintenance gap can preserve, reverse, or create an ordering in a concrete project, but each supplies additional information rather than extracting an ordering from the crossed directions.
6. **Agent statelessness by itself fixes neither relevant fresh-session volume nor net saving per reconstruction, while fast source access by itself fixes neither consultation, transformation, verification, recovery, nor recurrence costs.** — HOLDS — A stateless agent used for only one relevant session has no high recurrence, while a fast searcher facing expensive completeness verification can still obtain a large net saving from documentation; each property therefore leaves the opposing factor and both magnitudes open.
7. **Audience cache value alone does not determine the marginal audience-fit value of a separate content layer relative to the best shared content.** — HOLDS — A human and an agent can derive sharply different savings from the same exact command reference because their recurrence differs, while two audiences with equal total savings can still need a tutorial and a machine-readable schema respectively; neither equality nor inequality of the cache-value scalars determines the fit delta.
8. **A separately maintained audience-specific content layer carries a distinct incremental correctness and drift obligation, possibly with zero realized cost over a stable horizon, whereas consumer-specific views generated from one maintained source do not create that second content surface.** — HOLDS — A frozen source can make realized drift cost zero and shared tooling can reduce it, but neither turns separately authored claims into the one-source generated-view case; the incremental term may be zero without ceasing to be separate from gross audience cache value.
9. **Every in-scope content-segmentation decision is a discretionary value trade that requires specialization benefit to exceed incremental maintenance and drift; no independent constraint can require separate content layers.** — DEFEATED — A safety-regulated project can be contractually required to maintain a separately approved operator manual and engineering reference even when both documents cache source-reconstructible behavior and the measured audience-fit benefit of the second layer does not repay its upkeep. Certification imposes separation as a hard constraint rather than as a scalar benefit to trade against maintenance, so the commitment needs a discretionary-decision condition or a prior hard-constraint branch. — GLOBAL — instance

## For the human

Look first at premise 9: the segmentation rule omits legal, safety, access, or contractual constraints that can require separate content layers before audience-fit benefit is priced.
