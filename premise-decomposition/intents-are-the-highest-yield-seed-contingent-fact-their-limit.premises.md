# Premise decomposition: A specific intent may out-yield local rationales, but contingent facts stay separate

**Note:** kb/notes/intents-are-the-highest-yield-seed-contingent-fact-their-limit.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** Inside the design-theory residue an interpreter cannot otherwise recover, the note conjectures that a specific unrecoverable governing intent will usually let an interpreter reconstruct more held-out local rationale per retained token than an equal-size bundle of local rationale snippets, while contingent facts not entailed by that intent must still be retained separately.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **Read as a statistical conjecture over representative design artifacts, local implementation judgments are usually more strongly constrained by a specific governing intent than by a same-token bundle of local rationale snippets.** — DOUBTFUL — A prevalence-shaped defeater is mature software whose held-out local justifications are commonly dominated by compatibility obligations, incident history, migration scars, performance constraints, upstream quirks, or regulatory details; if those are ordinary rather than exceptional in the target population, intent plus implementation will not reconstruct more held-out local rationale. — GLOBAL — prevalence
2. **Read as a statistical comparator premise, local rationale snippets usually have lower transfer yield because each explains fewer choices than a governing intent.** — DOUBTFUL — Local rationales often cite a shared invariant or purpose, such as "this branch keeps tenant isolation in the database rather than caller discipline"; a few such snippets can expose the same governing structure as the intent treatment and may match or beat it unless the comparator excludes goal-bearing or invariant-bearing snippets. — LOCAL — prevalence
3. **Read as an experimental-design premise, "intent" and "local rationale snippets" are separable input treatments under an equal token budget.** — DOUBTFUL — A concrete local rationale can contain the intent itself: "we denormalize this table to make offline invoice reconciliation fast for field agents." In that case the local-rationale-fed agent is partly intent-fed, so the comparison no longer isolates intent from rationale. — LOCAL — instance
4. **Read as a universal boundary premise, retained design theory should prioritize only what the interpreter cannot regenerate from the artifact and available knowledge.** — DOUBTFUL — A recoverable rationale may still need retention when regeneration is too costly, too unreliable for bounded agents, or needed as an index into a large artifact; that dents the retention boundary without defeating the narrower yield conjecture. — LOCAL — instance
5. **Read as a statistical measurement premise, useful local judgments reconstructed per retained token is stable enough to compare intent against local rationales.** — DOUBTFUL — Different selections and encodings can change the result: an oracle-selected rationale bundle, compressed decision table, or rationale that names a cross-cutting invariant may produce more useful judgments per token than a prose intent, while a noisy rationale sample may not. — LOCAL — instance
6. **Read under the note's stated preconditions, specificity and non-recoverability are enough to make retained intent informative rather than redundant.** — HOLDS — The counterexample hunt found only cases the note already prices: vague intent, already obvious intent, and intent weaker than compact constraints or decision records are outside the claim's stated preconditions or outside the local-rationale comparison.
7. **Read as a universal contingent-fact boundary, a selected alternative's binding status and decision basis are not recovered from governing purpose alone when the artifact, history, standards, and available context do not carry them.** — HOLDS — The artifact may reveal what was implemented, but the hunt found no case where purpose alone identifies the rejected alternatives, why the selection remains binding, or whether the choice was incidental when those facts are absent elsewhere.
8. **Read as a universal contingent-fact boundary, project-specific world-side forces and encountered observations are not entailed by purpose alone when no artifact, test, type boundary, log, standard, or available context carries them.** — HOLDS — Cases where an API schema, validation test, incident log, or external regulation reveals the force are excluded by the premise; once those carriers are absent, the purpose does not supply the observed fact.
9. **Read as a universal contingent-fact boundary, applicability limits not included in the governing intent do not follow from an unscoped intent.** — HOLDS — A scoped intent can include its own domain or done-conditions, but that is not a counterexample to the stated premise; the hunt found no way for an unrecorded boundary to follow from an unscoped purpose by itself.
10. **Read as a universal contingent-fact boundary, arbitrary conventions among equally purpose-serving alternatives cannot be reconstructed from purpose or general knowledge.** — HOLDS — The hunt found no defeater for the narrowed case: if several values genuinely serve the purpose equally and no external standard selects one, the intent does not identify the chosen convention.
11. **Read as a universal separation premise, retaining more intent does not recover facts never entailed by the purpose, and retaining more facts does not necessarily reconstruct rationale elsewhere.** — HOLDS — Counterexamples either make the "fact" part of a richer intent, make it available through artifact or context, or make the retained fact a shared invariant rather than a merely contingent fact; none defeats the separated categories as the note defines them.

## For the human
Look first at premise 1: the conjecture needs the representative-artifact population to be one where specific intent ordinarily governs held-out local rationale more than compatibility, history, constraints, and other contingent design residue do.
