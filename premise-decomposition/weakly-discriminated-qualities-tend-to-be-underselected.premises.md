# Premise decomposition: Weakly discriminated qualities tend to be underselected

**Note:** kb/notes/weakly-discriminated-qualities-tend-to-be-underselected.md
**Register:** claim
**Claim mode:** statistical
**Discovery-lifecycle stage:** conjecture
**Central commitment (one sentence):** The note conjectures that, across representative actual proposal-selection loops in which candidates vary on multiple qualities and the operative acceptance process discriminates some qualities more reliably than others strongly enough to affect survival after weights, hard constraints, and correlations, weakly discriminated qualities usually receive less acceptance enrichment than strongly discriminated qualities.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **Under a fixed candidate distribution and acceptance rule, a directionally aligned survival differential toward better instances of a quality raises that quality's expected value among accepted candidates, and a larger differential produces greater enrichment all else equal.** — HOLDS — The hunt considered an accurate score that acceptance ignores, an inverted score, and a score whose errors matter only outside the acceptance boundary. The first is not operative, the second is not directionally aligned, and the third does not establish a larger acceptance-relevant survival differential; none defeats the premise as stated.
2. **Every loop counted in the tendency contains usable candidate variation in both the weakly and strongly discriminated qualities within the actual task frame.** — HOLDS — Abstract generator capacity without activation was the strongest candidate defeater, but the note expressly requires usable variation in the generated candidates. A loop whose current candidates lack the quality lies outside the stated condition rather than counterexampleing this premise.
3. **Every discrimination difference counted in the tendency belongs to the operative acceptance process: it favors better over worse instances and changes survival after scoring weights, hard constraints, and quality correlations are considered.** — HOLDS — A nominal rubric item, an ignored evaluator score, or a reliable but negatively aligned signal would not supply the predicted pressure, but the note excludes each by defining the oracle through actual survival and requiring directionally aligned discrimination.
4. **“More reliably separates” orders the checks by discrimination where the acceptance decision is made, on a basis that is relevant to accepted-set enrichment, rather than merely by an aggregate ranking or accuracy statistic.** — DOUBTFUL — On one fixed candidate set, check A can rank almost every pair correctly yet misrank the few candidates straddling the acceptance cutoff, while check B ranks many irrelevant pairs incorrectly but orders the cutoff candidates correctly; A is more reliable by aggregate pairwise accuracy, but B produces the greater accepted-set enrichment. The note's survival qualification points toward decision-relevant discrimination but does not specify the comparison closely enough to rule out this reading. — LOCAL — instance
5. **Across representative loops satisfying the stated conditions, differences in quality headroom, base rate, and scale, together with residual effects of weights, constraints, and covariance, are not prevalent enough to make weakly discriminated qualities receive equal or greater acceptance enrichment as often as the discrimination asymmetry makes them receive less.** — DOUBTFUL — A plausible ordinary class pairs a mature, strongly checked quality already near its ceiling with a variable soft quality: rejecting the rare hard-quality defect can discriminate nearly perfectly yet add little expected quality, while a weakly informative soft-quality check can add more because it has much more selectable headroom. The note makes the tendency refutable by prevalence and acknowledges that population-level dominance remains conjectural, but it provides no prevalence evidence that this common-looking class and related countervailing structures are exceptional among qualifying loops. — GLOBAL — prevalence
6. **Less acceptance enrichment is sufficient for underselection even when the weakly discriminated quality stays flat or improves, so the commitment does not require drift or absolute degradation.** — HOLDS — A weak but above-chance oracle can repeatedly produce positive improvement while trailing a stronger oracle. The note explicitly includes that trajectory in “less enrichment” and separately requires an adverse directional mechanism for absolute decline, so it is not a counterexample to this premise.

## For the human
Look first at premise 5: the statistical claim states a prevalence-shaped refuter without forcing a numeric rate, but the conjecture still depends on countervailing headroom, scale, weighting, constraint, and covariance structures not being ordinary enough to reverse the proposed tendency.
