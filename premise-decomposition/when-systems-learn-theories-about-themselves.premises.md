# Premise decomposition: When systems learn theories about themselves

**Note:** kb/articles/when-systems-learn-theories-about-themselves.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** Theory-mediated, reflective, and self-improving are independent properties whose causally coupled intersection requires an interpreted, retained, separably revisable self-theory and independent evaluation, while the systems examined here realize only parts of that loop and do not show recurrent compounding.

## Premises and counterexamples
1. **Universal (undeclared): revising a premise of a retained theory changes every case derived from that premise.** — DEFEATED — A system can materialize consequences as prompts, skills, procedures, validators, or code and then retain them independently of the theory from which they came; revising the theory does not change those live derivatives unless the system tracks the dependency and re-derives, invalidates, or separately edits them. Commonplace itself has this shape: changing a theory note does not automatically update instructions or validators that were derived from it. The propagation claim therefore needs a fresh-derivation or realization condition. — LOCAL — instance
2. **Universal (undeclared): membership, interpretation, and retention with separable parts are sufficient minimum conditions for the causally coupled three-way case.** — DEFEATED — A composite can retain and interpret a causally connected self-theory for diagnosis while a separate benchmark-driven rule patcher changes its prompts; all three named conditions hold somewhere inside the boundary, yet the theory neither determines those changes nor gets revised by their results. A fourth obligation must connect the same path in both directions: self-theory to update determination, and outcome evidence back to self-theory. — LOCAL — instance
3. **Universal (undeclared): theory-mediated, reflective, and self-improving are independent properties.** — HOLDS — The strongest attempted collapses fail under the article's path-relative definitions: a market theory can mediate a change to an agent without representing the agent, a reflective prompt editor can patch by score without retaining a derivational theory, and the Homeostat can change its organization without a self-representation. The thin reflective-plus-theory-mediated case depends on the absence of a declared improvement objective, but that is a constitutive condition of the article's self-improving predicate rather than an architectural entailment.
4. **Universal (undeclared): derivation from a retained object and reading later failures against it distinguishes a theory-mediated pathway from a collection of editable rules whose shared reasons remain unstated.** — HOLDS — A scoped rule can change many applications, and a hierarchical rule base can derive many conclusions, but neither defeats the functional cut: once a retained shared premise is the object from which consequences are derived and against which failures are interpreted, the pathway meets the article's operational use of theory regardless of whether its syntax is called a rule. No case was found in which the shared reason remains unrepresented while premise-level revision propagates across the rules that depend on it.
5. **Universal (undeclared): evidence-responsive operative self-change is self-improving relative to a declared objective even when a particular installed update makes outcomes worse.** — HOLDS — The Tuesday rule is the natural counterexample, but it still satisfies the process predicate: evidence shaped an operative change in pursuit of the declared objective, while the later evidence shows that change was bad. Blind mutation, accidental rewriting, and an unevaluated work product remain excluded because they fail evidence-responsiveness or operativity.
6. **Universal (undeclared): learning inside a fixed decomposition inherits that decomposition's omissions, while an explicit self-theory provides an addressable surface on which an omitted responsibility or bad division can be stated and revised.** — HOLDS — Parametric learning and search can sometimes discover equivalent behavior without explicit prose, but that does not defeat the narrower affordance claim: without an addressable representation, the pathway cannot identify and selectively revise the omitted commitment as a stable semantic object. Named rules provide partial addressability, but rules with an unstated shared reason do not provide the premise-level handle asserted here.
7. **Existential (stated as “can”): retained natural-language theory can become operative inside a system when an interpreter with application judgment consumes it.** — HOLDS — Ambiguity and model misapplication defeat reliability, not possibility, and the premise explicitly conditions on application judgment. The tag-README trace supplies a bounded realization in the article's human-inclusive frame: a retained claim was retrieved to interpret a strain, carried into a type and validator, and later behavior changed through those artifacts.
8. **Universal (undeclared): a self-theory needs consequences tested against evidence or a standard the theory did not itself produce; otherwise its evaluator can be self-sealing.** — HOLDS — Internal consistency alone is a possible objection, but it cannot distinguish two coherent rival self-theories that make different empirical predictions. A next failure, a criterion fixed in advance, an outside reader, or an independently maintained invariant can supply the needed contradiction; the article does not require that the standard be physically external to the system boundary.
9. **Universal (undeclared): after a system has been placed on the property grid, occurrence, revision-surface, and later-episode compounding tests are sufficient to show that the integrated self-theory loop is closed.** — DEFEATED — A system can contain an unused retained self-theory and also have a rule-only improvement path that installs a causal-evidence checklist and later uses that checklist to make another update. That path can pass occurrence, expose a bounded revision surface, and show ablated later dependence while never using or revising the self-theory. The evidence protocol needs an explicit mediation trace or intervention for theory → self-change → outcome → theory revision, not only the three generic improvement-path tests. — LOCAL — instance
10. **Universal (undeclared): compounding requires a later improvement to causally depend on an earlier retained benefit, and neither a contemporaneous acceptance score nor an unablated narrative trace establishes that dependence.** — HOLDS — A bundle can improve later performance while leaving the causal contributor unidentified, and a model can cite an earlier artifact without needing it; both attempted counterexamples are already excluded by the demand for ablation or assigned conditions. The requirement also correctly leaves a merely growing rule set as accumulation when each subsequent update remains equally hard to find and install.
11. **Statistical (stated as “most reported self-improving harnesses”): artifact-rewriting harnesses usually occupy the reflective-plus-self-improving, non-theory-mediated cell.** — DOUBTFUL — The article inventories a bounded set selected for readable revision paths, but gives no denominator, search protocol, or sampling argument for the population of “reported self-improving harnesses.” The examined cases support an existence claim and a recurring pattern; they do not yet support “most,” especially when weight-updating and search-based self-improvement systems are part of the named population. — LOCAL — prevalence
12. **Universal, bounded to Prime Agent, Recuris, and Apodex 1.1: the two artifact loops retain rules without a shared derivational self-theory, while the weight-based system is not a deployment-time self-improvement loop.** — HOLDS — Prime Agent's recorded triggers and intended effects are per-edit provenance rather than a shared premise from which edits derive; Recuris retains skills, triggers, state specifications, and checkers but no rationale whose revision propagates across them; and Apodex's durable weight change occurs in developer-run offline training while its coordination state is run-scoped. No contrary retained object or deployment-time update path appears in the evidence the article reports.
13. **Universal, bounded to the examined systems: none demonstrates recurrent later-episode dependence through a retained improvement.** — HOLDS — HyperAgents shows one later transfer but does not isolate the responsible retained change and finds no advantage from continued evolution of transferred rather than fresh meta-agents; Recuris's additional rounds do not rise beyond its reported rerun noise; and the article identifies no repeated causal chain in Prime Agent, Apodex, or Commonplace. These are candidate single links or accumulation, not a recurrent sequence.
14. **Universal, bounded to the declared Commonplace frame: Commonplace is a human-inclusive partial realization, with a causally operative self-theory but decisive interpretation, admission, and direction still substantially supplied by maintainers.** — HOLDS — Treating it as merely a documented codebase is the strongest counterexample, but the tag-README history supplies both directions of causal connection and one theory-mediated candidate-forming path. The model-only Naur repairs show a limit in actor allocation rather than defeating the human-inclusive attribution, because maintainers are inside the frame and the article explicitly declines a computational-closure claim.

## For the human
Premise 2 is the first place to look: the three named conditions do not require the self-theory and the self-improvement path to be the same causal loop, and premise 9 shows the matching evidential gap.
