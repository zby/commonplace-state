# Premise decomposition: An invitation to the SPADE authors

**Note:** kb/work/causal-impact-selective-evaluation/for-spade-authors.md
**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** SPADE's separation of environment proposal, validation, and learning-value estimation is a plausible precedent for HCL-style selective evaluation by generated acceptance procedures, but only if acceptance-specific gates independently establish observation validity, containment, decision value, and selection validity.

## Premises and counterexamples

1. **SPADE actually separates proposal breadth, validity checking, and value estimation in a way that can be abstracted from its training setting.** — HOLDS — The draft brackets SPADE's unreproduced benchmark gains and uses only the architectural separation; the strongest counterexample would be a bridge that depends on those empirical gains rather than on the separable roles, and the text does not make that move.
2. **A curriculum-oriented executable environment generator can be repurposed as precedent for an acceptance-evaluation procedure generator.** — DOUBTFUL — SPADE's generator seeks environments useful for policy learning, while acceptance evidence must test whether an incumbent or candidate satisfies an external obligation; the shared executable-environment form may hide a different objective, reward signal, and failure mode. — LOCAL — priced-exception
3. **Selective evaluation in an HCL-style harness has a real evidence-cost problem that makes generated or selected procedures worth considering.** — HOLDS — A tiny, cheap, complete evaluation suite would remove the motivation for selection, but the premise only needs the ordinary growing-historical-surface case the draft names, where repeated full evaluation becomes costly.
4. **An impact theory can predict affected behavioral functions or obligations well enough to guide evidence acquisition.** — DOUBTFUL — A candidate may introduce an indirect regression through a shared tool, routing rule, prompt constraint, or cross-step interaction outside the predicted impact surface, causing the selector to skip the evidence that would reveal the harm. — LOCAL — instance
5. **An obligation-directed synthesizer can generate procedures that observe the specified obligation rather than merely produce arbitrary incumbent-candidate differences.** — DOUBTFUL — A generated procedure could reliably distinguish incumbent from candidate by measuring prompt-length sensitivity, parser brittleness, fixture quirks, or another incidental artifact instead of the intended obligation. — LOCAL — instance
6. **Acceptance evidence can rely on an observation-validity authority independent of the procedure generator.** — DOUBTFUL — If the only available authority is the same generator, impact theory, or scoring loop that produced the procedure, then "valid observation" becomes circular and a real behavioral difference still does not show that the intended obligation was measured. — GLOBAL — instance
7. **Execution validity, observation validity, decision value, and selection validity are separable gates.** — HOLDS — A procedure can parse and terminate while observing the wrong behavior, observe the right behavior while costing too much, or be valid and cheap while selected from a biased surface; those cases support the separation rather than defeat it.
8. **Generated Python with functional checks is not enough to establish execution containment for acceptance evidence.** — HOLDS — Functional checks can show that a generated procedure produces expected outputs, but they do not establish resource limits, filesystem isolation, network isolation, or absence of unintended side effects.
9. **Memory of earlier regressions, false negatives, redundant procedures, and unsuitable procedures can improve later generation without collapsing exploration onto a narrow historical surface.** — DOUBTFUL — If remembered failures and reusable templates dominate generation, the system may overfit to already-known regression shapes and improve reuse while worsening discovery of novel impact routes. — LOCAL — prevalence
10. **The proposed four-arm experiment can separate failures of impact prediction, procedure synthesis, observation-validity certification, containment, and cost amortization.** — DOUBTFUL — A fixed collection of recorded harness candidates can measure detection and cost on that surface, but component failures may interact: a bad impact theory can starve synthesis of the right target, and a weak oracle can make procedure quality look better than it is. — LOCAL — instance
11. **A held-out audit surface can reveal whether selective generation misses effects outside its predicted surface.** — DOUBTFUL — If the audit surface is drawn from the same obligation taxonomy, historical candidate distribution, or impact model assumptions as the selector, it may preserve the same blind spots and undercount harmful misses. — LOCAL — prevalence

## For the human

Look first at premise 6: the bridge needs a non-circular observation-validity authority, because without it the generated procedure can expose a real difference that still is not acceptance evidence.
