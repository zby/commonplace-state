- Verification: every expected assignment has exactly one judgment, with no extra or missing rows; note outcomes agree with their row judgments. The final selector reports zero stale pairs. See [verification](../../state/tag-placement/2026-09-26/verification.json) and [freshness selection](../../state/tag-placement/2026-09-26/freshness-verification.json).
