# Friction check: Reflection may improve sample efficiency under structured shifts

**Note:** kb/notes/reflection-may-improve-sample-efficiency-under-structured-shifts.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** For task families sharing a stable mechanism or compositional rule that a reflective system's retained, addressable hypothesis correctly captures and can retrieve and validate economically, that hypothesis-based pathway should reach a fixed target performance using fewer new target observations than a matched parametric-adaptation baseline requires.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

The note concretizes to the single conditional prediction quoted in "What would test it," and it does not smuggle a second, incompatible claim under the same title. The nearest candidate for an "as fast as C and as dynamic as Lisp" split is the pull between (a) insisting the distinctive contribution of reflection is *addressability*, not more or better transfer ("What an explicit hypothesis offers is not transfer where parametric systems have none, but stronger, first-class addressability of the transferred thing") and (b) cashing the payoff out as a quantitative claim that explicit pathways need *fewer* target observations than parametric ones — which is, on its face, a claim about more effective transfer. These do not actually contradict: the note's implicit reconciliation (spelled out in the linked second-order-lessons note, not in this note's own body) is that addressability doesn't move more content, it makes the same retained content correctable by a single discontinuous operation (reject/rescope) rather than only by gradual, dilution-prone updates — so the saving is in update *cost per lesson*, not transfer *amount*. That reconciliation holds, but it lives outside this note and is only cross-referenced, not argued in place — see joint 4.

## Signal — thinnest joints

1. **"What persistence specifically adds over that transient case is availability across sessions without re-deriving or re-stating the commitment each time, applicability outside any single context window, and structure that can be checked by a machine, not only read by one."** (Parametric transfer section) — UNSUPPORTED — This sentence has to do real work: the preceding sentence just conceded that a plain prompt stating a mechanism, its applicability conditions, and its failure modes is *already* "a separately named, inspectable object... without any commitment ever being persisted or reflected on" — which threatens the note's whole basis for treating addressability as something reflection/persistence specifically buys. The rescue asserts persistence adds machine-checkability, but nothing about persistence-versus-transience implies that; whether an artifact is checkable by a machine is a question of representational form (schema vs. natural-language text), independent of whether it survives past one context window. A persisted markdown commitment is exactly as unstructured as a prompt unless something else (codification) makes it so. This is the joint that most needs repair — it patches a genuine concession with an unargued distinction.

2. **"what would distinguish this conjecture's structured shifts from that [bitter-lesson-absorption] pattern is that reach — naming a commitment's applicability conditions — is a falsifiable, per-commitment claim, not a bet that all captured structure stays durable"** (structured-shift section) — UNSUPPORTED — The bitter-lesson worry being answered is an empirical one: those who name legible structure usually watch a scaled general method eventually absorb and beat it. Answering "but our claim is falsifiable per commitment" addresses testability, not the empirical worry — a conjecture can be perfectly falsifiable and still be exactly the kind of claim the bitter-lesson pattern predicts will fail almost every time. Falsifiability distinguishes this conjecture from an unfalsifiable one; it does nothing to distinguish structured-shift cases from the routine cases the bitter-lesson critique already covers. This also sits in tension with the note's own earlier disclaimer that "reach... is not evidence for the conjecture" (para. 1) — here reach is doing exactly the work of rebutting a rival explanation, which is evidential work in function if not in name.

3. **"The transfer literature supports exactly this conditional shape and no more"** (structured-shift section) — THIN — Schölkopf et al. (causal mechanisms surviving intervention-like change), Bengio et al. (adaptation speed as a training signal), and Ellis et al./DreamCoder (compounding explicit program libraries) are three different research programs, cited with one clause each, none of which is framed by its authors as calibrating precisely where a coverage boundary starts and stops. "Exactly this shape and no more" is a totalizing precision claim that three thin parenthetical citations can't carry — the literature is consistent with the conditional shape, which is weaker than supporting it "exactly."

4. **"Addressability can support explicit, reusable hypotheses whose assumptions, applicability conditions, failures, and revisions are separately inspectable. When such a commitment captures structure that remains stable across a task shift... and it is retrieved, applied, and validated economically, it may reduce the new target evidence required for adaptation."** (core conjecture, para. 2) — THIN — This is the load-bearing step from "addressable" to "needs fewer target observations," and within this note it is asserted rather than derived: nothing in the surrounding text explains *why* addressability specifically (versus, say, any correct retained structure regardless of addressability) would reduce the observation count. The actual mechanism — that a single lesson can trigger a discontinuous reject/rescope instead of gradual gradient dilution — is developed in the linked second-order-lessons note and only gestured at here via a Relevant-Notes annotation ("mechanism: the operations by which a single target observation can trigger a rejection or rescoping instead of a gradient nudge"). A reader confined to this note's body has to take the central inferential step on trust.

5. **"That is physicist David Deutsch's point in *The Beginning of Infinity*: a good explanation applies far beyond the problem that produced it."** (para. 1, defining "reach") — THIN — Deutsch's criterion is about explanatory *quality* — what makes an explanation good is, among other things, that it applies broadly. The note borrows this to license its own technical definition of reach as a learned commitment's generalization scope, independent of whether the commitment is any good. A wrong or overfit commitment can also have wide reach in this note's sense (it's invoked wherever retrieval surfaces it) without being a "good explanation" in Deutsch's sense at all. The citation lends conceptual flavor to "reach" but doesn't establish that the two notions coincide.

## For the human
Look first at joint 1: the note concedes, in passing, that a plain prompt already gets the addressability it wants to credit to reflection/persistence, then patches the concession with an unsupported machine-checkability claim — check whether that concession quietly undercuts the note's basis for calling the conjectured advantage reflection-specific rather than explicitness-specific.
