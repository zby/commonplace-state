# Friction check: When systems learn theories about themselves

**Note:** kb/articles/when-systems-learn-theories-about-themselves.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** Reflective theory-mediated self-improvement is a coherent intersection in which a retained, interpretable, causally connected theory of a system guides evidence-responsive changes to that system and is revised from their results, while the systems examined here do not yet demonstrate recurrent compounding and still lack either a shared derivational self-theory or independence from human interpretation, admission, and direction.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES
The three properties can coexist in one architecture: the theory can be both an intermediate object and part of the system's causally connected self-representation; an interpreter can derive changes from it; an independently fixed objective and evaluator can judge the results; and those results can revise the theory. Keeping the judging standard outside the theory's unilateral control resolves the apparent tension between a theory guiding change and evidence remaining able to defeat that theory. The empirical claim that the examined systems have not demonstrated recurrent compounding is compatible with the architectural claim that such a loop is possible.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“a revised self-model that does drive changes becomes self-improving the moment an objective is declared, so what keeps a system here is the absence of a declared objective, not an architectural difference”** — UNSUPPORTED — A declared objective alone does not establish the article's other requirements for self-improvement: the changes must be operative, evidence-responsive, and directed at the system's behavior-determining organization. The cell's own example also says the self-model is not used to select changes, which is an architectural difference independent of whether an objective is declared.
2. **“Read against that boundary it has all three properties, and cheaply — so does any maintained codebase with design documents and a change process, which is why the reported systems, whose research teams sit outside their declared boundaries, are not directly comparable with it.”** — UNSUPPORTED — Design documents may be inert, a change process need not derive changes from them, and neither feature ensures that evidence revises a causally connected self-theory. The stated features therefore do not establish that every such codebase has all three properties; choosing different boundaries also complicates comparison but does not by itself make comparison impossible.
3. **“A system judging a theory about itself is contradicted by the world whenever the loop tests the theory's consequences against something it did not produce ... Where the loop judges the theory against a standard reconstructed from the theory itself, nothing contradicts it”** — UNSUPPORTED — An independently produced observation or standard can agree with the theory, while a self-derived standard can still expose an inconsistency or failed prediction. External provenance creates an opportunity for independent correction, and self-derived evaluation risks self-sealing, but provenance alone establishes neither inevitable contradiction nor its impossibility.
4. **“no rationale from which the skills were derived is retained, so revising one skill propagates to none of the others that share its reason. That is accumulation with a good gate.”** — UNSUPPORTED — Missing retained rationale blocks explicit derivational propagation, but it does not show that a changed skill cannot influence later revisions through behavior, shared context, model inference, or the gate. Counts of added, revised, and deprecated skills establish growth of the rule set; they do not establish the absence of the later-episode dependence the article defines as compounding.
5. **“That reading is about decomposition.”** — THIN — Argyris's contrast supports questioning governing objectives and policies, but the article immediately expands decomposition to component boundaries, missing responsibilities, representable failures, and available revisions. The cited contrast is one instance of revising governing commitments; the bridge from that instance to the broader representational claim is not supplied.

## For the human
Look first at the pairwise classification: specify what besides declaring an objective turns a reflective, theory-mediated diagnostic system into an evidence-responsive self-improving one, or narrow the claim that no architectural difference remains.
