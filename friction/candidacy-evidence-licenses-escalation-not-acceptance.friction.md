# Friction check: Candidacy evidence licenses escalation to assessment, not acceptance

**Note:** `kb/notes/candidacy-evidence-licenses-escalation-not-acceptance.md`
**Central claim (one sentence):** In staged evaluation, evidence that only warrants escalating a candidate to assessment cannot by that fact alone warrant accepting the assessed conclusion, even if the same evidence item might separately satisfy both bars.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

The claim's main commitments can coexist: staged workflows can distinguish routing from acceptance, the labels can track what a piece of evidence currently licenses rather than its intrinsic kind, and one evidence item can satisfy both roles only if it has warrant for each role rather than inheriting verdict authority from nomination alone.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **"Otherwise the workflow creates an immunizing slot, where a claim is accepted on evidence calibrated only to justify assessment."** — UNSUPPORTED — The note identifies a real danger, but it does not yet show why deciding on routing-grade evidence necessarily immunizes the claim rather than merely lowering the acceptance standard or raising error risk.
2. **"The conjecture therefore forbids one authority transfer: evidence that has cleared only the candidacy bar cannot, by that fact, decide acceptance."** — THIN — The Peirce frame, the warrant-granularity rule, and the two witnesses support a strong caution, but they do not yet derive a universal prohibition across staged assessments rather than a defeasible default.
3. **"Since theory warrant should be tracked at the finest granularity evidence licenses, warrant for pursuit-worthiness cannot silently become warrant for the assessed conclusion."** — THIN — This inference works if candidacy and verdict judgments are already specified as distinct licensed contents in the same way the linked note treats claims and bundles, but that extension from intra-theory bookkeeping to stage-role boundaries is still assumed here rather than argued.
4. **"The case does not show that role isolation caused the change, but it does show correct nomination without verdict authority."** — THIN — The Pirolli case shows that thematic fit did not settle support, but it does not establish "correct nomination" in any stronger sense than "worth checking," so the example supports verdict insufficiency more directly than successful candidacy.
5. **"In both witnesses below, cheap routing signals are attractive, but cheapness is not what makes them candidacy evidence."** — HOLDS — On the note's own role-based definition, the evidence is candidacy evidence because it licenses opening an assessment; the open questions already concede that cheapness is at most a shared correlate, not the defining property.

## For the human
Look first at the jump from "routing-grade evidence underdetermines verdicts in these cases" to "accepting on it creates an immunizing slot"; that is where the note most needs the author's missing concretization.
