# Friction check: Theory warrant should be tracked at the finest granularity evidence licenses

**Note:** `kb/notes/natural-language-theories-carry-warrant-claim-by-claim.md`
**Central claim (one sentence):** Warrant should attach to the most specific truth-apt claim, conjunction, or model and scope that evidence supports, with bundle-level warrant reaching components only through separately justified attribution or dependency propagation.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

Fine-grained attribution, joint or theory-level warrant, and propagation through explicit dependencies can coexist because the rule bars automatic component attribution, not component warrant established by further evidence or argument.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“When evidence discriminates only a conjunction or integrated model, the warranted unit is that bundle and its scope. Do not distribute this joint warrant to the bundle's components without evidence or an argument that identifies their individual contributions.”** — THIN — The reason works for attributing which component made an integrated model succeed, but a literal warranted conjunction entails each conjunct without identifying its individual causal contribution. The note later permits dependency propagation, yet this joint does not say whether conjunct elimination is permitted propagation or forbidden distribution.
2. **“A test that discriminates only an integrated bundle from a rival warrants the bundle-scope pair.”** — UNSUPPORTED — Such a result can warrant a comparative claim about the bundle's prediction or performance, but it does not by itself warrant every truth-apt commitment contained in the bundle. The inference needs to specify the bundle-level proposition that the test discriminated.
3. **“Evidential attribution and dependency propagation are separate operations. First, identify the most specific truth-apt unit the evidence supports. Then use the theory's dependencies to determine which other units inherit a derivation or lose warrant when a premise fails.”** — THIN — The conceptual distinction is useful, but the proposed order assumes that the directly supported unit can be identified before inspecting the dependency and identification assumptions that determine what the evidence bears on. The note names those assumptions elsewhere but does not resolve this operational dependence.
4. **“This makes the theory a candidate for explanatory-reach: the account may keep working wherever its named dependencies hold.”** — THIN — Exposing dependencies, rival-sensitive consequences, and falsifiers establishes criticizability. It does not establish that the named dependencies are complete or causal enough to support transfer, so the step from testable structure to explanatory-reach candidacy remains conditional on an unstated adequacy test.
5. **“Representational form … changes which checks are available, but it does not relax the non-distribution rule. … Joint empirical support remains joint until further evidence attributes it, whatever form records the theory.”** — THIN — The non-distribution principle can be form-independent, but the note defines theories through named, inspectable parts and does not explain how a distributed-parametric or mixed representation supplies stable truth-apt units to which the rule can attach. The conclusion outruns the natural-language and formal cases that the paragraph concretizes.

## For the human
Look first at the conjunction/integrated-model joint: distinguish warrant inherited by logical consequence from attribution of which component produced an integrated empirical result.
