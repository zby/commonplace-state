# Friction check: A provisional theory-mediated improvement loop

**Note:** `kb/work/explanatory-theories-deployment-time-learning/theory-mediated-improvement-loop.md`
**Central claim (one sentence):** An explicit system theory can serve as a shared intermediate between separately decided system-change and theory-revision loops, but cumulative theory-mediated improvement requires retained theory that later work actually consumes and causal tests that distinguish theory content from extra deliberation or mere uptake.

## Filter

**Verdict:** SURVIVES

The claim commits to one architecture whose goals can coexist: the working theory may affect proposal formation and judgment, evidence may update the system and theory separately, and only the retained treatment makes a cross-episode claim. Separate acceptance decisions prevent successful behavior and a successful explanation from being treated as the same result.

## Signal — thinnest joints

1. **“To count as a theory treatment rather than merely a plan, prediction cache, or candidate-ranking policy, the contract for `tau_n` must be fixed before the outcomes used to assess it are observed.”** — UNSUPPORTED — Recording the artifact before outcomes is needed to identify its prospective effect, but timing does not distinguish a theory from a plan or prediction cache. A mechanism-bearing explanation formed after observation can still be a theory proposal, while a precommitted artifact can still be only a plan; the stated reason supports an experimental-treatment constraint, not the categorical boundary asserted here.
2. **“These roles can fail independently. … Experiments should therefore isolate diagnosis and search, candidate generation and prioritization, evidence selection, post-evidence judgment, and cross-episode theory revision before testing their joint effect.”** — UNSUPPORTED — The two examples establish that some roles can separate, not that all five are independently variable. Even full separability would justify role-specific measurements or interventions, but it would not require isolating every role *before* a joint-effect experiment; that sequencing needs an additional identification or cost argument.
3. **“A useful promotion rule is not evidence that `tau_n` is true; a sound object theory does not determine whether retaining it is worth its lifecycle cost. Experiments should therefore name the theory type each treatment supplies and gate changes to the improvement process independently of the candidates that process judges.”** — THIN — The premises establish distinct truth and lifecycle questions, so they support distinct criteria. They do not by themselves establish that the decisions must use independent gates rather than one gate with explicit, non-substitutable criteria; the missing joint is why decision separation, not only criterion separation, is necessary.
4. **“Current-task improvement and sampled historical retention establish behavior only on the checked cases, not the broader reach of the rationale offered for the change.”** — THIN — Checked cases do not establish the rationale’s mechanism or explanatory reach, but a properly sampled evaluation can support a bounded population-level behavioral estimate beyond the literal cases observed. The sentence needs to separate that statistical generalization from the broader mechanistic generalization it correctly withholds.
5. **“Content interventions provide a stronger test: hold recommendations fixed while swapping or corrupting mechanism claims, or hold theory content fixed while hiding its recommendations.”** — THIN — These interventions target content rather than the mere presence of an intermediate artifact, but “stronger” depends on preserving coherence, information, and task difficulty across arms. Without a matching criterion, corruption can introduce a new quality cue or inconsistency, so an observed effect need not identify the mechanism claim itself.

## For the human

Look first at the pre-outcome contract: decide whether it defines a theory at all or only the conditions under which a theory artifact can serve as an identifiable prospective treatment.
