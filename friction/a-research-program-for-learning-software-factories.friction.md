# Friction check: A research program for learning software factories

**Note:** kb/articles/a-research-program-for-learning-software-factories.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** An LLM-based software factory can be tested for factory-level learning without changing model weights by intervening on its acquisition, retention, activation, and revision of project theory, then comparing the causally resulting successor state with its predecessor under a predeclared objective.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“the theory can be decisively tested only by becoming operative in machinery that acts on it, so the missing software-factory machinery should be forced into existence as retained theory demands consequences”** — UNSUPPORTED — Making theory operative may be necessary for the proposed self-directed test, but it does not establish that retained theory will cause absent machinery to be built; that requires the unshown activation and construction capacities the program is trying to create.
2. **“a factory that improves coherently past shallow patching needs a held, revisable theory of its own organization”** — UNSUPPORTED — The earlier Naur cases motivate project theory as a functional hypothesis, not as a necessity, and the article itself leaves raw-record retention and other learning mechanisms as live rivals.
3. **“judgment, unlike recomputation, is non-deterministic, so it could as easily settle differently”** — UNSUPPORTED — Non-determinism does not imply equally easy or likely alternatives, and neither the distinction between judgment and recomputation nor the impossibility of deterministically reaching the same commitment is established.
4. **“An index or a summary is derived organization, rearranging evidence the production record already contains and regenerable from it. Building a theory does more”** — THIN — Summaries and indexes can select, interpret, and resolve ambiguity rather than merely rearrange evidence, so regenerability alone does not cleanly separate derivation from theory-building commitment; the later per-resolution qualification narrows but does not repair the categorical setup.
5. **“The strongest current control is fact-matched: it supplies the same atomic project propositions without their theory-level organization, so a positive result shows more than that context helps”** — THIN — Removing organization also changes presentation, salience, and relations available in context, so the contrast identifies the supplied organization as a bundle, as the next sentence concedes, but does not by itself establish theory-holding or theory-specific activation.

## For the human
Look first at the claimed step from making theory operative to theory forcing missing factory machinery into existence; the article has not concretized the mechanism that would make that prediction follow.
