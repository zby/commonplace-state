# Friction check: Productive deferral requires a preserved option, discriminating evidence, and a convergence rule

**Note:** kb/notes/productive-deferral-requires-option-evidence-and-convergence.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** A consequential choice is productively deferred only when a future option remains exercisable, later evidence can change the action, and a rule reconnects a return condition to a response; these conditions are necessary but do not by themselves show that waiting is worth its cost.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

Keeping an option feasible, seeking action-changing evidence, and specifying how the choice returns to decision can all hold at once; the claim expressly leaves the separate cost comparison unresolved.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“The response may select, modify, abandon, authorize more bounded work, or transfer the reopened choice to an authorized decision procedure.”** — UNSUPPORTED — Treating authorization of more work as a convergence response does not establish convergence: bounded rounds can repeat indefinitely unless the rule also has a latest terminal point, a decreasing resource budget, or another condition that eventually forces selection, transfer, or abandonment.
2. **“If every possible result leaves the same action preferred, the delay or activity may produce information, but it does not make this decision better informed.”** — UNSUPPORTED — An unchanged preferred action does not imply an unchanged evidential basis; an observation can alter confidence or estimated consequences without crossing an action threshold. The premise establishes no decision-changing result, not that the decision cannot become better informed.
3. **“They support only the shared option–evidence–convergence mechanism.”** — UNSUPPORTED — The note’s summaries distribute different components across the cited methods, but do not show that the full three-part mechanism is shared by them or explain why their combination makes each component necessary for every productive deferral.
4. **“The three-part test rejects false deferrals before this domain-specific comparison”** — THIN — The test can reject candidates missing one of its necessary conditions, but the note also says a candidate can satisfy all three and still not be worth its cost. The inference needs “false deferrals” narrowed to hidden commitment, decision-irrelevant delay, and unmanaged indecision rather than all deferrals that should not proceed.
5. **“A date without a response merely schedules discussion. A signal without a mapped response still permits an ad hoc choice.”** — THIN — These sentences establish that a trigger alone under-specifies what follows, but not that a substantive response must be mapped in advance: a date or signal can route the choice into an authorized decision procedure, which the preceding paragraph itself accepts as a response.

## For the human
Look first at what makes the convergence rule actually terminal, because repeated authorization of “more bounded work” can preserve the unmanaged indecision the rule is meant to exclude.
