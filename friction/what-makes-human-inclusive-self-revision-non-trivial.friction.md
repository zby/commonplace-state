# Friction check: What makes human-inclusive self-revision non-trivial?

**Note:** kb/articles/what-makes-human-inclusive-self-revision-non-trivial.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** Human-inclusive self-revision requires a reusable path that can take criticism of repository-defined authority-bearing arrangements through warranted admission and operative installation into later revisable successors, and Commonplace currently demonstrates that path only broadly and incompletely.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

The claim wants four things at once: humans inside the system boundary, revision of authority-bearing artifacts rather than mere file edits, continuity when revision machinery itself changes, and no requirement to encode a maintainer's private judgment. Those coexist once the claim is scoped to repository-defined artifacts and relations within Commonplace's declared frame; the article does not need a fully internalized admission record for the claim to stay coherent, only for the affordance to remain incomplete in practice.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **"The same path could challenge the core criterion, not just refine or rename it."** — UNSUPPORTED — The article shows refinements, reuse, and renaming around explanatory-reach, but it does not exhibit a case where the criterion that currently warrants review is itself displaced through that same path.
2. **"The strongest gap in the broader revision affordance is generic maintainer admission."** — UNSUPPORTED — The article gives a real admission gap, but it does not compare that gap against the model-binding and authority-decomposition gaps strongly enough to earn the ranking word "strongest."
3. **"Adding research teams would make those systems broadly editable too, but it would not show how those teams' decisions become operative or remain open to later revision."** — THIN — The distinction is plausible, but on this page it depends on an unshown comparison with the companion article's systems rather than on evidence developed here.
4. **"Retaining the result in operative artifacts turns the intervention into part of the system's revision machinery rather than an undocumented action outside its description."** — THIN — Retention and operative consumption show that the change is recorded and used, but they do not by themselves show that later revision episodes must consult the retained path rather than bypass it.
5. **"This self-application structurally resembles a metacircular interpreter."** — THIN — The article establishes self-application of revisable governing artifacts, but the stronger interpreter analogy needs a more explicit mapping between those artifacts and interpreter semantics.

## For the human
Look first at the move from observed revision around explanatory-reach to the stronger claim that the same path could replace the criterion itself; that is where the article most needs the author's missing concretization.
