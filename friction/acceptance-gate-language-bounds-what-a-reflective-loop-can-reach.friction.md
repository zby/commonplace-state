# Friction check: Admitting nonbinding natural-language candidates lets criticism precede formalization

**Note:** kb/notes/acceptance-gate-language-bounds-what-a-reflective-loop-can-reach.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** Within reflective loops over externally interpreted theories, admitting nonbinding natural-language candidates permits direct criticism before faithful formalization, while semantic reach still depends on translation and re-expression paths and cheap prototyping still depends on authority, coupling, and rollback cost.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

The properties can coexist in one staged process: a language-capable critic can evaluate a nonbinding prose proposal before a trusted translation exists, later symbolic artifacts can remain low-authority prototypes, and an independently warranted translation can let a formal-only gate reach the same semantic candidate. The claim does not require natural language to provide proof-style warrant, make rejection cheap by itself, or remain the candidate's permanent form.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“When retained without operative authority, the theory becomes a preformal prototype: explicit enough to reuse and revise”** — UNSUPPORTED — Retention and lack of operative authority establish persistence and limited authority, not that the retained theory is explicit enough for reliable reuse or revision. A vague or internally ambiguous prose artifact can have both properties and still fail as a prototype; the inference needs an additional admission or retention criterion for explicit commitments.
2. **“Many theories need no crossing into symbolic form at all.”** — UNSUPPORTED — The preceding argument shows that codification need not end prototype standing and that natural language can remain useful before codification. It does not establish how many theories never acquire a symbolic consumer or supply cases in which remaining wholly in natural language is sufficient through the relevant lifecycle.
3. **“The important property is not a mandatory return to natural language, but an editable representation upstream of the operative artifact.”** — THIN — The two repair paths show that repair need not return to prose, but they do not establish an editable upstream representation as the necessary or decisive property. Direct replacement, regeneration from evidence, or revision of the operative artifact itself remain possible, while editability alone does not ensure that a repair preserves the intended commitments.
4. **“The gate can inspect the surrogate, but it cannot use the same formal check to establish that the surrogate is faithful to the original theory.”** — THIN — This holds when the formal check proves only a property of the surrogate under a fixed interpretation. The categorical wording does not delimit that case from a gate that also checks a proof-carrying translation or a formal correspondence claim; those devices still leave a world-facing interpretation boundary, but the note must identify where the extra warrant necessarily enters.

## For the human
Look first at the move from retention plus nonbinding authority to an explicit, reusable prototype; the note needs to say what makes a prose candidate concrete enough to earn that standing.
