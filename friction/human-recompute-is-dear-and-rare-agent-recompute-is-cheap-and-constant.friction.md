# Friction check: Opposed recompute factors do not decide documentation segmentation

**Note:** `kb/notes/human-recompute-is-dear-and-rare-agent-recompute-is-cheap-and-constant.md`
**Central claim (one sentence):** For documentation that caches a re-readable source, crossed audience profiles on net saving per reconstruction and reconstructions avoided do not by themselves order net cache value, and a separate audience-specific layer is warranted only when its marginal specialization value exceeds its incremental maintenance and drift burden.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

High net saving with few avoided reconstructions and low net saving with many avoided reconstructions can coexist, and either product can be larger once magnitudes are supplied. Treating cache value and the marginal value of a separate audience-specific layer as distinct comparisons adds no incompatible commitment.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“Human reacquisition — retention, context switching, source churn, contributor turnover, and the rate at which relevant new readers arrive” / “Agent recurrence — relevant fresh-session volume” as quantities to estimate before comparing cache values** — THIN — A relevant reader, context switch, or fresh session is not yet a reconstruction avoided. The bridge also needs the counterfactual rate at which the equivalent output would have been reconstructed from source and the rate at which consulting the document actually substitutes for that work at the required reliability; the listed proxies do not establish either rate.
2. **“net cache value ≈ (net cost saved per equivalent reconstruction) × (reconstructions avoided) − (maintenance)”** — THIN — The product equals a horizon total only if the per-reconstruction saving is constant or is an event-weighted mean over the reconstructions actually avoided. The note does not specify that aggregation, so heterogeneous tasks or correlation between saving and recurrence can make one scalar saving times one scalar count misstate the comparison.
3. **“The split decision therefore requires one more measurement: specialization value relative to the best shared content. A second layer pays only when that marginal audience-fit benefit exceeds its incremental maintenance and drift burden.”** — THIN — The examples establish that cache value and audience fit are independent, but they do not establish that specialization value is commensurable with maintenance or already nets out every other marginal effect of the layer, such as changed consultation and verification costs or changes to the shared artifact. Without that accounting boundary, the proposed comparison is not yet operational.
4. **“A low-traffic layer may receive fewer incidental comparisons with source and therefore fewer opportunities for someone to notice drift.”** — THIN — Lower traffic reduces detection opportunities only when some reads actually prompt comparison with source. The note correctly says traffic is not itself a detector, but it supplies no condition or comparison rate that turns traffic loss into an incremental drift-detection burden; the reason supports a possibility, not an estimate.

## For the human
Look first at the missing bridge from human-reacquisition and agent-session proxies to counterfactual reconstructions actually avoided; without it, the two-factor model cannot be populated even though the crossed-factor algebra holds.
