# Friction check: A theoretical artifact must preserve the scope of each named system choice

**Note:** kb/notes/a-theory-may-name-a-choice-only-as-a-bound-variable.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** A theoretical artifact preserves the scope of a proposition that names system-chosen machinery only when intended consumers can recover both which choice the name denotes and how that choice functions from context allowed by the interpretation boundary and guaranteed by the consumption boundary, whether or not the sentence contains explicit binder syntax.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

The requirements coexist: guaranteed artifact context may supply assignment and role without sentence-local quantifier syntax, while the boundary condition excludes dependence on origin context that consumers will not receive.

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“Because the collection has changed, the sampling recipe cannot reconstruct the historical cohort.”** — UNSUPPORTED — Change prevents replaying the recipe against the current tree, but it does not establish that no versioned tree, retained snapshot, or other lineage record can recover the cohort. The reported loss of the workshop and manifest rules out those two sources, not every reconstruction path.
2. **“A fixed subject can support theory, but varying its inputs is not enough. An input/output table may remain a compressed system description. To contribute theory, the artifact needs an explanation, counterfactual compression, or some other substantive proposition about the fixed design.”** — THIN — The possibility that a table remains descriptive does not establish that varying inputs is never sufficient for a theoretical contribution, and “some other substantive proposition” restates rather than specifies the missing criterion. The note needs the boundary that makes explanation or counterfactual compression theory-making while excluding a sufficiently structured input/output relation.
3. **“A collection that selects for explanatory-reach retains claims that keep working beyond the cases that produced them because they explain why the pattern works. Such a collection can require its theoretical artifacts to preserve the scope of every named choice.”** — THIN — The description of explanatory-reach does not itself derive the scope requirement. The missing bridge is that an unrecoverable assignment or role prevents consumers from determining which cases the explanation covers and therefore from testing whether it works beyond its source cases.
4. **“If a design-space proposition survives, keep the theory. If only the selected value or the current or historical state it produced survives, the contribution belongs where that system's choices and state are recorded.”** — HOLDS — Once the paragraph makes intended contribution the routing basis, its two conditions support the distinction: the first retains a proposition about the design space, while the second expressly stipulates that no contribution beyond a system's value or state remains.

## For the human
Look first at the historical-cohort claim: identify the exhausted reconstruction paths, or narrow “cannot reconstruct” to what the missing workshop, manifest, and current tree cannot reconstruct.
