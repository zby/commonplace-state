# Friction check: A consumption channel delivers force without the history that earned it

**Note:** kb/notes/a-consumption-channel-delivers-force-without-the-history-that.md
**Central claim (one sentence):** A channel that confers behavioral force without consuming the artifact's warranting history makes malicious injection and honest error instances of the same unearned-authority failure, which must be addressed by coupling admission, review history, consumption, and recovery to that force.

## Filter
**Verdict:** SURVIVES

## Signal — thinnest joints
1. **“Review is what actually earns the force”** — UNSUPPORTED — The note's own central distinction says the channel confers force regardless of review; review can earn warrant for that force, but the argument does not establish that it earns the operative force itself.
2. **“Provenance, write authority, review, and rollback ... are one operation applied at four points: putting the artifact's history back into the channel”** — UNSUPPORTED — Provenance exposes history, but write restriction controls admission and rollback limits consequences; neither necessarily represents history to the consumer, so the shared description is asserted rather than derived.
3. **“Where all four hold for a channel, the boundary is closed for that channel by construction rather than by vigilance”** — THIN — Possessing all four capabilities does not by itself show that every operative write is forced through an integrity-protected review path or that the consumer verifies the resulting mark; those enforcement conditions do the constructive closing but are added only in the following gloss.
4. **“Write authority ... [is] the one that decides whether the other three ever get a chance”** — UNSUPPORTED — Provenance can be evaluated at consumption, review can occur after admission, and rollback acts after use, so the note does not establish why entrance control determines whether those mechanisms can operate.
5. **“Neither half is optional if the boundary is the thing you are closing”** — THIN — The examples show that write control alone misses authorized error and review alone misses a bypass, but they do not establish universal necessity of both controls for every architecture; a channel could instead withhold force unless a cryptographically or mechanically enforced warrant is present.

## For the human
Look first at the move from “the channel confers force” to “review earns force,” then separate warrant evidence, admission control, enforcement, and recovery instead of making all four instances of restored history.
