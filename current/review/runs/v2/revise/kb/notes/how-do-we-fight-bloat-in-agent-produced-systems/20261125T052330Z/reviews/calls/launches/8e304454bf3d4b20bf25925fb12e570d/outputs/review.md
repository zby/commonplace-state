# Friction check: How do we fight bloat in agent-produced systems?

**Central claim (one sentence):** Bloat in agent-produced mixed natural-language and code systems compounds through context, maintenance and recursive-exposure channels; Commonplace now counters it with five partial mechanisms, and these leave named problems open, chiefly the lack of a reachability check for prose and the lack of retirement triggers.

## Filter
**Filter:** SURVIVES

The note asserts two things together: Commonplace has current mechanisms, and bloat control remains open. These can coexist because the note labels the mechanisms "partial" and the query's retirement condition requires a tested regime that it does not yet claim. The closest strain is between "operative retirement path" in Current answer and "Missing triggers for retirement" in Unsettled. A procedure that runs only after someone names a target is partially operative, so the two do not contradict each other. The strain appears below as a joint, not as a dissolved claim.

## Thinnest joints

The value-gate bullet ranks first. It fills a slot in a list of *current* mechanisms, but its support describes a requirement, and the link goes to an open question. Nothing in the bullet says a value gate operates.

The retirement-path bullet ranks second. The Unsettled section partly undercuts it, and the note never separates the part of retirement that works from the part that does not.

Recursive exposure ranks third. It is the one stakes channel unique to agent-operated systems, yet its "so" rests on an anchoring effect that the note does not support.

The last two joints have evidence behind them, but they draw more from it than it carries. The brevity-distortion bullet turns "encoding diverges" into "content is kept". The rewrite bullet uses "confirm" for two small readability cases.

The remaining joints hold under the skeptical default. These include the move from "no reachability check for prose" to the Unsettled problems, the bounded documentation-sweep result that the note itself calls bounded, and "different causes call for different countermeasures". None was surfaced.
