# Review

## Summary

Most cited evidence comes from the note's own domain. The documentation sweep, the rewrite and simplification evidence notes, and the scaffolding note all concern Commonplace or agent-operated KB artifacts.

The one cross-domain transfer is the brevity-distortion item. Its supporting result is the source's fine-tuning experiment under a length penalty. The note applies that result to operational brevity pressure on agents that write KB prose without stating why the mechanism carries over. The note does hedge on a different gap, that human reading cost was inferred rather than measured. It leaves the training-versus-inference gap unbridged.
