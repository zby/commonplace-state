# Review

## Summary

No bullet buries a separable second idea. The longer bullets each develop one point:

- The "Reader update and comparative work gain" bullet names two ideas in its label. The note presents them as one of five lenses, and the second sentence explicitly "grounds that test" from the first sentence. It is one lens with elaboration.
- The "Root questions versus sub-questions inside notes" bullet is long and runs through several sentences. Its two failure modes (an orphaned-looking root note and a closed loop of mutually citing notes) support one gap: there is no representation of query-centered roots. That is elaboration, not a second claim.
- The "Asymmetric selection pressure", "Cold-start selection" and "Combining dimensions" bullets each state one unsettled problem.

The "Validity separate from value" bullet comes closest to the failure mode. The INFO finding records it, and it does not require a split.
