# Premise decomposition: Credit assignment without a chain rule is a debugging problem over an interdependent store

**Register:** claim
**Central commitment (one sentence):** Selection-based learning over a growing, interdependent store has no chain rule. Its credit assignment is therefore a debugging problem, tractable as in large codebases when the store is made debuggable (traces, modularity, links, addressable theory, targeted replay, codification), and this is a plausible path to fully automated credit assignment over hundreds or thousands of artifacts.

## Premises and counterexamples

The commitment has three parts. (a) Gradient-free credit assignment is less scalable than backpropagation, but it is not blocked. (b) The software-debugging analogy carries over, with three named weaknesses. (c) A specific debuggability toolkit restores tractability. Part (a) consumes the chain-rule contrast and the coding-agent precedent. Part (b) consumes the claim that the three properties are the relevant differences and that they weaken debugging tools without breaking them. Part (c) is a pipeline: detect a failure, narrow the suspects (traces, links, near-decomposability, addressable theory), verify causation (replay with an entry withheld), and exit to code (codification). Each stage is a premise, and so is the unstated precondition that failures are detected at all.

The pipeline's single causal-verification step, single-entry replay, is the weakest node. Overdetermination and joint causes are standard defeaters of counterfactual tests, and the note's own phrase 'combination of edits' invites them. The narrowing premises are each dented by a case the note itself names elsewhere. Implicit coupling defeats links as an upper bound on interaction. 'Shared hypothesis justifies several rules' defeats traces as complete. Diffuse degradation defeats the implicit assumption that failures announce themselves. These are internal tensions, not outside attacks, so repairs that leave each part covered by another tool already in the note should be cheap.

No failure is routed GLOBAL. The note hedges its conclusion as 'a plausible path', and its description already states that the store is 'harder where prose instructions weaken traces and reproduction'. Every defeater found has a repair that keeps debugging as the mechanism: group ablation, an evaluation layer, model-version bisection, or qualifiers on traces and links. The commitment would face a GLOBAL threat only if diffuse, overdetermined failures turned out to be the ordinary case. Debugging would then reduce to large-scale statistical ablation, the approach the note itself calls prohibitively expensive. No evidence here shows that they are ordinary, so this stays a watch item.

## For the human

Look first at 'Replaying the failing case with a shortlisted entry withheld or modified verifies causal responsibility directly'. It is the only causal-verification step, and overdetermined or joint causes, a common case, defeat it. Replace it with group or delta-style ablation using repeated samples.
