### New patterns

- **inline-identification:** Identify a named person by role or field at first use and attach the specific work supporting the attributed concept.
- **operationalize-agent-state:** Replace possession, understanding, or other mental-state requirements with observable context, execution, accessibility, and validation conditions.
- **source-named-analogy:** Preserve an external analogy while naming its exact mechanism, linking a direct source, and limiting what the analogy is claimed to establish.
- **qualitative-cost-framing:** Replace equation-like prose whose terms lack units or a horizon with a directional comparison, unless a genuine expected-cost model is supplied.
- **define-derived-subset:** When a mixed artifact contains derived and native material, define the derived layer as a subset and state where the native material participates.
- **recomputation-timescale:** Distinguish avoiding repeated derivation during ordinary use from mandatory re-derivation after the generator changes.
- **unpack-enumeration:** Move a multi-item technical loan out of one overloaded sentence into a short framing sentence and parallel definitions.
- **explicit-antecedent:** Replace an ambiguous locative or pronominal reference with the intended conceptual level or object.
- **delete-empty-restatement:** Remove an elevation sentence that labels a consequence already stated concretely immediately before it.
- **bullet-capitalization:** Normalize prose link-label bullets to begin with a capitalized word.
