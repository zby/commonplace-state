## Governing formulation

Retain a bounded reason for revision order: when an unresolved choice can invalidate the usefulness of particular prose improvements, resolving that choice before substantial dependent polish can avoid that exposure to discarded work. This supplies a reason to address the choice, not a proof that immediate commitment minimizes total cost.

Treat this as a reasoned revision heuristic, not a prevalence claim. The mechanism has explicit conditions; the recommendation remains subject to evidence needs, authority, and competing costs. No statistical tendency, measured benefit, or universal best schedule will be asserted. The note's contribution is the dependency explanation, not a procedure or a summary of Purdue.

## Commitment dispositions

### D1. Dependence creates exposure to discarded polish — retain centrally

- **Basis:** Reconstruction §3's commissioned inference: prose improvements can depend on a claim, audience, scope, or organization surviving; changing that choice can remove or replace the text or its useful relationships.
- **Scope and qualifiers:** Only improvements affected by the contemplated choice are exposed. Resolving the choice first avoids this particular exposure when the polish would otherwise be discarded. Text that survives, or improvements that transfer, are not lost by this mechanism.
- **Dependencies:** An identifiable open choice and a credible explanation of how its resolution could undo the usefulness of the work. Resolution means an authorized selection, not permanent immunity to revision.
- **Unsupported boundary:** No assertion that all polish is lost, that governing choices are always stable once selected, or that this ordering yields net savings.
- **Destination:** Opening claim and causal explanation; the survival/transfer boundary stays adjacent to the mechanism rather than being buried in Scope.

### D2. Early attention is not forced early commitment — retain as a governing distinction

- **Basis:** Reconstruction §§2–4, especially the separation of timing and evidence in `fix-what-the-executor-cant-determine-not-what-it-will.md`.
- **Scope and qualifiers:** Identify and check consequential open choices early. Their resolution may properly wait for evidence that revision itself produces.
- **Dependencies:** A distinction between recognizing a dependency and selecting an answer. Waiting is justified by a possible decision-changing result, not by the passage of time alone.
- **Unsupported boundary:** No requirement to settle every governing choice before any editing; no instruction to manufacture certainty.
- **Destination:** Immediately after the mechanism, shaping the recommendation before examples or comparison.

### D3. Bounded local work may produce decision evidence — retain

- **Basis:** Reconstruction §2's account of `productive-deferral-requires-option-evidence-and-convergence.md`; its revision applications are commissioned inference.
- **Scope and qualifiers:** A trial example, paragraph, or reader interpretation may precede commitment when some possible result could change selection, timing, modification, or abandonment. Editing a sentence is not by itself evidence production.
- **Dependencies:** A named question and a possible result that changes the decision. The same visible activity may be a probe or dependent polish, depending on this role.
- **Unsupported boundary:** No claim that diagnostic intent makes an activity useful, that probes are free, or that each trial produces evidence.
- **Destination:** Explanation of the diagnostic-work exception, with the trial-transition illustration in D10.

### D4. Deferral needs a live option and a return rule — retain in compressed supporting form

- **Basis:** Reconstruction §2's internal deferral account: an exercisable option, possible decision-changing evidence, and a rule connecting a return condition to a response are necessary for controlled postponement.
- **Scope and qualifiers:** These conditions make postponement controlled; they do not show that it is worth its costs.
- **Dependencies:** D2–D3; link the internal note for the fuller account rather than importing its entire argument.
- **Unsupported boundary:** No numerical threshold or guarantee of convergence or benefit merely from satisfying the conditions.
- **Destination:** The discussion of evidence-producing delay, as a brief guard against indefinite postponement, not a separate procedural checklist.

### D5. Independent repairs need not wait — retain

- **Basis:** Reconstruction §§3–4's consequence of the dependence mechanism.
- **Scope and qualifiers:** Independence is relative to actual contemplated changes, not the repair's size or apparent locality.
- **Dependencies:** D1: an unaffected repair lacks the exposure that motivates delay.
- **Unsupported boundary:** No blanket claim that links, spelling, or other small repairs are independent.
- **Destination:** Exception beside diagnostic work, optionally illustrated by a broken link that the named claim revision cannot affect. It does not justify a global work freeze.

### D6. Compare rework with delay and other costs — retain explicitly

- **Basis:** Reconstruction §§2, 4–5 and the commissioned acceptance boundary. Waiting, probes, maintained alternatives, and delayed delivery can cost more than the polish avoided.
- **Scope and qualifiers:** Proceeding provisionally or committing on available evidence can be warranted. Avoiding one waste mechanism does not establish lowest total cost.
- **Dependencies:** D1 supplies the rework side; D3–D4 supply evidence and waiting costs.
- **Unsupported boundary:** No portable cost formula, invalidation probability, numerical score, or optimal conflict-resolution rule is available.
- **Destination:** Main recommendation's cost qualification, with missing estimators named once in Scope.

### D7. Option preservation and rework exposure are different ordering problems — retain explicitly

- **Basis:** Reconstruction §2's faithful representation of `solve-low-degree-of-freedom-subproblems-first-to-avoid-blocking.md`.
- **Scope and qualifiers:** Its mechanism concerns an early flexible commitment consuming another choice's few viable alternatives. This note concerns an unresolved choice undoing completed prose investment. Many possible answers can coexist with much dependent prose; one permissible metadata value can coexist with no discarded prose.
- **Dependencies:** Distinguish viable options from dependence of work's usefulness; do not assign option counts from document hierarchy.
- **Unsupported boundary:** Neither mechanism refutes the other. They need not recommend the same timing, and the evidence supplies no universal priority between them and evidence needs.
- **Destination:** A short comparison section with an inline link to the adjacent note. Retain the paired examples only if needed to make the distinction concrete; they are reasoning illustrations, not observations.
